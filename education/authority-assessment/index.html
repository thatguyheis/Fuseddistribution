Self-Assessment Tool

Know Your Authority.
Build Your Influence.

Most people assume they're operating at a higher level than they actually are. The Hughes Authority Self-Assessment gives you a measurable baseline across the five behavioral traits that determine how much influence you carry — in business and in life.

Why This Matters

Self-Awareness Is a
Business Advantage

You can't improve what you haven't measured. Knowing your actual behavioral baseline — not the one you imagine — is the foundation of intentional growth, both personally and professionally.

🎯

The Gap Between Intention and Impact

Most operators and leaders believe they're communicating confidence, inspiring their teams, and handling stress well. The data says otherwise. The gap between how we intend to show up and how we actually do is where businesses stall, relationships erode, and momentum dies.

Self-assessment doesn't just reveal weaknesses — it reveals your real strengths, the ones worth building on. Knowing exactly where you stand makes every coaching conversation, every goal you set, and every habit you try to build dramatically more effective.

📈

Behavioral Traits Compound Like Assets

The five traits in this assessment — Confidence, Discipline, Leadership, Gratitude, and Enjoyment — aren't soft skills. They are the invisible infrastructure of every business relationship, every sale, every hire, every client retained. People buy from and stay loyal to people they trust, enjoy, and are inspired by.

When these traits are operating at the Inspirational or Contagious level, they generate compounding returns: referrals come unsolicited, teams self-organize, clients renew without being asked. That's not luck — it's behavioral leverage.

🔁

A Baseline You Can Return To

Growth without measurement is guesswork. This assessment gives you a snapshot of where you are today across five dimensions that directly influence your output as a business owner, operator, or leader. Retake it in 90 days and you'll have real evidence of progress — or an honest signal that something needs to shift.

Most people never take this step. They operate on assumptions about themselves, then wonder why results stay flat. A 10-minute assessment today can change the direction of the next 90 days.

🌐

Authority Is Contagious — In Both Directions

Chase Hughes built the Authority framework around a core insight: your behavioral traits don't stay with you. They spread. A leader operating at Level 1 in Confidence pulls everyone around them down. A leader at Level 5 lifts the entire room — employees, clients, partners — without trying.

For business owners, this is the highest-leverage thing you can work on. Your team, your brand voice, your client experience — all of it is downstream of how you show up. The assessment tells you exactly where to start.

The Framework

Five Traits. One Profile.

Each trait maps directly to outcomes in your business and personal life. Low scores are starting points, not judgments — the framework is built to show you exactly what to work on next.

💪
Confidence

How you carry yourself in social and professional situations. Not arrogance — the quiet, settled certainty that lets you engage anyone, take action without hesitation, and receive feedback without crumbling.

Business impact: client trust, sales conversations, negotiation, networking
⚙️
Discipline

Your ability to build systems around yourself — habits, routines, financial plans, goals — and execute them consistently regardless of how enjoyable they are in the moment.

Business impact: delivery consistency, financial health, team reliability, execution
🧭
Leadership

How you show up when things go wrong, how you treat the people around you, and whether your behavior inspires others to operate at a higher level — even when you're not in the room.

Business impact: team culture, retention, delegation, client relationships
🌱
Gratitude

Your baseline orientation toward what you have versus what's missing. Genuine gratitude isn't just a mindset — it's a social signal that generates loyalty, resilience, and long-term thinking.

Business impact: customer retention, team morale, resilience in downturns, reputation
⚡
Enjoyment

Whether you experience life as something that happens to you or something you bring energy to. High-enjoyment operators are magnetic — their momentum is visible, and it draws people in.

Business impact: personal brand, creative output, sustainability, referral generation
The Scale

From Burden to Contagious

Each trait is measured on a five-point scale. The goal isn't to be at Level 5 everywhere — it's to know where you are and move deliberately.

1
Burden

These behaviors are creating drag — for you and the people around you. The first and hardest step is honest recognition.

2
Developing

You're aware of the gap and beginning to shift, but old patterns still dominate. Progress here is visible and fast when targeted.

3
Positive

You're operating at a healthy, constructive level. Others respond well to you. Intentional deepening is what moves the needle now.

4
Inspirational

You actively lift others. Your behavior sets a standard people around you begin emulating. Consistency across all conditions is the frontier.

5
Contagious

This quality spreads automatically. People become better just by being around you. Rare, and the most powerful position to operate from.

The Assessment

25 Questions. Honest Answers.

Answer based on how you actually behave — not how you'd like to. The results are only useful if the input is honest. Takes about 8–10 minutes.

💪 Confidence

1. When you're in a social situation with people you don't know...

2. When you receive criticism from others...

3. Where does your sense of self-worth primarily come from?

4. When you disagree with someone...

5. How would you describe your social presence and body language day-to-day?

⚙️ Discipline

6. How consistent is your daily routine?

7. How do you approach goals?

8. When a task isn't fun or enjoyable...

9. How are your financial habits?

10. When it comes to forming new habits...

🧭 Leadership

11. When something goes wrong in your work or life...

12. How do you treat the people around you (team, family, friends)?

13. Under pressure or stress, you...

14. How honest and direct are you in communication?

15. Over time, how do others respond to your example?

🌱 Gratitude

16. How would you describe your general daily outlook?

17. How often do you express appreciation to others?

18. When setbacks or hardships occur...

19. How do you relate to what you have in life (shelter, health, relationships)?

20. When others are succeeding or happy...

⚡ Enjoyment

21. What most determines whether you have a good day?

22. How do you handle difficult or unpleasant situations?

23. What motivates your goals?

24. What kind of impact do you have on the people around you?

25. How do you approach situations or tasks you don't enjoy?

Your Authority Profile

What To Do With Your Results

A Score Is a Starting Point,
Not a Verdict

Your profile is most useful when you act on it. Here's how to translate your results into real change.

01

Target Your Lowest Trait

Don't try to improve everything at once. Find your lowest-scoring trait and treat it as your primary focus for the next 30 days. Small, consistent actions in one area compound faster than scattered effort across five.

02

Identify One Specific Behavior

Look at the descriptions for your current level and the one above it. Pick one concrete behavior that marks the difference — one thing you could actually do differently this week. Broad resolutions don't move the needle; specific behavioral targets do.

03

Retake in 90 Days

Bookmark this page and set a reminder to retake the assessment in 90 days. Growth without measurement is just hoping. Comparing your scores across time gives you real evidence — and real motivation to keep going.
