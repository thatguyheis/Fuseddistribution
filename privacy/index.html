Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fused Direct Distribution ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use our website at fuseddistribution.com, including all sub-pages, contact forms, and email newsletter subscriptions.

By using this site or submitting any form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use this site or submit your information.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message or inquiry content

Information collected when you subscribe to a mailing list:

  • Email address
  • First name (where requested)
  • Subscription source (which form or page you used)
  • Date and time of subscription
  • IP address at time of subscription (collected by MailerLite for fraud and compliance purposes)

Information collected automatically:

We do not use cookies, third-party analytics scripts, or tracking pixels on this website. We do not build behavioral profiles of site visitors. Email newsletters sent through MailerLite may include standard email tracking such as open-rate pixels and link-click tracking. You can disable image loading in your email client to prevent open tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to contact form submissions and business inquiries
  • To deliver newsletters and email content you opted into
  • To send the signup offer or lead magnet associated with your subscription
  • To send occasional related updates from the brand you subscribed to
  • To maintain subscriber records and process unsubscribe requests
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations (including CAN-SPAM and GDPR)
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized use

We do not use contact form data to add you to any mailing list. Newsletter subscribers are only added through explicit opt-in forms.

3. Our Mailing Lists

We operate two email newsletters:

  • The Silver Report — published under Fused Reserve. Covers silver investing, market updates, and buying guides.
  • The Tech Brief — published under Fused Technology Solutions. Covers website performance, local SEO, and digital marketing for local businesses.

Subscription to either list is entirely optional. You will only be added if you complete a signup form and confirm your intent. We will never add you from a contact form submission, purchase, or any other source without a separate, explicit opt-in.

Unsubscribing: Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You may also contact us directly to request removal. We will process all unsubscribe requests within 10 business days. Once removed, your email address is not retained for marketing purposes.

CAN-SPAM compliance: All commercial emails include our physical mailing address, a clear identification as commercial communication, and a functional unsubscribe mechanism, as required by the US CAN-SPAM Act.

GDPR compliance (EU/EEA subscribers): If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, we process your email subscription data under the lawful basis of consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing. See Section 9 for your full rights.

5. Data Transmission and Security

All data submitted through this site is transmitted over HTTPS using TLS encryption. We implement standard HTTP security headers including Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options, and Strict-Transport-Security to protect against common web vulnerabilities.

Access to subscriber data is limited to authorized personnel with a business need. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security against all possible threats.

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

  • Contact form submissions: Retained only as long as necessary to respond to and close out your inquiry. Typically no longer than 12 months from last contact.
  • Newsletter subscribers: Retained in MailerLite until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Suppression records (to prevent re-adding unsubscribed addresses) may be retained indefinitely as required by anti-spam regulations.
  • Transactional email logs: Resend retains delivery logs per their own retention policy. We do not separately archive transactional email content.

You may request deletion of your data at any time. See Section 9 for how to submit a request.

7. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, localStorage tracking, or third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar). No tracking data is collected as you browse the site.

Email newsletters sent via MailerLite may include a tracking pixel (a small transparent image) that records whether the email was opened, and may track clicks on links within the email. This is standard practice for email marketing and helps us understand engagement with our content. If you do not want this tracked, you can disable automatic image loading in your email client, or unsubscribe from the newsletter.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately using the contact form at fuseddistribution.com/#contact and we will delete it promptly.

All users:

  • Right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to request deletion of your personal information

EU/EEA residents (GDPR):

  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the form at fuseddistribution.com/#contact. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
