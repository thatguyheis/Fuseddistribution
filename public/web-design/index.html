Questions

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own your website. If you ever cancel, you keep the raw files and can take them to any other host or provider. Your domain and content are always yours too.

How long does it take?

A first draft is usually ready within 24 to 48 hours of our kickoff call. The biggest factor is how fast I get your logo, photos, hours and services.

Do I pay before I see the site?

No. You see the whole site built and working first. You only pick a plan once you like it.

Do you work outside Vancouver and Portland?

Yes. Most clients are in Vancouver, WA, Portland, OR and the towns around them, but remote work is always available.

What if I want to cancel?

Monthly plans are month to month. Cancel any time with no penalty, and keep the raw files of your site.

Do you set up Google Business Profile?

Yes, on the Expansion plan. Standard includes local SEO for your website.
