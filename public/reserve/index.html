How It Works

A controlled way to build a physical silver position

The model combines subscription convenience with local dealer sourcing, so early members can accumulate silver over time without locking themselves into one rigid product.

1. Subscribe

Choose a monthly silver budget. The early rollout starts with $100, $250, and $500 memberships.

2. Source

Your budget is pooled into monthly buying power, then routed through local dealer relationships, coin shows, and secondary market opportunities.

3. Select

Set your silver preferences: junk silver, bullion, silver dollars, numismatic, world coins, or best price. Your tier controls how many slots you can use.

4. Ship Or Hold

Your silver is allocated after sourcing. Ship when ready, or hold until the package size makes better economic sense.

Preference Options

Choose what silver you receive each month

When you subscribe, you set your preferences. We source to match them. Your plan determines how many preferences you hold. Collector Access members can weight their own split.

Junk Silver

Pre-1965 US 90% silver coins

Dimes, quarters, and half-dollars. The most liquid and recognizable form of constitutional silver. A stacking staple.

Bullion

Modern .999 silver rounds, bars, and ingots

Pure silver by weight. Easy to price, easy to trade, and ideal for buyers focused on accumulating raw silver content.

Silver Dollars

Morgan, Peace, and large-format US silver dollars

Sourced as cull, junk, or found inventory. The value is in the silver content (~0.77 oz per coin), not the grade. Iconic pieces at honest prices.

Numismatic

Collectible US coins with value above melt

Key dates, proof sets, and graded coins where condition and rarity add a premium. Best for collectors building beyond a pure silver stack.

World Coins

Foreign silver coins from global mints

Often undervalued relative to silver content. A variety-focused preference that adds history and geographic diversity to your reserve.

Best Price

We source whatever is closest to spot that month

No category restriction. We use this slot to find the best available deal from our sourcing network and pass the value directly to you.
