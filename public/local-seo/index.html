Local SEO in Vancouver and Portland

Local SEO gets your business found when people nearby search for what you do, on Google and in AI answers. It is part of the Standard plan at $499 a month, with Google Business Profile management on Expansion.

What local SEO means

Local SEO means showing up when someone near you searches for what you sell. It comes down to three things: a site Google can read, a Google Business Profile that matches it, and a steady stream of useful pages and reviews.

Local SEO is part of the Standard plan at $499 a month. Google Business Profile setup and management is on Expansion, from $999 a month.

What I do

  • Page titles and descriptions written for local searches
  • Local business schema so Google knows who and where you are
  • The same name, address and phone everywhere
  • Sitemap and Google Search Console setup
  • 8 fact checked blog posts a month on Standard
  • Review requests to your happy customers

What I will not do is promise a #1 ranking. Nobody can honestly promise that, and Google says to be wary of anyone who does.

Showing up in AI answers too

More people now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI for a recommendation. The same work helps there: clear pages that answer real questions, consistent business details, and reviews. Every site I build lets the AI search crawlers read it.

What Clients Say

Questions

How long until I see results?

Local SEO builds over months, not days. Most businesses see steady gains over the first few months as pages, reviews and listings add up. Anyone who promises a ranking by a date is guessing.

Do I need a blog?

It helps. Each fact checked post answers a real question your customers search for, and gives Google and AI tools something specific to show. The Standard plan writes 8 a month for you.

What is NAP?

Name, address and phone number. They need to match everywhere your business is listed. Mismatches confuse Google and cost you rankings.

Is Google Business Profile included?

Google Business Profile setup and management is on the Expansion plan. Standard covers local SEO on your website.

Yes. Remote work is always available.
