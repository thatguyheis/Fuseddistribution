One post in. A blog, a reel, and social posts out.

Standard runs on the same system I built for this site. It plans the topic, writes the post, checks every number, makes the images, narrates a video in your voice, and posts it. You stay in control with an approval step before anything goes out.

9Steps running daily
  1. 01 Topic

    Plan the topic

    Topics are picked from what your customers actually search for.

  2. 02 Draft

    Write the post

    AI drafts the article in your voice, with your services and location.

  3. 03 Fact check

    Check every number

    Any stat without a real source gets blocked. Nothing made up goes live.

  4. 04 Approve

    You approve

    You get a preview. Reply to approve, or it goes out after 48 hours.

  5. 05 Publish

    Publish and verify

    The post goes live and the system confirms the page actually loads.

  6. 06 Visuals

    Images and charts

    A hero image, brand graphics, and data charts are made for each post.

  7. 07 Voice

    Narrate in your voice

    The post becomes a short script, read by a clone of your own voice.

  8. 08 Video

    Render the reel

    A vertical video with captions, timed to the narration, checked before release.

  9. 09 Post

    Post and report

    Shared to YouTube, Instagram, and X, then confirmed and tracked.
