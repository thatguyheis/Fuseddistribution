## OVERLAY: TELL PEOPLE EXACTLY WHAT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Tell people exactly what they get and how to stop getting texts. A clear, concise welcome message is key. For example, “Hi {Name}, thanks for joining the {Business Name} text list! You’ll receive exclusive offers and appointment reminders.” Don’t be vague. Email is great for sending out long guides.

## STAT: 69% CONSUMERS PREFER RECEIVING PROMOTIONAL

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Consider this: a recent survey showed that 69 percent of consumers prefer receiving promotional messages via text compared to email. Email marketing is fantastic for building a long-term relationship with your customers. You can use it to send out service guides, showcase your portfolio, or share customer testimonials.

## OVERLAY: TEXTING WORKS ANSWER FAST

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: Texting works when you need an answer fast. If someone calls you and needs immediate help, SMS gets the message through right away. Make sure you follow the rules for texting. You need people to give you permission to text them, and they need an easy way to stop getting texts.

## OVERLAY: HYBRID APPROACH WORKS BEST

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: That hybrid approach works best for local service. SMS marketing gives you a direct line to your customer. It’s like having a personal assistant who can instantly notify you of a potential booking. If someone calls you right now, that’s a huge win for your booking rate.

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

## STAT: 24 HOURS BUSINESS BEFORE SCHEDULED MOWING

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 24s minimum

Narration: You need to decide what channel works best for your specific business. If you need a customer to call you right away, text messaging is your best bet. Use it for appointment reminders or urgent service updates. For instance, a landscaping company could send a text reminder 24 hours before a scheduled mowing service.
