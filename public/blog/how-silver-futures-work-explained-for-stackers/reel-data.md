## segments
- type: overlay
 text: CONTRACT SIZE MULTIPLIES EXPOSURE
 narration: CME’s standard Silver futures contract represents 5,000 troy ounces and is quoted per troy ounce. A hypothetical 30 dollar quote implies 150,000 dollars of notional exposure. That is contract arithmetic, not a live quote or trading recommendation.
- type: overlay
 text: SMALL MOVES CHANGE DOLLARS
 narration: For 5,000 ounces, a hypothetical ten cent per ounce move changes notional exposure by 500 dollars. A twenty five cent move changes it by 1,250 dollars. The calculation shows sensitivity, not a prediction of direction or outcome.
- type: overlay
 text: MARGIN IS PERFORMANCE SECURITY
 narration: Margin is performance security, not a down payment that buys silver. Initial and maintenance requirements can change, and a broker can require more than an exchange minimum. Never treat a current margin figure as permanent.
- type: overlay
 text: DAILY SETTLEMENT MOVES CASH
 narration: Futures are marked to a daily settlement price. Variation gains and losses move through the clearing system before expiry. A final price chart does not show the path or the cash needed during that path.
- type: overlay
 text: EXPIRY HAS SPECIFIC RULES
 narration: Each contract month has a last trading date and procedures for notices, settlement, or delivery. CME’s standard contract is physically settled with exchange specifications for weight, fineness, and approved delivery. Read the current product rulebook.
- type: overlay
 text: DELIVERY IS NOT RETAIL OWNERSHIP
 narration: Exchange delivery can involve approved forms, warehouses, warrants, financing, insurance, and transport. A standard contract is not automatically a parcel of retail coins. Identify the product code and settlement type before interpreting a headline.
