## Variant A

**Hook:** Bars beat coins on price. Here is the math.

I bought Silver Eagles for about a year before I looked at the numbers. Eagles run 15 to 20 percent over spot. A 10 oz bar runs 3 to 5 percent. On 100 ounces, that gap is 480 dollars. Not a small number. Now I buy bars for most of my position and keep a few coins for small-lot flexibility. The breakdown is on the blog.

## Variant B

**Hook:** I switched from coins to bars. Here is why.

Both hold the same silver. But a 10 oz bar costs about 4 percent over spot. An Eagle costs 15 to 22 percent over spot. At 30 dollars an ounce, that 16-point difference is 48 dollars per 10 oz you are not getting in metal. I was not against coins. I just did not know the premium gap was that wide until I ran the numbers myself.
