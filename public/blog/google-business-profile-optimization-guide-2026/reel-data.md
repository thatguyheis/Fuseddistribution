- text: POST AT LEAST ONCE EVERY WEEK
  explanation: GBP posts expire after 7 days. Weekly posting maintains a current post and signals active profile management to Google
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Google Business Profile posts expire after 7 days. If you post once a month, your profile sits dark for 3 weeks out of 4. Post once a week to keep a current update visible. Offers, what's-new updates, and product posts all count. Batch them monthly and schedule them forward.

- text: SEED THE Q&A SECTION YOURSELF
  explanation: Any Google user can answer Q&A questions. Seeding your own Q&A with accurate answers prevents competitors or incorrect information from appearing
  graphic_type: none
  narration: The Q&A section on your Google Business Profile is publicly editable. Any Google user can answer a question, including incorrectly. Seed the section yourself by asking and answering the 5 questions your customers ask most often. Complete sentences. Include your service keyword and city once each naturally.

- text: GEO-TAG PHOTOS BEFORE UPLOAD
  explanation: Photos taken at your business location contain GPS coordinates in EXIF data that reinforce geographic relevance signals
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Photos taken at your physical business location contain GPS coordinates embedded in their file data. When you upload those photos to Google Business Profile, those coordinates reinforce the geographic relevance signal. Shoot photos at your location, not at home. If you edit before uploading, use software that preserves location metadata.

- text: ADD VIDEO TO YOUR GBP MEDIA TAB
  explanation: GBP supports video uploads up to 75 MB and 30 seconds. Videos appear in local search results and few local competitors have added them
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Google Business Profile supports video uploads directly in the Media tab. Most local competitors haven't added any. A 30-second video of your storefront or a service being performed adds a visual layer most Map Pack results don't have. Maximum 75 megabytes, 720p minimum, no overlaid text or logos.

  narration: 64 percent of consumers use Google to find local business information, according to BrightLocal's 2024 Local Consumer Review Survey. - text: RESPOND TO NEGATIVE REVIEWS IN 4 HOURS
  explanation: Fast professional responses to negative reviews reduce reputational damage and signal active management to Google
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Negative reviews sit in plain sight for every future customer who searches your business. Respond within 4 hours. Acknowledge the problem, apologize without excuses, and offer a direct way to resolve it. Never argue publicly. A professional response to a 1-star review often converts more new customers than the review loses.
