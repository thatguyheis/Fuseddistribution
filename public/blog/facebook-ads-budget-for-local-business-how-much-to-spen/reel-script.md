## STAT: 5 MILE RADIUS BUSINESS RUN

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Pick one specific local zone - say, a 5-mile radius around your business - and run simple ads there. This lets you learn without risking a significant loss. ## STAT: $10 BUDGET BUSINESS COST LESS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: If you’re spending money and not getting leads, you’re wasting your budget. Ignore how many people saw your ad. Focus on the action - the actual business you’re generating.
