## OVERLAY: Start with business email
**Visual:** Original conceptual business workflow diagram. No product interface or customer records are shown.
**Duration:** 27s
Narration: A customer inquiry can become a connected workflow. Use Gmail for a business email address at your own domain. Before moving existing mail, confirm domain ownership and follow Google's setup instructions. Test sending and receiving with an outside account. Then map one real handoff: who receives the inquiry, where the working file lives, and who is responsible for the next step.

---
## OVERLAY: One file, clear permissions
**Visual:** Original conceptual business workflow diagram. No product interface or customer records are shown.
**Duration:** 27s
Narration: Keep the estimate in Drive and share its link instead of sending another attachment. Use Docs comments to collect questions on the working document. Choose access deliberately: a viewer can read, a commenter can comment, and an editor can change the file. Check the recipient address and test that exact link from another account. One shared link still needs careful permissions and a clear owner.

---
## OVERLAY: Agree on the next action
**Visual:** Original conceptual business workflow diagram. No product interface or customer records are shown.
**Duration:** 27s
Narration: When a conversation is useful, create a Calendar event and include the right document link. Add a Meet conversation where it fits the task. Check the invitees, time zone and file access before sending. After the call, record the decision and assign the next action. This is an illustrative workflow, not a claim about measured time savings. Start with one process before moving the whole business.

---
## OVERLAY: Compare the plans
**Visual:** Original conceptual business workflow diagram. No product interface or customer records are shown.
**Duration:** 27s
Narration: Compare storage and features before selecting a plan. Google lists thirty gigabytes for Starter, two terabytes for Standard, and five terabytes for Plus: pooled storage per user. Review current pricing and trial terms rather than assuming every offer applies. This is my referral link. I may earn a reward. Choose the plan for your team's actual needs and test the workflow before committing.
