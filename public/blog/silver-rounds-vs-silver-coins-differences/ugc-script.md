## Variant A

Hook: Rounds gave me more silver for my money.

I started buying Silver Eagles because they looked official. After six months I learned generic rounds cost 3 to 8 percent over spot while Eagles run 15 to 25 percent. The silver inside is identical. Both are .999 fine. I was paying 15 percent extra for a coin design and a government guarantee I never needed. Now I hold mostly rounds for my reserve and a few Eagles for resale. Know your premium before you pick a format. The silver content is the same either way.

## Variant B

Hook: Nobody told me about the premium difference.

I compared Silver Eagles and generic rounds side by side for the first time last year. Same weight. Same .999 fine silver. The Eagle cost 6 dollars more per ounce. That is about 20 percent over spot. The round was 5 percent over spot. Over 50 ounces that gap was 150 dollars in extra premiums for the exact same silver. I still buy a few Eagles for gifting. Rounds handle the bulk of my reserve now. Same metal. Different wrapper cost. The math is simple once you see it.
