## OVERLAY: READ FINENESS AS A PROPORTION

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 24s minimum

Narration: The notation point nine nine nine means 99.9 percent silver, while point nine nine nine nine means 99.99 percent silver. These figures describe fineness, or the proportion of silver in the material. They do not tell you the total weight of an item, prove that a particular object is genuine, or establish that one purchase will produce a better return. Read the purity figure alongside the exact product specification and the evidence supporting the seller's description.

## OVERLAY: COMPARE EQUAL GROSS WEIGHT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 25s minimum

Narration: Gross weight is the mass of the material as a whole. Fine silver weight is the mass attributable to silver. For a clearly labeled mathematical example, imagine two objects that each have a gross mass of 1,000 grams. At a fineness of point nine nine nine, the calculated silver mass is 999 grams. At point nine nine nine nine, it is 999.9 grams. The difference is 0.9 gram for that equal gross mass. These are calculations, not tested products.

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: The number stamped on an object is also a claim that needs context. Check the exact product, seller and available authentication evidence. A result is useful when its meaning is clear, rather than when it merely sounds reassuring.
