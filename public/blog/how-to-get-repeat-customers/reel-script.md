# Reel Script: how-to-get-repeat-customers
format: long-form
segments: 8
target-duration: 111s

---
## HOOK
**Visual:** Topic intro shot
**Duration:** 6s minimum
Narration: A practical way to earn repeat customers is to make the next useful step easy.

---
## OVERLAY: DEFINE THE SECOND ACTION
**Visual:** Customer journey card
**Duration:** 15s minimum
Narration: Choose the behavior you want to improve before choosing a tool. It could be a second appointment, repeat purchase, maintenance visit, or referral. Define the period and compare customers by month so the measure has a clear meaning.

---
## OVERLAY: MAKE THE FIRST VISIT CLEAR
**Visual:** Handoff checklist
**Duration:** 15s minimum
Narration: Retention starts with the first experience. Give the customer a clear scope, price or estimate, timing, and way to ask questions. At handoff, explain what happens next. A useful answer today creates a natural reason to help again later.

---
## OVERLAY: SEND THREE USEFUL FOLLOW UPS
**Visual:** Three message cards
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Build a simple sequence. Confirm the work and provide help, check for problems after a sensible interval, then offer the next relevant action. Give each message one purpose, one call to action, and a human reply path.

---
## OVERLAY: ASK FOR AUTHENTIC FEEDBACK
**Visual:** Feedback loop
**Duration:** 15s minimum
Narration: Ask for feedback after a completed interaction. Never buy praise, write a review for a customer, or pressure people to choose only positive language. Route problems to a person, resolve them, and use repeated themes to improve the process.

---
## OVERLAY: KEEP THE RETURN PATH READY
**Visual:** Profile and booking path
**Duration:** 15s minimum
Narration: Keep your Google Business Profile hours, services, phone number, and booking link accurate. Make calling, booking, and directions easy on a phone. Test forms and confirmations after changes so customers can return without searching.

---
## OVERLAY: REVIEW ONE CHANGE MONTHLY
**Visual:** Cohort metrics
**Duration:** 25s minimum
Narration: Each month, review second actions, time to return, unsubscribes, unresolved complaints, and referral sources. Write down one change to test. Keep a dated log and treat one result as evidence for your next test, not a universal promise. A result is only useful when you know what changed and which customers were exposed to it. If a change improves repeat behavior, keep it and retest periodically. If it does not, remove it and learn from the reason.
