## OVERLAY: Early payoff
**Duration:** 20s
Text: Give AI four useful inputs
Narration: Give it a brief before asking for a script. Define one audience, one problem, one checkable result, and the evidence the viewer should see. Those four inputs give the draft a direction. You are not promising business results. You are deciding what this one video will teach and how someone could inspect that lesson.

---
## OVERLAY: Make the problem visible
**Duration:** 22s
Text: Make the request concrete
Narration: Here is a fictional example for a small repair shop owner. The problem is an estimate request that has no measurements or requested date. The result is a checklist for a reviewable request. The evidence is a mock request with the missing fields clearly marked, followed by a corrected example. Use invented names and keep private customer details out.

---
## OVERLAY: Review the middle
**Duration:** 21s
Text: Compare the information supplied
Narration: Compare two requests. Make a video about AI names only a topic. The example brief names an audience, problem, result, and evidence. The bars count one supplied field versus four. They do not predict video quality, views, or leads. The useful improvement is that a reviewer can now check whether the script follows each instruction.

---
## OVERLAY: Keep the ending specific
**Duration:** 21s
Text: Map words to visible evidence
Narration: Ask AI to return a scene plan beside the narration. For each scene, require the spoken point, the visible evidence, and the question it answers. If the voice says confirm the date, show a labeled date field. A generic office photograph may provide context, but it cannot demonstrate which information the viewer needs to collect.

---
## OVERLAY: Review the export
**Duration:** 22s
Text: Review before you render
Narration: Before rendering, read the hook and the closing question together. Check that the middle actually delivers the promised method. Verify any factual claims against the sources, keep illustrative examples labeled, and inspect the captions after speech generation. Save the brief with your source of truth so the next AI can continue from the same instructions instead of starting over.
