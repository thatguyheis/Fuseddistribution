## OVERLAY: DEFINE THE COMPLETED JOB

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Choose one restaurant task before choosing an AI tool. Rewriting approved menu descriptions is different from taking an order and delivering it to the kitchen. Define the starting request and the required outcome. A fluent response cannot prove that the selected item, price, quantity or modifier is correct in the order record.

---

## OVERLAY: REVIEW GENERATED DESCRIPTIONS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Square documents an AI description generator for items, with a review step before insertion and saving. Supply approved facts and compare the proposed wording against them. Reject invented ingredients or preparation claims. Generated prose does not verify a recipe, and it should not decide dietary or allergen suitability for a guest.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK CHANNELS AND MODIFIERS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Keep a clear source of truth for prices, options and availability. Check each affected sales channel after a menu change. Seeing an item online does not prove its required modifiers are available there. Record what the customer sees and compare it with the accepted order, instead of assuming every setting synchronized correctly.

---

## OVERLAY: VERIFY THE VOICE INTEGRATION

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: For voice ordering, request a demonstration of the actual connection to your point of sale. Include a modifier, a quantity change and a request for staff. Identify who owns payment, acceptance and kitchen delivery. A demonstration on a different channel does not validate your own installation or establish its accuracy during service.

---

## OVERLAY: TEST THE ENTIRE ORDER PATH

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: Write expected outcomes before testing. Check a basic order, an unavailable item, an order outside operating hours and a supported interruption test. Confirm the kitchen destination and look for duplicates. Use the provider approved testing procedure, then preserve failed cases so repairs can be checked against the same inputs afterward.

---

## OVERLAY: MEASURE AND KEEP A FALLBACK

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Measure complete orders, corrections, duplicates and staff handoffs using a written definition of success. Keep the sample size beside any percentage. Save the previous configuration and decide how staff can pause the new automation. When a failure is repaired, record its cause and verification so the next shift has a repeatable procedure.
