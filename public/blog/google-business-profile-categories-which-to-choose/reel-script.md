# Reel Script: google-business-profile-categories-which-to-choose
format: long-form
segments: 8
target-duration: 120s

---
## HOOK
**Visual:** Topic intro shot
**Duration:** 10s
Narration: Choosing a Google Business Profile category is a classification task, not a keyword contest. Start with what the business is, then separate its products, services, and amenities.

---
## OVERLAY: WRITE THE REAL BUSINESS
**Visual:** Business identity card
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Describe the business using its real-world identity, main activity, customers, and location model. Use the name customers see on signage and the website. Keep the core business separate from a product it sells or another business operating inside the same location.

---
## OVERLAY: CHOOSE THE PRIMARY CATEGORY
**Visual:** Category selector
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Google says the primary category should best match what the business does. Use the supported category list and choose the most specific truthful option for the whole business. If the exact choice is unavailable, use a more general supported category. Never invent one.

---
## OVERLAY: APPLY THE IS TEST
**Visual:** Is versus has card
**Duration:** 14s
Narration: Ask whether the category completes “This business IS a.” A dentist describes an identity. Evening appointments are an attribute, and a parking lot is an amenity. Put those details in their proper fields instead of turning every feature into a category.

---
## OVERLAY: ADD ONLY REAL SECONDARY CATEGORIES
**Visual:** Secondary category filter
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Additional categories are optional. Add one only when it represents a genuine customer-facing activity that the business operates and that is not redundant. Do not use categories solely as keywords or to describe every product, service, or amenity.

---
## OVERLAY: CHECK DEPARTMENTS AND LOCATIONS
**Visual:** Co-located businesses and branch cards
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: A business inside another business is not automatically a category of the host. Check ownership, staffing, customer access, and public identity. For multiple locations, keep the best category consistent within comparable operations and document any real exception.

---
## OVERLAY: RECORD THE CHANGE
**Visual:** Decision worksheet
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Save the current categories, write the reason for the change, and use one accountable owner. Record the old value, new value, evidence, and date. Google notes that editing a category may trigger another verification request. A category is an identity decision, not a ranking promise.
