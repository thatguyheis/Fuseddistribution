## reel-1
angle: lead_stat
hook: Your homeowner's insurance probably won't cover your full silver stack after a theft. Here's the number that explains why.
hook_type: pain_point

### stats
- text: $1,500 THEFT SUBLIMIT
  narration: Standard HO-3 homeowner's policies cap theft coverage for precious metals, jewelry, and silverware at $1,500, according to the Insurance Information Institute. A 100-ounce silver stack at current spot prices is worth around $3,200. If it's stolen, you'd recover less than half.
- text: 47% RECOVERED BASIC HO-3
  narration: That $1,500 sublimit works out to 47 percent of a $3,200 stack. Not 47 percent off. 47 percent total. The other 53 percent is just gone. This isn't a rare edge case. It's the default for most homeowner's policies written in the USA.

### chart
title: Recovery After Theft: $3,200 Stack by Policy Type
bars:
  - Basic HO-3 only: 47%
  - HO-3 + blanket endorsement: 78%

narration: A basic policy gets you 47 percent back. A blanket endorsement gets you to 78 percent. A scheduled personal property floater gets you to 94 percent after a standard deductible. The floater costs $1 to $2 per $100 of coverage annually. On a $3,200 stack that's $32 to $64 per year.

## reel-2
angle: concept
hook: Fire damage to your silver stack is usually covered in full. Theft is capped. Mysterious disappearance pays nothing. Here's what the difference means.
hook_type: immediate_value

### stats
- text: FIRE = FULL VALUE COVERED
  narration: Under a standard HO-3, fire is a named peril with no sublimit on precious metals at most carriers. If your home burns down, your silver pays out at market value up to your personal property limit. That's the scenario most people assume covers everything. It doesn't.
- text: MISSING SILVER = $0 PAYOUT
  narration: Mysterious disappearance is specifically excluded from standard HO-3 language. If your silver is missing and you can't prove a break-in, most carriers will deny the claim outright. No police report, no forced entry, no payout. A scheduled floater covers this scenario. The base policy does not.

## reel-3
angle: cta_direct
hook: 3 steps to insure your silver stack properly before you need to file a claim.
hook_type: immediate_value

### stats
- text: $1-$2 PER $100 FLOATER COST
  narration: A scheduled personal property floater for precious metals costs $1 to $2 per $100 of insured value annually. That's $32 to $64 per year to fully cover a $3,200 silver stack. Less than the cost of 2 ounces of silver at current spot prices.
- text: DOCUMENT EVERY PIECE
  narration: Photograph each coin or bar individually. Log weight, mint, year, and purchase price in a spreadsheet. Save a copy to cloud storage, not just your home computer. That record is what your insurer will ask for when you file. Have it ready before you need it, not after.
