## STAT: 10.49 GRAMS BARS SILVER CUBIC CENTIMETER

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: One of the most reliable methods involves checking the weight and density of the silver bars. Genuine silver bars consistently maintain a specific density, typically around 10.49 grams per cubic centimeter (g/cm³). Counterfeit bars often exhibit inconsistencies in weight or density measurements. You can perform a simple density test yourself: measure the bar’s weight accurately and then measure its volume by submerging it in water.
