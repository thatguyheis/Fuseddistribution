## segments
- type: overlay
 text: RECORD THE LIVE WEBSITE
 narration: When a customer opens your Google Business Profile, the website link should take them to the right business site. Keep a short record of the URL, the account that manages the profile, the important service pages, and the person who can update them. This website access documentation makes handoffs easier and gives you a clear checklist when a page or link changes.
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK CUSTOMER ACCESS
 narration: Open the address you plan to share with customers and confirm that it loads on a phone and a desktop. Check the spelling of the domain, the secure connection, the phone number, the service summary, and the main contact action. Next, list the pages a customer is most likely to need. For example, a plumbing company might record its plumbing repair, water heater, service area, and contact pages.
- type: overlay
 text: KEEP PROFILE DETAILS CURRENT
 narration: If your business uses a booking, ordering, or appointment link, document that separately so the main website link does not get replaced by the wrong destination. Keep the profile and website details consistent. The business name, phone number, hours, service description, and location or service area should be reviewed together whenever one of them changes. If you need the setup steps, see this Google Business Profile setup guide.
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK SEARCH CONSOLE
 narration: Google Search Console offers URL Inspection. This tool can show the current index status of a page and let an owner or full user test the live URL. Check the homepage and the most important service page. Record the inspection date and any issue that needs follow up. If a page was recently changed, use the inspection result as a diagnostic signal, not as a promise that the page will appear immediately.
- type: overlay
 text: NAME THE CHANGE OWNER
 narration: The goal is not a complicated SEO binder. It is a current, private handoff note that answers four questions: Which profile is ours? Which website is live? Who can change it? When did we last verify the customer path? Keep those answers current and website access becomes easier to manage.
