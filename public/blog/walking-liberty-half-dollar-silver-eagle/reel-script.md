# Reel Script: The Walking Liberty design's return
format: long-form
content-kind: educate

---
## HOOK
Narration: Why does the same Liberty appear on a half dollar and a modern Silver Eagle? The design outlasted its first coin.

---
## OVERLAY: 1916 / THE WALKING LIBERTY DESIGN
Narration: In nineteen sixteen, sculptor Adolph A. Weinman's Walking Liberty design appeared on the half dollar. Liberty strides toward the sunrise, wrapped in the American flag. One arm reaches forward; the other carries laurel and oak branches. Her pose gives us a clear way to recognize the design across generations.

---
## OVERLAY: 1947 / 1948 / A NEW HALF DOLLAR
Narration: The half dollar carried Weinman's Liberty from nineteen sixteen through nineteen forty-seven. For much of American coinage history, the half dollar's front showed Liberty and its back showed an eagle. On this design, the reverse eagle stands on a rock beside a pine branch. Then the design changed. In nineteen forty-eight, Benjamin Franklin replaced Liberty on the half dollar, and the reverse changed to the Liberty Bell.

---
## OVERLAY: 1986 / THE SILVER EAGLE
Narration: But the image did not disappear. In nineteen eighty-six, the United States Mint launched the American Eagle coin program for investors, including the Silver Eagle. Its obverse brought back Weinman's Walking Liberty. A design first made for a circulating fifty-cent coin now appeared on a one-ounce silver bullion coin. The purpose had shifted to a precious-metal program, with bullion issues and versions made for collectors.

---
## OVERLAY: 2021 / A MODERN EXAMPLE
Narration: Our photos show two different objects: a Walking Liberty half dollar and a Silver Eagle dated twenty twenty-one. The date on that photographed Eagle identifies this example; it does not mark the design's beginning. The Mint refreshed the Silver Eagle's reverse in twenty twenty-one, while Liberty remained on the front. That split makes the coin a bridge between eras: the familiar face continues as the other side moves forward.

---
## OVERLAY: ONE DESIGN / TWO CHAPTERS
Narration: So when you see Liberty walking toward the sun, look again at which coin you are holding. One image connects a half dollar's nineteen sixteen debut to a modern Eagle program launched in nineteen eighty-six. The coins are different. The design carries the history between them.
