# Mexican Silver Libertad Coins: Sizes, Fineness, and Design

The Libertad is a Mexican bullion and collector coin series produced by the Mexican Mint and distributed through Banco de México. The official specifications vary by size and issue. A useful first step is to check the year, weight, finish, and stated fineness for the exact coin rather than relying on a generic description or a dealer listing.

## Design and background

Banco de México’s history of the silver series describes the National Shield on one side and the Winged Victory design, with the Valley of Mexico and the Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl volcanoes, on the other. The design and the arrangement of the National Shield have changed across issues. Larger sizes added distinct eagle motifs, so compare the specific year and size when using design details for identification.

The series began with one ounce silver pieces. Fractional sizes were added in 1991. Banco de México later added two ounce and five ounce pieces and introduced an updated presentation for the one kilogram coin. These changes mean that size, diameter, finish, and packaging should be checked against the applicable issue information.

## Published sizes and fineness

Banco de México lists silver Libertad sizes including 1 kilogram, 5 ounces, 2 ounces, 1 ounce, one half ounce, one quarter ounce, one tenth ounce, and one twentieth ounce. The modern series information lists .999 fineness across these sizes. Its diameter table differs by size, and the one ounce diameter varies between the older and new series pages. That is a reason to identify the coin’s issue before using diameter as a check.

The official descriptions include brilliant uncirculated, proof-like, and proof finishes for some sizes. Finish is a manufacturing and surface description. It does not establish that a coin will rise in price or sell for a particular premium. Packaging and certificates may also differ by product and year.

## Identify the exact coin before comparing listings

When reviewing a listing, record:

- Issue year and stated weight
- Silver fineness and finish
- Diameter listed for that issue
- Any packaging or certificate described by the mint
- The seller’s condition description and return terms

The mint’s specification pages help check product details, but they do not authenticate an individual coin or independently verify a seller. For a high value or uncertain piece, ask a qualified numismatic professional to examine it. Do not clean, scratch, or apply chemicals to test it.

## Price is not the same as specification

Banco de México’s product descriptions explain the coin series and its technical details. They do not set a universal retail or resale price. Dealer quotes can vary with issue year, finish, condition, availability, fees, and market conditions. Compare current written quotes for the same item and keep bullion value separate from any collector premium.

This guide is for identification and reference. It does not recommend a purchase, forecast returns, or state that one Libertad size or finish is more suitable for a particular person.
