## OVERLAY: PRIMARY MINT MARK INDICATING

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: The primary mint mark is “S,” indicating coins struck at the San Francisco Mint. These are generally the most sought-after and command the highest premiums, often due to limited availability and collector demand. However, coins marked “M” originate from the Mexico City Mint, and “U” comes from the United States Mint. While “M” coins are still desirable, they typically hold slightly lower value than “S” coins.
