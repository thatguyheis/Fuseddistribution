## STAT: 259,000 PIECES RESPECTIVELY MINTAGES CONTINUED

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: This scarcity, coupled with the coin’s patriotic significance, fueled early collector interest. The 1910-D (Denver) and 1910-S (San Francisco) issues were even smaller - just 259,000 and 157,000 pieces, respectively. The mintages continued to decrease throughout the series, with the 1913-S (Philadelphia) being the rarest of all, produced in just 1,400 pieces. The 1914-S, 1916-S, and 1917-S also saw dramatically reduced mintages.
