# Reel Script: website-handover-checklist-for-small-business-owners
format: long-form
segments: 8
target-duration: 128s

---
## HOOK
**Visual:** Topic checklist
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: A website handover should leave the business able to operate its site without relying on one person. A folder of files is only part of that result. Verify account access, recovery options, working copies, renewal responsibilities, and a practical support route before calling the handover finished.

---
## OVERLAY: INVENTORY THE WEBSITE ACCOUNTS
**Visual:** Inventory The Website Accounts
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: List the domain registrar, DNS service, hosting, website editor, email provider, forms, analytics, and any paid extensions. Record the business purpose and account owner for each. Include renewal dates and who receives billing notices. Do not place passwords or recovery codes in this inventory document.

---
## OVERLAY: VERIFY BUSINESS ACCESS INDEPENDENTLY
**Visual:** Verify Business Access Independently
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Have an authorized business representative sign in with their own account while the outgoing provider remains available. Check that the role permits the necessary administration. Verify recovery email and multifactor setup. A screenshot of another person being logged in does not establish that the business can recover access.

---
## OVERLAY: SEPARATE DOMAIN AND HOSTING CHANGES
**Visual:** Separate Domain And Hosting Changes
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Document where the domain is registered and where its DNS records are managed. These may be different from the website host. Record the current arrangement before making changes. If a registrar transfer is needed, check its eligibility and instructions first; do not combine unrelated moves merely to finish a checklist.

---
## OVERLAY: TEST A USABLE WEBSITE BACKUP
**Visual:** Test A Usable Website Backup
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Request the site files, content, configuration, and other exports appropriate to the platform. Note what an export does not include. Restore a copy in a safe test environment if the platform supports it. Confirm the recovery procedure with the owner rather than treating a downloaded archive as proof of recoverability.

---
## OVERLAY: CHECK THE BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
**Visual:** Check The Business Workflows
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Walk through a content edit, image replacement, form inquiry, and any booking or purchase path that applies. Use test modes or harmless inquiries where available. Check who receives notifications and how they respond. Record the steps a colleague would need to repeat these tasks without the original provider present.

---
## OVERLAY: CLOSE THE WEBSITE HANDOVER DELIBERATELY
**Visual:** Close The Website Handover Deliberately
**Duration:** 16s
Narration: Agree who handles updates, outages, renewals, and future changes, then store that contact route with the inventory. Remove obsolete access only after the business confirms its own access and recovery work. Keep a dated acceptance record listing unresolved items and their owners, rather than calling an incomplete handover finished.

---
## QUESTION
**Visual:** Question
**Duration:** 16s
Text: Could your business update and recover its website without calling its original builder?
Subtext: COMMENT BELOW
Narration: A useful handover record is short enough to use during an outage. Keep account locations, responsible people, backup instructions, and unresolved items together, with secrets stored separately. Could your business update and recover its website without the original builder? Verify that ability while help is still available.
