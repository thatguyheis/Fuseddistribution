## STAT: .9999 VS .999 PURITY

**Visual:** Maple Leaf coin close-up, purity markings
**Duration:** 18s minimum (40 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: The Canadian Silver Maple Leaf is .9999 fine silver, 99.99 percent pure. The American Silver Eagle is .999 fine, 99.9 percent pure. One extra nine of purity. At current spot prices, that difference is worth less than a penny per coin. Both are investment-grade silver.

---

## STAT: $4-8/OZ EAGLE PREMIUM

**Visual:** Price tag graphic, spot price vs total cost
**Duration:** 22s minimum (51 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: American Silver Eagles carry a premium of 4 to 8 dollars per ounce above the silver spot price for standard bullion. At 32 dollars spot, that puts the total cost between 36 and 40 dollars per coin. The United States Mint charges authorized dealers a per-coin surcharge, and that cost flows directly to you at the counter.

---

## STAT: $3-6/OZ MAPLE LEAF PREMIUM

**Visual:** Maple Leaf coin, price comparison graphic
**Duration:** 20s minimum (45 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: Canadian Silver Maple Leafs run 3 to 6 dollars over spot for standard bullion. The Royal Canadian Mint distributes through a broader international network with no surcharge structure like the United States Mint uses. For buyers who want the most silver per dollar, Maple Leafs typically have a lower entry cost.

---

## STAT: $1-3/OZ PREMIUM DIFFERENCE

**Visual:** Dollar bills, coin stack, calculator
**Duration:** 23s minimum (53 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: The gap between Eagles and Maple Leafs runs 1 to 3 dollars per ounce. On a 20-coin purchase, that is 20 to 60 dollars in real money. Over a year of buying 10 coins a month, it is hundreds of dollars in extra premium paid for identical silver content. That math compounds fast when you are building a position.

## STAT: RESALE AND LIQUIDITY

**Visual:** Coin shop interior, dealer buying silver
**Duration:** 21s minimum (49 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: American Silver Eagles sell fast at local coin shops in the USA. Most dealers recognize them without any verification and pay up because American buyers specifically request them. Canadian Maple Leafs sell easily through online dealers and internationally, but local coin shops may offer a slightly lower buy-back price compared to Eagles.

## STAT: SECURITY FEATURES

**Visual:** Coin under magnification, radial lines, mint marking
**Duration:** 22s minimum (50 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: The Royal Canadian Mint added Bullion DNA authentication in 2014. Every Maple Leaf has radial line engraving and a micro-engraved maple leaf laser mark. Since 2023, Mintshield surface treatment resists milk spots. The United States Mint redesigned the Eagle reverse in 2021 and added micro-text and enhanced edge patterns.

**Visual:** Split screen comparison, both coins side by side
**Duration:** 21s minimum (48 words / 2.5 + 2)

Narration: Calculate your true cost per ounce for both coins at the same dealer. If the difference is under 1 dollar per coin, buy the Eagle for its resale advantage in the USA. If Maple Leafs are 2 dollars or more cheaper per ounce, the lower entry cost tips the decision.
