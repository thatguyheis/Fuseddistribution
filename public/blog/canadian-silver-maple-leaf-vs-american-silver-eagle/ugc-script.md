## VARIANT A

**Hook (on-camera, direct to lens):**
They both hold the exact same silver.

**Body:**
People argue about which coin is better. Canadian Maple Leaf, American Silver Eagle. Same one troy ounce of silver in each. The difference is what you pay above spot. Maple Leafs run three to six dollars over spot. Eagles run four to eight dollars over spot. On a twenty-coin buy, that gap is twenty to sixty dollars. Know the math before you go to a dealer. Always compare cost per ounce, not just the coin price.

## VARIANT B

**Hook (on-camera, direct to lens):**
Maple Leafs are cheaper than Silver Eagles.

**Body:**
I see people buying Silver Eagles without checking the price of Maple Leafs first. The Maple Leaf is four-nines fine silver, slightly purer than the Eagle's three-nines. The real gap is the premium. Maple Leafs run three to six dollars over spot. Eagles run four to eight dollars over spot. That is a one to three dollar difference per ounce. Over a year of buying, that adds up to real money. Check both prices before you buy either coin.
