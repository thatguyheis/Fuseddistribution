## Fix Your Google Business Profile in 3 Steps

Let’s get your Google Business Profile dialed in. This is your most powerful, free marketing tool. Here’s how to do it right:

1. **Verify Your Listing:** Go to Google Business Profile and verify your business. This confirms you own the listing. It’s a quick process that’ll unlock more features.
2. **Complete Your Profile:** Fill out *every* section. Add detailed descriptions of your services, list your hours of operation (and update them if they change!), and upload high-quality photos of your business, team, and work. Don’t skimp on detail.
3. **Request Reviews Regularly:** Don't be shy! After a successful job, send a personalized message to your clients asking them to leave a review on your GBP. A simple, “Hey {Client Name}, I really enjoyed working on {Project}. Would you mind leaving us a review on our Google Business Profile? It really helps other customers find us.” can go a long way.

Reviews are your new website. Forget spending hours crafting elaborate content. Focus on gathering genuine feedback from your customers. Asking for reviews is a direct investment in your local visibility.
