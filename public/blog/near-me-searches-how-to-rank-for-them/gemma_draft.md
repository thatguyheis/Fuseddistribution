## Fix Your Google Business

Your Google Business Profile is your single most important asset. It is free, and most local businesses set it up incorrectly, missing critical details that stop them from getting calls. This profile controls how you appear on Google Maps and in local search results.

Here is how you fix it today:

**1. Optimize Your Profile Details**
Go to your Google Business Profile. Fill out every section completely. Use your exact business name and make sure your address, phone number, and hours are 100% accurate. If you are a service business, make sure your service area is clearly defined. This tells Google exactly where you operate.

**2. Get Real Reviews**
Google prioritizes proof. Getting real, local reviews is far more effective for ranking than fancy website features. Ask every satisfied customer for a review. Respond to every single review you receive. This builds trust and signals to Google that you are a legitimate, active business. Studies show that businesses with a strong review history see a significant increase in customer trust and conversion rates.

**3. Focus on Proximity**
Local search is not about general keywords. It is about proximity and relevance. You must optimize your profile for your specific service area. Make sure your profile clearly lists the neighborhoods or cities you serve. This ensures that people searching "plumber near me" or "salon downtown" see your business first.

**4. Make Your Website a Call Machine**
Your website should not just be a brochure. It must be a tool designed to get calls. When a potential customer lands on your site, they should immediately know how to hire you. Make your phone number easy to find. Use clear calls to action like "Call Now" or "Book an Appointment." If your website doesn't clearly tell people how to hire you, it is wasting the traffic you earn.
