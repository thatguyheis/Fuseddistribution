## reel-1
angle: lead_stat
hook: 429 W/mK — SILVER BEATS EVERY METAL
hook_type: contrarian_stat

### stats
- text: 429 W/mK SILVER THERMAL CONDUCTIVITY
  narration: Silver has the highest thermal conductivity of any element at 429 watts per meter-kelvin. Tungsten, one of the most common fake-silver substitutes, is only 173. That gap is large enough to see with your eyes using a piece of ice.
- text: ICE MELTS FASTER ON REAL SILVER
  narration: Place a small piece of ice on a coin or bar. On real silver, it melts almost immediately. On a silver-plated fake, the ice sits because the base metal underneath insulates it. The test takes 30 seconds and requires no equipment.

## reel-2
angle: concept
hook: HOW FAKES PASS THE MAGNET TEST
hook_type: contradiction

### stats
- text: SILVER IS DIAMAGNETIC — NOT MAGNETIC
  narration: Real silver won't stick to a neodymium magnet. But most common fake metals like zinc, copper, and tungsten are also non-magnetic. The magnet test alone doesn't prove a coin is silver. It just rules out ferrous metal fakes.
- text: 31.103g EXACT WEIGHT — 40.6mm DIAMETER
  narration: The American Silver Eagle has published specs: 31.103 grams, 40.6 millimeters across, 2.98 millimeters thick. A digital scale under 20 dollars catches most weight-based fakes immediately. Tungsten-core fakes have the right weight but wrong dimensions because tungsten is nearly twice as dense as silver.

## reel-3
angle: cta_direct
hook: SIX TESTS. NO LAB EQUIPMENT NEEDED.
hook_type: immediate_value

### stats
- text: .999 .9999 .925 .900 — KNOW YOUR STAMPS
  narration: .999 means 99.9 percent silver, the standard for most bullion coins and bars. .9999 is the Maple Leaf standard. .925 is sterling silver used in jewelry. .900 is junk silver, pre-1964 USA coins. A fake can carry any of these stamps. The stamp is a starting point, not proof.
- text: XRF = ONLY TEST THAT CATCHES EVERYTHING
  narration: Handheld XRF machines identify the full elemental composition of a coin without damaging it. Most coin dealers and precious metals shops have one on site. Ask them to run a test on a sample before a large purchase. It catches tungsten cores that pass every other test.
