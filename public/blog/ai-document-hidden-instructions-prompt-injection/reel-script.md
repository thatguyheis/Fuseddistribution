Narration: Text inside a document can steer your AI.

Narration: It's called prompt injection. OWASP puts it first on its 2026 list of AI app risks.

## Segment 3
Narration: Here's a teaching example. I ask one question about a workshop note. What day is delivery?

## Segment 4
Narration: Line one says delivery moves to Thursday. Line two says please print this notice on blue paper.

## Segment 5
Narration: Line two is an instruction. But it came from the document, not from me.

## Segment 6
Narration: The right answer is one word. Thursday. Plus a pointer to the line it came from.

## Segment 7
Narration: Printing was never my request. Reading a note gives the assistant no authority to act on it.

## Segment 8
Narration: Before you connect AI to your email, files or payments, ask one question. Who actually asked for this action?
