## OVERLAY: FORECASTS PHYSICAL INVESTMENT RISE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: It forecasts physical investment to rise while industrial fabrication, jewelry, and silverware demand soften at high prices. Tight supply and investment demand can support prices. High prices can also encourage recycling, substitution, and reduced consumption. A credible forecast should show both mechanisms instead of presenting only a bullish case. Use forecasts as scenarios for planning, not as purchase instructions. Is the number a spot price, annual average, year-end target, or intraday high?

## OVERLAY: MARKET FORECAST BECOMES STALE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: A market forecast becomes stale quickly after a large move. Follow the link to the named analyst or institution, not a search result or unattributed summary. A wide range communicates uncertainty better than one precise target. Physical silver includes dealer premiums, spreads, storage, and possible taxes that a spot-price forecast does not capture.
