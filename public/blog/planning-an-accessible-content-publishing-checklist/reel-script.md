## HOOK

**Visual:** Before publish: Check the journey.
**Duration:** 28s minimum

Narration: An accessibility checklist should help the person publishing content make concrete decisions. Organize it around the actual work: drafting headings, choosing images, adding links, preparing media and reviewing the published page. A list stored elsewhere and never used at those moments is unlikely to catch a missed step. W3C WAI planning guidance treats accessibility as ongoing work with responsibilities and resources. The checklist below is an original operational approach informed by that principle.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK STRUCTURE AND MEANING

**Visual:** Check structure and meaning
**Duration:** 25s minimum

Narration: Read the draft headings in order. They should describe the sections and represent their relationship rather than merely change the size of text. Confirm that important instructions remain understandable without relying only on color, position or a decorative symbol. Review link text in its sentence. A reader should have useful context for the destination or action. Our article on accessibility ownership explains assigning review responsibilities.

---

## OVERLAY: REVIEW IMAGES AND MEDIA DELIBERATELY

**Visual:** Review images and media deliberately
**Duration:** 26s minimum

Narration: Decide what each image contributes before writing its text alternative. Describe information needed to understand the content; do not copy a long keyword list. A decorative image and an informative diagram have different purposes. For a complex graphic, provide the important explanation in nearby text as well. For video or audio, check the actual available alternatives and their accuracy. Auto generated captions still need review, particularly names and technical language.

---

## OVERLAY: TEST THE PUBLISHED JOURNEY

**Visual:** Test the published journey
**Duration:** 26s minimum

Narration: Previewing the draft is not the final step. Check the published URL, the links and the important action on the devices and interaction methods in the team's test plan. A content update can expose a template issue that was not visible in the editor. For contact journeys, use our contact form label guide as a focused starting point. Keep checks proportional to the change while retaining a clear escalation route.

---

## OVERLAY: RECORD EVIDENCE AND FOLLOW THROUGH

**Visual:** Record evidence and follow through
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: For each release, save the URL, reviewer, date, checks performed and unresolved issues. Describe the issue in terms another person can reproduce. Avoid recording a blanket statement that the site is accessible merely because one page passed a limited review.
