## OVERLAY: STRUGGLING LOCAL CUSTOMERS SEE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: Are you struggling to get local customers to see your business? Instagram is a powerful tool for local businesses. It lets you connect directly with people in your community. This guide shows you exactly how to use Instagram to build awareness, drive visits, and turn followers into loyal, paying customers right here in your area. We will cover everything from setting up your profile to creating content that stops people from scrolling.

## OVERLAY: WHAT KIND CONTENT ALREADY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: What kind of content do they already engage with? Are they looking for quick tips, local recommendations, or inspiration? Knowing this helps you tailor your message precisely. If you serve coffee, your audience cares about the morning rush or the best local pastry. If you offer plumbing, they care about reliability and quick fixes. We know that a significant number of Instagram users follow at least one business account.

## OVERLAY: USERNAME EASY REMEMBER CLEARLY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Your username should be easy to remember and clearly state what you do. Use it to clearly state your service area and what makes you unique. Include keywords people in your area might search for. For example, instead of just saying "We do great baking," try "Custom Cakes in {Your City Name} | Handmade with Local Ingredients." Use the link in your bio strategically.

## OVERLAY: SHOW PEOPLE WHO WORK

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: Show the people who work for you. Post about your team, your process, or how you source your materials. Post about events happening in your city. Tag local landmarks or partner with other local businesses. This shows you are part of the community. A third pillar is product or service demonstration. This is where you show off what you offer.

## OVERLAY: SHOW ATMOSPHERE SHOP VIEW

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Show the atmosphere of your shop, the view from your office, or the friendly faces of your staff interacting with customers. People buy from brands they feel they understand. Create short video clips that walk a potential customer through your space. A quick tour of your kitchen, a demonstration of a service, or a snapshot of a successful local event works wonders.

## OVERLAY: CREATES EXCITEMENT DRIVES TRAFFIC

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: This creates excitement and drives traffic to your main feed. People follow stories because they want to be part of the ongoing conversation. Location tagging is crucial for local discovery. Every post you make should be tagged with your physical location. This tells the Instagram algorithm exactly where your business is. It helps people searching for things "near me" find you.

## OVERLAY: LOCAL BUSINESSES REELS FANTASTIC

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: For local businesses, Reels are fantastic for showing quick tips, behind-the-scenes action, or local challenges. Create Reels that solve a small, immediate local problem. If you are a landscaper, show a quick fix for a common local garden issue. If you are a restaurant, show a quick, delicious plating technique. Reels get you seen by people who do not follow you yet.

## OVERLAY: SHOW PRESENT CARE ABOUT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: Show that you are present and care about your neighbors. Building these local relationships is the foundation of long-term customer loyalty. Use Instagram Shopping features when available to make the purchase process as easy as possible. Run contests that require tagging local friends to enter.
