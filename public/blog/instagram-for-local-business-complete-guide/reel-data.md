  text: POST THREE TO FIVE TIMES
  narration: Post three to five times a week, tag your exact location on every post, and reply to every comment within an hour. That combination puts your local business in front of nearby buyers faster than any other free tactic on the platform.

  narration: This unlocks the contact buttons, insights, and the address field that pins you to a map. Fill out your bio with three things: what you sell, who you serve, and where you are. - type: overlay
  text: DROP IN OFFERS AS YOU
  narration: Drop in offers as you have them. Sixty-eight percent of Instagram users discover new brands, products, or services while scrolling their feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore page , which means consistent posting is how new neighbors find you in the first place.

  text: SAVE YOUR TAG SETS IN
  narration: Save your tag sets in your phone notes so you can paste them in fast. Reels reach beyond your followers, which makes them your best tool for discovery. A non-follower in your city can land on your Reel through the Explore page or the audio you used.
- type: stat
  text: 24 hours WHICH MAKES THEM PERFECT FOR
  narration: Stories sit at the top of the app and disappear after 24 hours, which makes them perfect for low-pressure, in-the-moment updates. Post one or two Stories most days. They keep you visible without cluttering your main feed.
- type: stat
  text: 15 MINUTES A DAY DOING THIS
  narration: Spend 15 minutes a day doing this: Reply to every comment on your posts, ideally within an hour Answer DMs the same day, since people often message to ask hours or prices Comment on posts from nearby businesses you don't compete with, like the coffee shop next door Like and reply to posts where customers tag your location When you reply fast and sound like a person, people remember.
