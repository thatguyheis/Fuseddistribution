Welcome Email Sequence for New Customers

July 7, 2026 Local Business Marketing

Are you a small business owner feeling overwhelmed by the digital world? You’re not alone. Building a strong online presence can seem daunting, especially when you’re already juggling a million other things. That’s where a well-crafted welcome email sequence comes in. It’s your secret weapon for turning new website visitors into loyal customers. At Fused Distribution, we specialize in helping local businesses like yours thrive online. This isn’t about complicated marketing jargon; it’s about clear communication and providing exactly what you need to succeed. This guide outlines a 10-email sequence designed to onboard new clients, build trust, and ultimately, drive results. Let’s get started.

The Core Problem: New Customers Need Guidance

Many small business owners land a new website through Fused Distribution, and then…nothing. They’ve invested in a beautiful website, but they don’t know how to use it to attract customers. They’re unsure of the best way to promote their business online, and they’re struggling to see a return on their investment. This is a common problem, and the gap is almost always follow-up: most leads go cold because nobody stays in touch after the first contact. A welcome email sequence addresses this head-on by providing a structured path for your new customers to understand your services and start seeing results. It’s about removing the confusion and providing actionable steps.

Email 1: The Immediate Gratification - The Welcome

Subject: Welcome to the Fused Family! Let’s Get Started.

Body:

Hey {Client Name},

Welcome to Fused Distribution! We’re thrilled to have you join our community of local businesses. We know starting a new website can feel a little like jumping into the deep end, so we’re here to guide you every step of the way.

As a first step, we’ve created a quick checklist to help you get the most out of your new website:

  1. Claim Your Google Business Profile: This is critical for local search visibility. It’s free and helps people find you on Google Maps and Search. You can claim yours here: {Google Business Profile Link}
  2. Add Your Business Hours: Make it easy for customers to know when you’re open.
  3. Upload Your Logo & High-Quality Images: A professional look builds trust.

We've also attached a short video tutorial demonstrating how to do these three things. {Video Link - 2-3 minute video showing the steps}.

We're here to support you. If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to reply to this email.

Best,

The Fused Distribution Team

Email 4: Quick Start Guide - Getting Started Easily

Subject: Your First 7 Days with Your New Website

Body:

Hi {Client Name},

Ready to get your new website up and running? Here’s a simple 7-day plan to get you started:

  • Day 1: Claim your Google Business Profile (if you haven’t already - link: {Google Business Profile Link}).
  • Day 2: Update your website with your business hours and contact information.
  • Day 3: Add at least three high-quality images to your website. Photos of your products, services, or team are great.
  • Day 4: Share your website link on your social media channels.
  • Day 5: Encourage your customers to leave a review on your Google Business Profile.
  • Day 6: Send an email to your existing customers announcing your new website.
  • Day 7: Monitor your website traffic in Google Analytics (we’ll provide you with a tutorial).

We’ve created a short video walkthrough to help you through each step: {Video Link - 3-5 minute video}.

Email 6: Customer Success Tip - First Steps for Success
