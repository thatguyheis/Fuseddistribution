# Reel Data: silver-premiums-explained
topic: silver
hook: The spot price is what silver costs. The premium is what you actually pay. Knowing the difference determines how much silver you get for your money.

## stats
- text: "3–8% Premium — Junk Silver"
  narration: Junk silver — pre-1965 US coins — carries the lowest premiums in the market, typically three to eight percent. There's no fabrication cost, just melt value plus a dealer margin. It's the best value for pure silver exposure.
- text: "18–28% Premium — Silver Eagles"
  narration: American Silver Eagles carry an 18 to 28 percent premium above spot. You get US Mint quality, legal tender status, and high liquidity anywhere in the country — but you pay for that reputation.
- text: "~15% Average Premium Saved vs Eagles"
  narration: Buying generic rounds or junk silver instead of Eagles saves roughly 15 percent in premium costs. Over a long accumulation period, that difference means meaningfully more silver for the same dollars spent.
