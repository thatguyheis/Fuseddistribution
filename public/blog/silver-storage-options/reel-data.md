## reel-1
angle: lead_stat
hook: Most people storing silver at home have zero insurance on it. Here's what your options actually cost.
hook_type: pain_point

### stats
- text: $0 HOME INSURANCE
  narration: Standard homeowner's insurance typically caps silver and precious metals at $1,000 to $2,500. If you have more than that, the rest is uninsured. Most people don't know this until after a loss.
- text: $75/YR BANK BOX
  narration: A small safe deposit box at a bank runs $25 to $100 per year. It gets your silver off-site with commercial vault security for less than the cost of a dinner out each month.

## reel-2
angle: concept
hook: Three ways to store silver. They trade off completely differently on cost, access, and insurance.
hook_type: immediate_value

### stats
- text: 8-12 MIN BURGLARY
  narration: The average burglar spends 8 to 12 minutes inside a home. A bolted safe slows them down but most residential models can be defeated with tools in that window. Off-site storage eliminates this risk entirely.
- text: $240/YR FULL COVER
  narration: Third-party allocated vault storage runs about $240 a year for a modest silver position. For that cost you get a segregated account, serial-numbered inventory, and full insurance at current spot prices.
