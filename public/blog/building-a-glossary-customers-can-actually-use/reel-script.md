---

## HOOK

**Visual:** Explain the word. Keep the reader moving.
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: A useful customer glossary explains the words people encounter while trying to complete a task. Start with unfamiliar terms on your service pages, forms and messages. Explain what each term means in that particular context, and make the definition easy to reach without losing the customer's place. W3C's G101 technique discusses definitions for words used in unusual or restricted ways, including specialized vocabulary.

---

## OVERLAY: CHOOSE GLOSSARY TERMS FROM CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

**Visual:** Choose glossary terms from customer questions
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Collect words that customers ask about or that your team routinely has to explain. Keep the original question alongside each candidate. A term that is familiar inside your business may still confuse someone comparing services for the first time. Prioritize words that affect a choice, a price explanation or an instruction. Check whether the term is necessary at all.

---

## OVERLAY: WRITE GLOSSARY DEFINITIONS IN THE RIGHT CONTEXT

**Visual:** Write glossary definitions in the right context
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: Define the meaning used on your website. Avoid pasting every possible dictionary meaning into a short entry. A definition should help the customer interpret the sentence they were reading. Include a concrete, clearly hypothetical example when it clarifies the distinction, and avoid introducing several more unexplained terms to explain the first one. For example, a website provider might define a draft as a version prepared for review, with publication handled as a separate step.

---

## OVERLAY: CONNECT THE GLOSSARY TO THE CUSTOMER TASK

**Visual:** Connect the glossary to the customer task
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Place a short explanation near the term when that is enough. For longer entries, use a descriptive link to the definition. Make it possible to return to the original task. Do not hide essential meaning behind an interaction that customers cannot reliably discover or operate on their device. Keep links consistent when a term appears on several pages.

---

## OVERLAY: TEST GLOSSARY ENTRIES WITH REAL QUESTIONS

**Visual:** Test glossary entries with real questions
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Ask a reader to explain the term in their own words while using the relevant page. Notice whether the definition helps them make the intended decision. Simply asking whether an entry looks clear may miss a misunderstanding. Keep the test question and the observed difficulty so the next edit has a specific purpose.
