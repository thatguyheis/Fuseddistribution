## HOOK

**Visual:** Choose a theme. Build a story.
**Duration:** 28s minimum

Narration: A silver collection can begin with a subject you enjoy rather than a list of things to buy. Write a theme, decide what belongs, and keep a record of why each piece fits. A theme helps organize attention; it does not establish authenticity, future value or investment performance. The U.S. Mint lists design theme and artist among several approaches to collecting. You can use that idea without trying to complete an expensive set.

---

## OVERLAY: CHOOSE A DESIGN THEME YOU CAN EXPLAIN

**Visual:** Choose a design theme you can explain
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Write one sentence describing the subject. “Birds depicted on silver coins” gives you something concrete to investigate. “Coins I like” is a valid personal preference, but it will not help much when you want to compare designs or explain why one item belongs.

---

## OVERLAY: DEFINE WHAT BELONGS IN THE COLLECTION

**Visual:** Define what belongs in the collection
**Duration:** 25s minimum

Narration: Choose the boundaries that actually matter: coins or medals, one issuer or several, a time period, or a particular design feature. Record uncertain cases separately. If a seller's description is the only evidence that an item meets a boundary, mark that fact instead of treating the description as independently verified. Keep metal content separate from appearance. A silver-colored object or a familiar motif does not establish composition.

---

## OVERLAY: SET LIMITS BEFORE LOOKING AT LISTINGS

**Visual:** Set limits before looking at listings
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: Decide how much time, storage space and money you want the project to use. Treat those limits as ceilings, not targets to reach. A theme can remain interesting with a few carefully documented pieces. There is no requirement to fill every gap in a checklist, particularly when the available details are uncertain. Compare complete purchase terms if you consider buying. Keep the item price, delivery, applicable charges and return conditions together.

---

## OVERLAY: MAKE THE COLLECTION RECORD USEFUL

**Visual:** Make the collection record useful
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: Give each item a stable inventory identifier and connect it to its photographs, description and supporting documents. Record the theme feature that caught your attention. Keep observed details separate from information supplied by an issuer, seller or specialist. This lets another reader see where each statement came from. Use consistent photographs for comparison while keeping original files. Different lighting or crop choices can make two designs appear more different than they are.
