# Best Online Silver Dealers in 2026: Compare the Complete Order

The best silver dealer is the one that fits a specific, documented transaction. A low displayed price can lose its advantage after shipping, payment charges, tax, or a product mismatch. A familiar name does not establish the best current offer. This guide compares three US retail dealer candidates and shows how to rank actual quotes without inventing a permanent winner.

Policies below were checked on September 14, 2026. This is a desk review of published terms, not a mystery shopping test, an audit of financial condition, or a claim about customer satisfaction. We have not measured delivery performance or placed the same basket with every dealer. The shortlist is limited to APMEX, JM Bullion, and SD Bullion because their public ordering and selling policies provide concrete comparison points. It is not an exhaustive ranking of every US dealer.

## Rank silver dealers by the order you need

Start with three requirements: the exact product, your intended payment method, and the destination. A one ounce government coin is not interchangeable with a private round, a proof, or a graded collectible. Match year, condition, packaging, quantity, and stated fine metal content before comparing price. An unavailable product should remain unavailable in your worksheet; replacing it with another item changes the question.

Then rank the matching offers by delivered cost. Treat documented shipping, cancellation, return, and resale terms as separate decision criteria. If the lowest cost offer fails a requirement, explain which requirement it fails. This produces a useful ranking for your purchase rather than an unsupported statement that one business is always cheapest or safest.

## APMEX: check the payment basis and quantity tier

[APMEX’s payment FAQ](https://www.apmex.com/faq/how-to-pay-for-gold-silver-purchases-at-apmex) states that eligible check, eCheck, wire, or trade payments receive a 4 percent discount from list or retail prices. It also says displayed product prices generally include that discount. Do not subtract it again from a displayed cash price. Confirm the selected payment method at checkout and include any fee your own bank charges.

Its [pricing FAQ](https://www.apmex.com/faq/silver-gold-pricing) describes quantity tiers on eligible products and a confirmed order as a binding agreement. A volume price is relevant only if you intend to buy that quantity. Buying extra pieces merely to reach a lower unit price increases the total money committed. Compare the order you need before comparing the larger order a promotion encourages.

APMEX belongs on a shortlist when its exact item and payment conditions fit the transaction. These documented features do not demonstrate that it has the lowest current premium or the fastest delivery. Save the product page and final quote rather than relying on a general claim about selection or service.

## JM Bullion: read cancellation and later sale terms

[JM Bullion’s terms](https://cdn.jmbullion.com/terms-and-conditions/) describe cancellation through a discretionary offset request before shipment, with potential market loss. After shipment, cancellation requests to offset an order are denied under the published terms. Read the current agreement before submitting an order; a changing silver price does not turn an accepted order into a free reservation.

For a later sale, [JM Bullion’s payout help](https://help.jmbullion.com/article/508-how-will-you-pay-out-my-buyback-order) lists ACH without a payout fee, bank wire with a $25 fee, and paper check with a $30 fee. That help page was last updated September 14, 2023 and remained available at this review. Reconfirm the terms for your sale. These are payments from the dealer to a seller, not purchase payment charges.

JM Bullion belongs on the comparison sheet when the current basket and terms match your needs. A convenient payout route may matter for a future sale, but it cannot establish what the dealer will bid later. Buying from a dealer does not make a profitable resale automatic.

## SD Bullion: distinguish indicative bids and binding orders

[SD Bullion’s sell page](https://sdbullion.com/sell) describes displayed premiums as indications that can change with inventory needs and market conditions. The page ties its listed silver indications to quantities above 500 ounces. A small collection should obtain its own quote rather than multiplying a large lot indication by a few coins.

The same page lists ACH and bank wire payout routes, with a $30 wire fee. Its [terms](https://sdbullion.com/terms-use) describe telephone cancellation requests and a cancellation fee of 5 percent of the order total or $35, whichever is greater, plus applicable market loss. Cancellation approval is discretionary. Compare these conditions before ordering or locking a sale, and preserve the terms you accepted.

SD Bullion belongs on the shortlist when it offers a matching product and workable transaction terms. The existence of a published bid does not establish the best net sale result. Shipping, inspection, fees, timing, and the locked quote still need to be recorded.

## Verify the transaction and delivery

Use the dealer’s known website and published contact route. Check the legal business name, invoice details, product description, and payment instructions. Website encryption protects a connection; it does not independently establish a seller’s honesty. An unexpected message changing bank instructions should be verified through an independently obtained contact route before payment.

Read when shipping insurance applies, when it ends, how a claim must be reported, and whether redirected deliveries affect coverage. Record handling estimates separately from carrier transit estimates. Keep the order confirmation, packaging, label, delivery record, and photographs of contents. Report discrepancies promptly under the stated procedure rather than assuming a generic return window covers every issue.

For collector coins, grading and condition may matter more than a small metal premium difference. Preserve holders and labels. A photograph, seller description, or attractive packaging is not by itself authentication. Ask an appropriate professional about unusual or valuable material before treating it as ordinary bullion.

## Keep a quote record you can revisit

Save the date and time, product URL, quantity, payment method, merchandise subtotal, shipping, other charges, tax, discounts, and delivered total. Record fine ounces separately from gross alloy weight. Add the policy pages and the quote’s expiration or lock conditions. Keep a separate row for a resale bid with its own date, fees, and shipping responsibilities.

A later change in terms should trigger a new comparison, not a silent change to the old record. Customer reviews can identify questions to investigate, but an unverified rating should not replace written evidence or become a fabricated performance score. Choose from the evidence available for the actual transaction and leave unresolved criteria unresolved.
