## Why Local Businesses Need Google Ads (The Basics)

Google Ads is designed to connect you with people who are *right now* looking for what you offer. Unlike traditional advertising, which tries to reach a broad audience, Google Ads focuses on capturing those immediate, high-intent searches. Someone in your town searching “best pizza near me” is actively looking for a pizza place. That’s a valuable lead. This targeted approach is crucial for small businesses looking to build a local customer base.

Stop tracking clicks. Track calls and quotes. You don’t care about how many people *saw* your ad. You care about how many people actually *call* you or ask for a quote. That’s your real measure of success. Focus your money on getting that phone to ring. A recent study showed that 68% of local searches lead to a purchase within 24 hours. That’s a powerful statistic demonstrating the immediate impact of targeted online advertising.

## Your Google Business Profile Is Your Starting Point

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is absolutely critical. It's your free storefront on Google, and it needs to be impeccable. Think of it as the foundation for your entire Google Ads strategy. A well-optimized GBP dramatically increases your chances of showing up in local search results. Most people set up their GBP incorrectly, leaving it incomplete or outdated. This is a huge missed opportunity.

Don’t try to run a big ad campaign right away. Start small. Pick one service, set a small budget, and see if it brings in calls. If it doesn’t work, you just stop. You’ll save money and learn what resonates with your local customers. A study showed that businesses with complete and optimized GBP profiles receive 2.5x more calls than those with incomplete profiles. That’s a significant difference!

## Measuring Success: Beyond Clicks and Impressions

Google Ads provides a wealth of data, but it’s crucial to focus on the *right* metrics. Don’t get bogged down in impressions (how many times your ad is shown) or clicks (how many people click your ad). Those are vanity metrics. What matters is the *quality* of the leads you’re generating.

Track your cost per call. This is the most direct measure of your campaign’s effectiveness. It tells you how much you’re paying for each phone call generated by your ads. A cost per call of $5 or less is generally considered excellent for local businesses.
