## OVERLAY: DOCUMENT BOTH FACES
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Photograph both faces, edges, serial number, and packaging before you clean or alter a bar. Record weight, dimensions, every mark, and whether the measurement includes a capsule. Exact evidence is more useful than a casual vintage label.
---
## OVERLAY: SEPARATE SILVER FROM COLLECTOR INTEREST
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Separate the silver content from collector interest. NIST identifies a troy ounce as 31.1034768 grams, so use troy units consistently. A bar’s metal content is one question; demand for a specific variety is another.
---
## OVERLAY: COMPARE EXACT VARIETIES
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Poured, extruded, and specialty bars can have different markings and collecting context. Compare logo treatment, wording, hallmark, serial format, dimensions, and finish. A familiar logo does not identify every production variety.
---
## OVERLAY: USE ARCHIVES AS RESEARCH AIDS
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Specialist archives such as AllEngelhard can help compare photographed varieties and serial references. They are research aids, not an official production ledger. A reference image can show that a design exists, but it cannot authenticate the bar in your hand.
---
## OVERLAY: LOG COMPLETED TRANSACTIONS
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Keep a comparable sales log with date, venue, exact variety, weight, fineness, condition, and whether the record is a completed sale. Do not borrow a price from a different size or from one dramatic asking listing.
---
## OVERLAY: RECORD WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN
**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Rarity claims need restraint because registries can be incomplete. Record unknown dates, limited samples, and uncertain provenance. A careful description protects you from paying for an unsupported story and preserves useful evidence for a specialist.
