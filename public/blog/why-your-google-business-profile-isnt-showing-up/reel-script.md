# Reel Script: why-your-google-business-profile-isnt-showing-up
format: long-form

---
## HOOK
**Visual:** Topic intro shot
**Duration:** 10s
Narration: If your Google Business Profile is not showing up, do not start by stuffing keywords. First find out whether the issue is eligibility, verification, suspension, location rules, duplication, or ranking context.

---
## OVERLAY: CHECK ELIGIBILITY
**Visual:** Eligibility checklist
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Google says a Business Profile is for a business that makes in-person contact with customers during stated hours. Online-only businesses, lead generation companies, rental properties, and remote mailboxes may not qualify. Confirm the real customer contact model before changing the listing.

---
## OVERLAY: CONFIRM CLAIM AND VERIFICATION
**Visual:** Owner account and verification card
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: An unclaimed profile is different from a verified profile that ranks lower. Sign in to the account that should manage the listing. Check whether Google asks for verification or shows a pending action, then use the verification method presented. Keep one accountable owner.

---
## OVERLAY: READ SUSPENSION NOTICES
**Visual:** Notice and appeal path
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: If the profile or managing account is suspended, ordinary edits are not the first move. Read the exact notice, review the name, address, category, and service area against current guidelines, and correct the underlying issue before an appeal. Do not create a replacement listing during review.

---
## OVERLAY: MATCH LOCATION RULES
**Visual:** Storefront and service area split
**Duration:** 19s
Narration: A storefront can show a real customer-facing address. A service-area business that travels to customers should hide an address it does not serve and use specific places it actually covers. Check the map pin, signage, hours, and service area. A mailing address alone is not an eligible office. Follow Google’s staffing and customer-contact requirements, and list only areas you serve.

---
## OVERLAY: RULE OUT DUPLICATES
**Visual:** Duplicate profile cards
**Duration:** 14s
Narration: Search Maps for the business name, phone number, address, and website. Look for an older listing, a duplicate managed by another account, or a profile marked closed after a move. Resolve ownership through the proper flow and keep the real business identity consistent.

---
## OVERLAY: SEPARATE VISIBILITY FROM RANKING
**Visual:** Three search tests
**Duration:** 18s
Narration: Test the exact business name, the main service plus a town, and a broader service phrase from a documented location. A profile may appear for its name but lower for a broad query. Google says local results mainly reflect relevance, distance, and prominence. No one can buy a guaranteed position.
