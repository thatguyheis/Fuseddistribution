Narration: Want clear voiceovers without recording?

---
## OVERLAY: Prepare the script
**Visual:** Script card flows into short narration blocks.
**Duration:** 22s
Narration: Start with a script written for the ear. Use short sentences, say names and numbers the way you want them spoken, and split the narration where a person would naturally pause. In our local profile, chunks stay under 240 characters. That is a production rule for this setup, not a universal model limit.

---
## OVERLAY: Confirm voice and model
**Visual:** Voice permission, model version, and settings checklist.
**Duration:** 21s
Narration: Confirm the Chatterbox model you installed and use only a voice reference you have permission to reproduce. Standard, Turbo, and other variants can expose different controls. Our Standard profile uses exaggeration point seven five and CFG point three, but those values are only a starting point for this local workflow. Test one short passage before a batch.

---
## OVERLAY: Listen before the batch
**Visual:** Audio waveform with seed and settings record.
**Duration:** 21s
Narration: Give each new segment a fresh random seed and record it with the settings. Then listen for pronunciation, pacing, and emphasis. Check the measured duration and peak level too. A seed makes the generation traceable; it cannot tell you whether the voice sounds right. If one line misses, replace that line and keep approved audio unchanged.

---
## OVERLAY: Captions follow final audio
**Visual:** Narration moves through transcription, captions, and export review.
**Duration:** 22s
Narration: Once the voice track passes, use Whisper to transcribe the audio and time captions. Compare names, technical terms, and the final call to action with the approved script. Then render the video and inspect the actual file. Check the opening image, phone sized captions, voice over music, and that the last sentence is complete.

---
## OVERLAY: Keep a generation record
**Visual:** Reusable checklist: text, model, seed, duration, review.
**Duration:** 18s
Narration: Keep the text, model version, settings, seed, output path, duration, and review status together. That record lets you reproduce a good take and retry only the line that needs work. Publish after the voice, captions, media rights, and final export have all passed review.
