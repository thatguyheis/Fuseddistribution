# Reel Script: A Repeatable AI Reel Workflow, Part 2
format: long-form
segments: 8
target-duration: 115s

## HOOK
**Visual:** A topic card enters a twelve step flow graphic, then loops back to the start.
**Duration:** 5s minimum

Narration: An AI reel workflow is not a button. It is a loop with clear owners and a real review step.

---

## OVERLAY: GATHER AND APPROVE
Narration: The flowchart begins with real business material, then a topic bank and approved ideas. Its graphic has ten numbered boxes, while its written checklist adds cost control and handoff to existing posting help. Keep the audience, question, evidence, and one main lesson with each topic. Get permission for people and footage. Protect private details before production.

---

## OVERLAY: ORGANIZE THE BACKLOG
Narration: Label B roll by what it actually shows. Save its source, license, and permission beside the clip. Choose footage that fits the narration. Stock video is support, not proof that a scene happened to your customer or your team.

---

## OVERLAY: LOCK THE VOICE
Narration: Pick one permitted voice. Review names and numbers in the final recording, then check captions against the audio. Keep the topic brief, script, and files together so a reviewer can tell whether the finished edit still matches what was approved.

---

## OVERLAY: TEST AND FINE TUNE
Narration: Make a small batch, watch every exported video, and write down the exact problems. Fix facts, pacing, text size, or mismatched scenes. Change one major element at a time. Then watch the new export again. A corrected script does not repair an old video file.

---

## OVERLAY: TRACK COST AND CADENCE
Narration: Track time, paid services, local compute, and rework. Keep a posting pace you can sustain while still checking the work. Two posts a day may suit one team, but it is not a universal reach rule. YouTube says Shorts have no minimum cadence for performance.

---

## OVERLAY: PUBLISH AND REVIEW
Narration: Choose a home platform, then adapt the video for other places your customers use. Check the crop, captions, music rights, and link. Record the platform confirmation. Later, compare similar posts using retention, comments, shares, inquiries, and sales you can actually verify.

---

## OVERLAY: CLOSE THE LOOP
Narration: Keep what works, retire what does not, and add new customer questions to the topic bank. The flowchart is available with the full article. Use it to see who owns each step and where the next review happens.
