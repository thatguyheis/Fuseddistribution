  narration: Complete Google Business Profiles get 7 times more clicks than incomplete ones. That is the gap between a profile with every field filled in and one that just has a name and address. The difference costs nothing to close.

- text: FREE AT BUSINESS.GOOGLE.COM
  explanation: Google Business Profile is completely free. No charge to create, verify, or manage
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Google Business Profile is completely free. There is no subscription, no listing fee, and no charge to respond to reviews. You create and manage it at business.google.com using any Google account.

- text: PRIMARY CATEGORY IS MOST IMPORTANT
  explanation: Primary business category is the single highest-weight ranking field in GBP. It determines which searches the profile appears in
  graphic_type: none
  narration: The primary business category is the single most important field in your entire profile. It determines which search queries you are eligible to appear for. Choose the most specific category that accurately describes your main service. You can add secondary categories for everything else.

- text: NAP CONSISTENCY ACROSS THE WEB
  explanation: Name, Address, Phone must match exactly across your website, GBP, and other online listings for local ranking
  graphic_type: none
  narration: NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone. Those three fields must match exactly across your website, your Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, and every directory listing. Inconsistencies signal distrust to Google and suppress local ranking even when the rest of your profile is complete.

- text: 5-14 DAYS POSTCARD VERIFICATION
  explanation: Postcard verification is the most common GBP verification method. The code arrives by mail in 5-14 days
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Google mails a postcard with a 5-digit verification code to your business address. It arrives in 5 to 14 days. Some businesses qualify for faster options: phone or text verification is immediate when available, and video verification typically resolves in 3 to 5 business days.

- text: 42% MORE DIRECTION REQUESTS WITH PHOTOS
  explanation: Profiles with photos receive 42 percent more requests for directions than profiles without photos
  graphic_type: percent_fill
  graphic:
    value: 42
    label: Direction Lift
    remainder_label: No Photos
  narration: Profiles with photos receive 42 percent more requests for directions than profiles without any photos. You need at least 10 to start. Exterior shots, interior shots, team photos, and product or work examples. Minimum size is 720 by 720 pixels. Update with new photos monthly to signal an active profile.

- text: REVIEW LINK FROM YOUR DASHBOARD
  explanation: GBP dashboard provides a short link to send customers directly to your review page
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Once verification is complete, go to your GBP dashboard and find the Get More Reviews section. Copy that short link. Send it in follow-up emails, text messages, and on receipts. The best time to ask is 24 to 48 hours after service, while the experience is still fresh.
