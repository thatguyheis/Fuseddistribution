## OVERLAY: PICK ONE CHANNEL

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Choose one channel first. Email is usually the easiest place to start because message history is visible and staff already expect to review drafts before they send them.

## OVERLAY: BUILD AN ANSWER LIBRARY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Write down the approved answers your business already gives. Cover hours, service area, booking steps, pricing rules, cancellations, and what the tool must never answer on its own.

## OVERLAY: DRAFT BEFORE AUTO REPLY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Use AI for draft mode before direct replies. Let the tool write the first version, then have a staff member confirm facts, tone, and promised next steps.

---

## OVERLAY: SET ESCALATION RULES

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 13s minimum

Narration: Create hard escalation rules from day one. Billing issues, custom quotes, complaints, legal threats, and safety concerns should always go to a person.

---

## OVERLAY: SAVE PROMPT TEMPLATES

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Give your team saved prompt templates instead of asking each person to invent the process from scratch. Reusable prompts make the output more consistent and easier to review.

---

## OVERLAY: PROTECT CUSTOMER DATA

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 13s minimum

Narration: Only feed the tool the information it needs for the task. Redact sensitive details during testing and keep private account notes out of public AI systems.

## OVERLAY: MEASURE THE REAL WIN

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Measure whether the setup actually helps. Track first response time, edit time, escalations, corrections, and whether customers are getting clearer answers.

## OVERLAY: ASSIGN ONE OWNER

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: One person needs to own the answer library, prompt rules, and failure review. AI customer service gets messy fast when everybody assumes somebody else is checking it.
