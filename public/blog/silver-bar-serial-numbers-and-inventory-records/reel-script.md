## OVERLAY: SILVER BAR INVENTORY PRIVATE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: A silver bar inventory is a private record of what you own, what the item says about itself, and which documents support the purchase. A serial number can help you match a bar to a receipt or a supplier record, but it is not a stand alone authenticity certificate and it does not create a public ownership history. If a bar is unnumbered, record that fact instead of inventing an identifier.

---

## OVERLAY: RECORD REFINER BRAND MARK

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Record the refiner or brand mark, marked fineness, marked weight or unit, production date if present, and any assay or product code. Do not normalize a mark by guessing that a smudged character is a zero or an O. Use one row per bar when the item has its own number.

---

## OVERLAY: STORE RECEIPTS CORRESPONDENCE ACCESS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Store receipts and correspondence with access controls appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. The Perth Mint counterfeit guidance recommends checking the seller, product specifications, and physical characteristics. Packaging, certificates, and security features vary, so do not turn a feature of one product into a rule for every silver bar. When a bar arrives, compare the item with the order and invoice before filing it as complete.

---

## OVERLAY: STRUCTURE MAKES POSSIBLE ANSWER

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: This structure makes it possible to answer a simple question later: which document supports the current status of this item? Back up the record using a method you can restore and test. Restrict editing access, protect exported files, and review who can see photos, invoices, and storage notes. Avoid publishing the number of bars you hold or where they are kept.

---

## OVERLAY: COPY EVERY VISIBLE MARK

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Copy every visible mark, link the purchase document, record the unknowns, and add one change log entry if the item has moved. If you are deciding between product formats, compare the record fields with our guide to silver coins, rounds, and bars. Can you trace the current status of that item to the document that supports it? If not, keep the gap visible and make verification the next action.
