# Separate Damaged Coin Holders for Careful Review

hook: Separate Damaged Coin Holders for Careful Review

## segments
- type: overlay
 text: IDENTIFY THE HOLDER
 narration: Record the collection ID, holder type, and where the damage is visible. Photograph the holder from a position that does not require extra handling of the coin. Note whether the issue was present when you received the item or was noticed later. If you cannot establish when it occurred, say that. Do not turn a recent discovery into an unsupported claim that the damage is new.
- type: overlay
 text: RECORD REVIEW LOCATION
 narration: Give the item a clearly identified review location within your existing secure storage arrangement. Label the surrounding container or index card rather than adding adhesive to the coin or its holder. Update the inventory location immediately so the item does not appear lost during the next count. Keep unrelated holders out of the review group unless you have observed a reason to include them.
- type: overlay
 text: RETAIN EVIDENCE
 narration: Keep the purchase record, seller description, and earlier photographs connected to the same inventory entry. Preserve the existing holder and packaging when asking for advice about a problem. Do not scrub residue or polish the coin to improve a photograph. A clean looking image is less useful if the action changes the condition you were trying to document or removes evidence needed to understand the issue.
- type: overlay
 text: ASK SPECIFIC QUESTIONS
 narration: Describe the holder problem to a qualified coin professional and ask what handling or replacement approach is appropriate. Explain whether the coin is in original packaging or a grading service holder. Avoid assuming that damage to the case establishes damage to the coin. Record the advice, who provided it, and the next agreed step instead of converting a general comment into a promise about condition or value.
