Simple Website Speed Tests You Can Do Yourself

Simple Website Speed Tests You Can Do Yourself

As a local business owner, you know that first impressions matter - and that includes your website. A slow website can drive potential customers away before they even read a single word. Luckily, you don't need to be a tech expert to check your site’s speed. There are several free tools you can use to get a quick assessment. Google PageSpeed Insights is a fantastic starting point; it gives you a score and clear recommendations for improvement. Another easy option is GTmetrix, which breaks down your website’s performance into detailed sections like loading speed, page size, and requests.

These tests will highlight areas like large image files, too many plugins, or unoptimized code that are slowing things down. For example, studies show that a one-second delay in page load time can result in a 7% decrease in conversions. Don’t let a sluggish website hurt your bottom line - a few minutes of testing can reveal valuable insights and help you optimize your site for a better user experience and increased sales.
