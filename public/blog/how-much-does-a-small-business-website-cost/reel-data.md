  narration: The template-build tier costs $500 to $2,000 one time. A freelancer or small agency takes a pre-made theme, swaps in your branding, writes a few pages, and launches it. Fast and cheap, with limited custom work. The custom tier costs $3,000 to $8,000 one time .
- type: overlay
  text: MOST OWNERS CAN LAUNCH A

  text: $100 TO PER PAGE KEYWORD RESEARCH
  narration: Words and photos cost money if you do not supply them. Professional copywriting runs $100 to $400 per page. Keyword research and local search optimization usually cost extra. - type: stat
  text: $150 TO A YEAR FOR DOMAIN
  narration: Budget $150 to $300 a year for domain plus hosting on a self-hosted setup. A website is not a one-time purchase. Ongoing upkeep for a professionally built small business site, covering the domain, hosting, and routine updates, averages $150 to $500 per month .
- type: overlay
  text: THE SECOND OPTION IS WHY

  text: $16 TO A MONTH DO THE
  narration: Pay $16 to $40 a month, do the work yourself, and launch in a weekend. Pay for a template build at $1,000 to $2,000. You get a professional look without custom-build prices, and you can launch in two to three weeks. Invest in a custom build at $3,000 to $8,000.
