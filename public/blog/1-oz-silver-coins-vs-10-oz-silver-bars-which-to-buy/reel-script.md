## HOOK

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Compare ten one ounce silver coins with one ten ounce silver bar before deciding which format suits your needs. First verify that both quotes describe the same total fine silver content in troy ounces. Equal metal content does not mean equal purchase prices, resale offers or transaction costs.

## OVERLAY: MATCH THE PRODUCTS

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Record the exact coin or bar, maker, stated weight, purity and condition. Distinguish ordinary bullion from collectible editions before comparing prices. A description that only says silver coin is not enough. Ask the seller to identify the product and explain any premium tied to its particular version or packaging.

---

## OVERLAY: COMPARE TOTAL COST

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Use quotes from the same time and payment method. Add the stated product price, shipping, insurance, payment charges and applicable taxes to find the delivered total. Divide that amount by the verified fine silver ounces. Label any missing charge as unknown rather than treating an incomplete quote as cheaper.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK SMALLER SALES

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Ten separate coins let you offer fewer pieces for sale while keeping the others. A single bar is one item to sell. That practical difference does not guarantee a buyer, a better price or faster payment. Ask prospective buyers which exact products they accept and what deductions they apply.

---

## OVERLAY: ASK ABOUT BUYBACK

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Request a current buyback quote as well as a selling price. The difference helps reveal the transaction spread today; it does not predict a future return. Include testing, shipping and other stated resale costs when comparing the amount you would receive. Keep the quote time and conditions with your notes.

---

## OVERLAY: PLAN SAFE STORAGE

**Visual:** Original equal silver quantity comparison
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Check the dimensions and packaging of the actual products before choosing storage. Keep invoices and identifying details in a separate inventory record. Follow the maker or a qualified conservator on handling questions instead of assuming that a container guarantees preservation or value. Confirm insurance coverage for your specific arrangement.
