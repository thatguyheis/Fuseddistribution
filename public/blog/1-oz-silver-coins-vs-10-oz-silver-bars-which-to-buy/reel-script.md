## STAT: 12-22 percent COIN PREMIUM
Visual: Percent fill graphic — 18 percent
Narration: When you buy a 1 oz Silver Eagle, you pay 12 to 22 percent above spot price. That covers minting, distribution, and dealer margin. At $32 spot, total cost runs $35.84 to $39.04 per ounce.
Duration: 8

---

## STAT: 4-6 percent BAR PREMIUM
Visual: Percent fill graphic — 5 percent
Narration: A 10 oz silver bar from a major bullion mint costs 4 to 6 percent over spot. At the same $32 spot, that is $33.28 to $33.92 per ounce. That is a meaningful difference.
Duration: 7

---

## STAT: 416 DOLLAR DIFFERENCE ON 100 OZ
Visual: Side-by-side dollar comparison graphic
Narration: Run the math on 100 ounces. One hundred Silver Eagles at 18 percent premium costs $3,776. Ten 10 oz bars at 5 percent costs $3,360. The difference is $416. That buys over 12 additional ounces of silver at spot.
Duration: 9

## CHART: PREMIUM COMPARISON
Visual: Horizontal bar chart — 10 oz bar at 5 percent, 1 oz round at 8 percent, Silver Eagle at 18 percent
Narration: The chart shows why format matters. Generic bars run about 5 percent over spot. Rounds sit around 8 percent. Silver Eagles carry the highest premium at around 18 percent. The format you choose determines how much silver you get for your dollar.
Duration: 9

## STAT: 5X STORAGE FOOTPRINT
Visual: Storage space comparison graphic
Narration: Storage is where format shows its cost in a different way. One hundred individual coins need 100 capsules or five tubes. Ten bars stack flat and take up a fraction of the space. For a home safe where every inch counts, bars are the smarter fit.
Duration: 8

---

## STAT: 1 to 3 DOLLAR RESALE EDGE ON COINS
Visual: Resale premium comparison
Narration: Coins win on exit. American Silver Eagles are the most recognized silver product in the USA. Dealers pay $1 to $3 per ounce above their buy price on Eagles. Generic 10 oz bars usually sell at or near spot. If you need to sell fast or in small amounts, coins give you more options.
Duration: 10

---

## STAT: 50 OZ BREAKEVEN POINT
Visual: Stack of silver bars
Narration: Below 50 ounces, the dollar difference between coins and bars is real but modest. Above 50 ounces, the savings compound. Most buyers building a serious position choose bars for the bulk of their stack and add coins selectively for flexibility.
Duration: 9

## DECISION GUIDE
Visual: Two-column comparison graphic
Narration: Choose bars for maximum silver per dollar, long-term holds, and home safe storage. Choose coins when you want to sell individual pieces, prefer the most liquid silver format, or plan to gift silver. Most buyers end up using both.
Duration: 9
