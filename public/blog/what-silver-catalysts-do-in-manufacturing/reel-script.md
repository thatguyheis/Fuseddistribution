## HOOK

**Visual:** Silver helps make it. Where does it act?
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Silver can help a manufacturing reaction take place without being the material a customer buys. The Silver Institute identifies silver catalysts in the production of ethylene oxide and formaldehyde. That is a different role from silver used as an electrical contact, a coating or a collectible coin. The useful question is where the silver acts.

---

## OVERLAY: FOLLOW THE SILVER CATALYST THROUGH THE PROCESS

**Visual:** Follow the silver catalyst through the process
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Start with a simple diagram: input chemicals, a reaction using a silver catalyst, and a chemical output. Label the diagram as conceptual. It should explain the relationship without pretending to show a particular factory, operating temperature or equipment design. Those details require documentation for the actual process. On a reading worksheet, give each stage its own line.

---

## OVERLAY: CONNECT SILVER CATALYSTS TO NAMED CHEMICALS

**Visual:** Connect silver catalysts to named chemicals
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: The Silver Institute describes ethylene oxide as a starting point for materials including polyester. It also describes silver catalysts in making formaldehyde from methanol, with downstream uses in resins and adhesives. These examples explain how an industrial material can contribute to products far removed from a silver bar. Use those examples as routes to investigate, not universal product specifications.

---

## OVERLAY: SEPARATE CATALYST USE FROM SILVER CONSUMPTION

**Visual:** Separate catalyst use from silver consumption
**Duration:** 24s minimum

Narration: A claim about silver used in a process needs a defined measure. Ask whether the author means silver held in equipment, fresh purchases, material recovered or a net demand estimate. Write the original label next to any number. Do not combine quantities simply because they are all expressed in ounces. The distinction is similar to the one in our guide to mine and refined production.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK A SILVER CATALYST CLAIM BEFORE SHARING IT

**Visual:** Check a silver catalyst claim before sharing it
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Save the source URL, its title, the date you checked it and the exact claim you intend to make. Then write a plain explanation of what the source supports. Keep your interpretation in a separate field. This small separation makes it easier to catch an unsupported jump from an industrial application to a claim about investment returns.
