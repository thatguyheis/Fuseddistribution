## STAT: 20 BUSINESS REVIEWS ONLY HALF

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: To get your direct review link, go to your Google Business Profile dashboard and click "Ask for reviews." Copy the URL. Run it through a free link shortener like bit.ly so you have a clean, short link to paste into texts. Getting to 20 reviews is only half the problem. 74 percent of consumers only trust reviews written within the last three months, and 32 percent want reviews no older than two weeks.

## OVERLAY: AFTER FEW MONTHS REVIEWS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: After a few months, reviews come in steadily without any extra push because the habit is already running. If your team handles customer contact, assign one person to own review requests. Give them the message template and the short link. Track it in a simple spreadsheet: customer name, job date, request sent, review received. If requests are going out but reviews aren't coming back, your message or timing needs to change.

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: For most local service businesses, 2 to 4 new reviews per month is enough to maintain freshness and build volume steadily over time.
