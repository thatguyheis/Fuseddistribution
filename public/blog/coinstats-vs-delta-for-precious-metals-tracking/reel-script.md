## HOOK
**Visual:** App charts beside inventory card
**Duration:** 10s
Narration: A portfolio chart cannot prove that a silver bar exists. Here is what CoinStats and Delta document, and where physical bullion needs its own record.
---
## OVERLAY: COINSTATS SCOPE
**Visual:** Manual portfolio
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: CoinStats documents manual portfolios, transaction entry, wallet connections, and portfolio analytics. Its official help does not establish automatic physical bullion inventory or vault verification.
---
## OVERLAY: DELTA SCOPE
**Visual:** Investment overview
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Delta describes itself as a read-only investment tracker. Its documentation covers stocks, funds, ETFs, crypto, wallets, indices, and broker connections. That is a market portfolio view, not custody of coins or bars.
---
## OVERLAY: SECURITY VERSUS BULLION
**Visual:** ETF beside coin
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: A silver ETF or mining share can provide market exposure. It is still a security, not allocated bullion in a safe or depository. A symbol is not evidence of ownership.
---
## OVERLAY: BUILD THE PHYSICAL RECORD
**Visual:** Inventory card
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: For coins, record an inventory ID, denomination, date, mint mark, stated fineness, weight, photographs, invoices, and storage location. For bars, include maker, serial number, assay card, packaging, and storage details.
---
## OVERLAY: KEEP CATEGORIES SEPARATE
**Visual:** Separate categories
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Keep physical holdings, ETFs, mining equities, and other proxies separate. Do not force a coin into a ticker merely to make an app dashboard balance. Preserve custody records outside the tracker.
---
## OVERLAY: RECONCILE RECORDS
**Visual:** Export and invoice folder
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Export app data when supported and reconcile it with invoices after every purchase, transfer, sale, or storage change. App analytics cannot authenticate an item or replace missing records.
