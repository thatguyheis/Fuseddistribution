## segments
- type: overlay
 text: HEAT TRANSFER IS NOT PROOF
 narration: An ice cube melting on a metal object is a demonstration of heat transfer. It is not proof that the object is silver, and it cannot tell you its silver percentage. The useful question is what the observation actually establishes. A fast melt can show that heat is reaching the ice efficiently in that particular setup. It does not identify every material inside the object or verify the seller's description. Silver is not the only good heat conductor; copper also conducts heat well.
- type: overlay
 text: DOCUMENT THE STARTING CONDITIONS
 narration: A comparison is easier to interpret when the starting conditions are documented. Record whether the objects began at the same temperature, whether the ice pieces were comparable, and how the ice contacted each surface. A broad flat bar and a small curved item are different setups. The supporting surface and any protective packaging also belong in the description.
- type: overlay
 text: PROTECT THE ITEM DURING CHECKS
 narration: Do not describe the demonstration as guaranteed to catch plated items. A method must be assessed against the question it is supposed to answer, including what it can miss. Decide whether a demonstration is appropriate for the item at all. An educational experiment can use an inexpensive object whose handling is acceptable. A collectible coin, a sealed graded holder, or an item that belongs to someone else presents a different situation.
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK THE AUTHENTICATION SCOPE
 narration: Some products have an authentication route tied to their manufacturer. The Royal Canadian Mint describes Bullion DNA as a system through which approved dealers compare security mark images with registration data. Its stated coverage includes Silver Maple Leaf bullion coins dated 2015 and later. This is a defined system for covered products, not a universal test for all silver. Ask whether the exact issue is supported.
- type: overlay
 text: ASK WHAT EACH CHECK MEASURES
 narration: For a purchase, use suitable product evidence and a clear remedy if the item differs from its description. Fused's reserve resources provide further educational material. The practical lesson is to ask what each check measures before deciding what its result means.
