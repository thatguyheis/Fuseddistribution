topic: silver
focus: Best Apps to Track Your Silver Portfolio Value
format: long-form
hook: Tracking silver starts with the kind of asset you own. A physical inventory and a market portfolio answer different questions, even when both display a dollar value.
hook_type: practical
## segments
- type: overlay
 text: PHYSICAL INVENTORY
 narration: For coins and bars, record identity, quantity, documented fine silver, invoices, photographs, and storage. Keep serial numbers and sensitive locations private. An app chart cannot authenticate an item.
- type: overlay
 text: SHEETS AND EXCEL
 narration: Google Sheets and Excel support custom tables and formulas. Use a controlled workbook for physical records, transaction history, and calculations. Keep your inputs and assumptions visible.
- type: overlay
 text: COINSTATS SCOPE
 narration: CoinStats documents crypto portfolios, wallet connections, and manual transactions in supported assets. Those instructions do not establish custom physical bullion inventory. Keep coins and bars separately.
- type: overlay
 text: DELTA SCOPE
 narration: Delta documents a read only market investment view. A silver ETF or mining share is a security, not a physical bar. Keep shares and fine ounces in separate categories.
- type: overlay
 text: COUNT FINE OUNCES ONCE
 narration: For ten pieces each specified as one fine troy ounce, record ten fine ounces. Do not apply fineness a second time. Gross alloy weight requires a different calculation.
- type: overlay
 text: COST IS NOT A BID
 narration: Divide total delivered cost by total fine ounces for acquisition cost. A dated buyer bid gives a separate sale estimate before fees. Neither an app total nor spot alone is an appraisal.
