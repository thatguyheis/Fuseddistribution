Narration: In 1918, Britain urgently needed silver to redeem paper rupee currency in India. The United States had a large supply sitting in Treasury vaults, already made into silver dollars. Turning those coins into bullion could help meet that demand.

**Visual:** 350 MILLION / THE LEGAL LIMIT
Text: 350 MILLION / THE LEGAL LIMIT
Narration: On April twenty-third, Congress approved the Pittman Act. It authorized the Treasury to melt as many as 350 million silver dollars. That figure was the legal ceiling, not the number actually melted.
