## What Are Silver Panda Coins?

Silver Panda [coins](/blog/mexican-silver-libertad-coins-guide/) are officially produced by the People’s Bank of China and are denominated in Chinese Yuan (CNY). They’re made of .999 fine silver, meaning they contain 99.9% pure silver. While initially produced in 1983, the modern Silver Panda coins, which are the most commonly traded today, began production in 2009. They’re instantly recognizable by their distinctive panda design, which changes annually. The coins come in a variety of sizes and weights, offering flexibility for different budgets and investment goals. Currently, there are several sizes available: 1 oz, 2 oz, 5 oz, 10 oz, and 1 kg. The 1 oz size is the most popular among retail investors.
