# Reconcile a Silver Inventory After a Sale

hook: Reconcile a Silver Inventory After a Sale

## segments
- type: overlay
 text: MATCH SOLD ITEMS
 narration: Compare the transaction description with the coin or bar records before changing their status. Check quantity, visible identifiers, and any holder or certificate reference used in the agreement. If an item cannot be matched confidently, leave the discrepancy open while you review the paperwork. Do not pick a similar entry simply to make the collection count balance. A correct unresolved note is more useful than a neat but inaccurate register.
- type: overlay
 text: RECORD THE HANDOVER
 narration: List the holders, original packaging, certificates, or other records included in the handover. Keep copies of the relevant transaction documents where appropriate in your private records. Record the date and method of dispatch or collection using the evidence available. If the recipient later reports a discrepancy, this list provides a starting point for checking what was agreed and recorded, without inventing a conclusion about responsibility.
- type: overlay
 text: PRESERVE HISTORY
 narration: Mark the matched entries with a clear transaction status and date instead of erasing them from your only inventory file. Retain the original acquisition record and earlier observations alongside the new event. If your system uses a separate archive, confirm that the links to photographs and paperwork still work after moving the entry. An archived item should remain traceable even though it no longer belongs in the current physical count.
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK REMAINING COUNT
 narration: Compare the remaining recorded quantity with the relevant storage location after the handover. Keep this check focused on the affected items and record any difference before making adjustments. A count mismatch may involve an outdated location, a group recorded differently, or an entry that needs investigation. Do not rewrite several old transactions to force agreement. Document the discrepancy and resolve it from records and an actual count.
