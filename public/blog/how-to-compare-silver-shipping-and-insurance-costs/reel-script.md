# Reel Script: how-to-compare-silver-shipping-and-insurance-costs
format: long-form
segments: 7
target-duration: 120s

---

## HOOK

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 11s minimum

Narration: A silver shipping quote is useful only when you compare the whole order. Keep the products, quantities, payment method and destination consistent, then check the final amount and the receiving terms.

---

## OVERLAY: MATCH THE CARTS

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Write down the exact product and quantity before opening several carts. Different coins, bar sizes or product conditions can change the comparison before shipping is considered. Record when you checked each quote. Keep a provisional estimate separate from a final checkout total, and mark an unknown charge as unknown rather than zero.

---

## OVERLAY: COUNT EACH CHARGE ONCE

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: List the product subtotal, shipping, optional services, any separately charged transit protection and the taxes shown at checkout. Check that the lines add up to the displayed total. Do not add insurance again if it is already included. If you add products to reach free shipping, compare that larger purchase with the order you actually intended.

---

## OVERLAY: READ THE DELIVERY TERMS

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Find the current seller policy and record when its transit protection ends. Check how signatures, unattended delivery or a different recipient affect the arrangement. A carrier option should not be assumed to preserve the seller protections. Ask the seller about your specific receiving plan before changing delivery instructions, and save the response with your order.

---

## OVERLAY: PLAN THE HANDOFF

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Separate the payment clearing period, the dispatch estimate and the carrier transit estimate. Decide whether someone can receive the order under the stated conditions. Find the support channel and reporting deadline before you need them. If a shipment is missing or damaged, follow the seller instructions and retain the relevant order details and evidence.

---

## OVERLAY: KEEP OTHER RISKS SEPARATE

**Visual:** Order comparison diagram
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: A return shipment can use different terms from delivery to you. Get the seller instructions before choosing your own label or carrier. Likewise, transit protection does not establish coverage after the silver arrives. Keep storage questions separate. The useful comparison shows both what you will pay and what you must do at the handoff.
