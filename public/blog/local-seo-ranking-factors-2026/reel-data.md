- text: SERVICE AREA DEFINITION IS CRITICAL
  explanation: Service-area businesses must define specific cities they serve in GBP, not broad radii
  graphic_type: none
  narration: If you're a contractor, cleaner, plumber, or any service business that goes to the customer, your service area settings in GBP are the primary geographic signal Google uses to match you with nearby searches. Don't set a radius or claim an entire county. Set the specific cities you actually work in. Broad areas reduce precision and hurt your ranking in the places that matter most.

- text: RESPOND TO REVIEWS IN 24 HOURS
  explanation: Fast review responses signal to Google that you are an actively managed, engaged business
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Responding to reviews is both a ranking signal and a conversion signal. Google treats review responses as evidence of an active, engaged business. Customers read them before calling. Respond to every review within 24 hours. For negative reviews, acknowledge the issue, skip the excuses, and offer a resolution. A professional response to a one-star review converts more new customers than that review loses.
