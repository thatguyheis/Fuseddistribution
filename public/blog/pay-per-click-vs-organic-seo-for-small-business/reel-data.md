## segments
- type: overlay
  text: SMALL BUSINESS OWNER STRUGGLING
  narration: Are you a small business owner struggling to get noticed online? The digital market is crowded, and figuring out the best way to reach your local customers can feel overwhelming. Should you invest in Pay Per Click (PPC) advertising, or focus on building your online presence through Organic Search Engine Optimization (SEO)?
- type: overlay
  text: PAY CLICK PPC ADVERTISING
  narration: Pay Per Click (PPC) advertising, like Google Ads, offers an immediate injection of visibility. When someone searches for a service you offer - let’s say “plumber near me” - your ad appears at the top of the search results. You only pay when someone clicks on your ad. This is fantastic for generating leads quickly. If you need a new customer today, PPC is a powerful tool.
- type: overlay
  text: SIGNIFICANT INVESTMENT DISAPPEARS PAUSE
  narration: That’s a significant investment that disappears when you pause the campaign. Organic SEO, on the other hand, is a slower, more strategic approach. It’s about optimizing your website and online content to rank higher in search results organically - meaning without paying for each click. Think of it as building a solid foundation for your business’s online presence.
- type: stat
  text: $500 TO $1000 COST BUSINESS MONTH PPC
  narration: While there are costs associated with website design, content creation, and potentially SEO tools, the ongoing cost is significantly lower. Consider this: a small business might spend $500-$1000 per month on PPC, while a comparable investment in SEO could range from $300-$1500 per month, depending on the scope of work. The key difference is that with PPC, you’re paying for access, while with SEO, you’re paying for earned visibility.
- type: overlay
  text: MAKE SURE GBP COMPLETELY
  narration: Make sure your GBP is completely filled out with accurate information - your business name, address, phone number, website, hours of operation, and a detailed description of your services. Beyond your GBP, focus on creating high-quality content that addresses the needs of your local audience.
- type: overlay
  text: EXAMPLE RUN PPC CAMPAIGN
  narration: For example, if you run a PPC campaign targeting “dog grooming services city name” and see a high click-through rate for “mobile dog grooming,” you know that’s a keyword worth investing in for your SEO efforts. Don’t get caught in the trap of chasing vanity metrics like website views. Focus on keywords that mean someone is actively looking for your services and is ready to call you.
- type: overlay
  text: USE DATA REFINE SEO
  narration: Then, use that data to refine your SEO strategy. For example, if you discover that “emergency plumbing repair” is a high-converting keyword in your PPC campaigns, you can create a dedicated page on your website that targets that keyword and optimize it for SEO. Don’t waste time chasing traffic numbers that don’t translate into calls.
- type: overlay
  text: ADD PHOTOS VIDEOS POSTS
  narration: Add photos, videos, and posts to keep your profile fresh and engaging. Target Local Keywords: Research the keywords your potential customers are using to find your services. Include these keywords in your website content, GBP description, and PPC ads. Get Reviews: Positive reviews on Google and other platforms can significantly boost your local rankings. Encourage your satisfied customers to leave reviews.
