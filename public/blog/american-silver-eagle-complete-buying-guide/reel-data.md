  narration: Each American Silver Eagle contains exactly 1 troy ounce of .999 fine silver. The coin weighs slightly more because a small copper alloy core adds durability. The troy ounce is 31.1 grams, not the everyday 28.35 grams. That difference matters when you calculate value per gram.

- text: SINCE 1986
  explanation: The US Mint has produced Silver Eagles continuously every year since November 1986 under the Liberty Coin Act
  graphic_type: none

  narration: The national Mint has struck Silver Eagles continuously since 1986. That is 40 years of unbroken production. In 2015, the Mint sold a record 47 million coins in a single year. Silver Eagles remain the best-selling silver bullion coins in the world by annual volume.

- text: 3 TYPES TO KNOW
  explanation: Bullion for investors, Proof for collectors, Burnished for limited editions — each carries a different premium and resale market
  graphic_type: none
  graphic:
    value: 3
    label: Coin Formats
    remainder_label: ""
  narration: The national Mint produces Silver Eagles in three distinct formats. Bullion Eagles are for investors, distributed through authorized dealers. Proof Eagles are collector coins with mirror-finish surfaces, sold directly by the Mint. Burnished Eagles are limited-mintage collectibles. For pure silver value, bullion is what most buyers choose.

- text: $4-8 OVER SPOT
  explanation: Bullion Silver Eagles typically carry a $4-8 premium per coin over the silver spot price — roughly 12 to 25 percent at current levels
  graphic_type: percent_fill
  graphic:
    value: 20
    label: Typical Bullion Premium
    remainder_label: Spot Value
  narration: Bullion Silver Eagles typically cost 4 to 8 dollars more than the spot price per coin. At a spot price of 32 dollars per ounce, that is roughly 12 to 25 percent over spot. A generic silver round runs about 7 percent over spot. The Eagle premium reflects the Mint guarantee and worldwide recognition.

- text: $30+ OVER SPOT
  explanation: Proof Silver Eagles carry $30-50+ premiums over spot — collector value, not silver content — and don't recover at resale if prices stay flat
  graphic_type: percent_fill
  graphic:
    value: 80
    label: Proof Premium Over Spot
    remainder_label: Spot Value
  narration: Proof Silver Eagles carry dramatically higher premiums, often 30 to 50 dollars or more above spot. The Mint sells these directly with original packaging and a certificate of authenticity. If you buy for silver content only, proof premiums do not recover at resale. Proof coins are for collectors, not stackers.
