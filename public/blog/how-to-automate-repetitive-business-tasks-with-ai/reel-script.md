## OVERLAY: PRACTICE MEANS CONNECTING SOFTWARE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: In practice, it means connecting software to a repeatable task so a defined input produces a draft, classification, or action for review. Examples include scheduling posts, responding to common questions, or generating a first draft of website content. It’s not about robots taking over your job. AI tools can analyze supplied information and follow defined instructions, but they can also misunderstand context or repeat an error at scale.
