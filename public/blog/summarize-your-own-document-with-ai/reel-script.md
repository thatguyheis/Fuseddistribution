format: long-form
engagement-policy: 1

---
## HOOK
**Duration:** 4s
Narration: Can AI summarize a document accurately?

---
## OVERLAY: Choose a safe file
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Start with a public guide or sanitized procedure you are allowed to share. Remove customer, payment, health, and employee details first. Check the tool’s current file types, size limits, and workspace rules before attaching anything. A scanned or poorly formatted document may be read incompletely, so confirm the important pages are legible.

---
## OVERLAY: Name the reader
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: A new employee needs different details than an owner preparing for a meeting. Say who will use the summary and what they need to understand or do. This gives the model a purpose for deciding what belongs in the short version. Without it, you may get a generic recap that hides the useful steps.

---
## OVERLAY: Set the purpose
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Ask for the information that matters, such as deadlines, approvals, decisions, or open questions. Choose a short list or a table and name its columns. Keep the request tied to the original file. Do not ask for advice or facts that are not in the document unless you request a separate, sourced research task.

---
## OVERLAY: Keep source references
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Ask the model to name the section heading for each key point and list unclear or conflicting instructions separately. Treat references as a map for checking, not proof that the summary is right. If it cannot find a location, mark the point as unverified. Open the original section before sharing the summary.

---
## OVERLAY: Compare key details
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Check names, dates, quantities, conditions, and exceptions against the source. Confirm that warnings and stopping rules remain. Keep the original as the source of truth, label the summary as a draft, and record which version it describes. Have a responsible person check consequential policy details before anyone acts on them.
