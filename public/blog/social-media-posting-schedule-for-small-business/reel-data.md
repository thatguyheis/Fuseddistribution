## segments
- type: overlay
 text: DEFINE THE JOB
 narration: Decide what social activity should support. It might help people understand a service, ask a question, book an appointment, or return for another purchase. Pick one primary job for the month, then write who you help, with what problem, and in which situation.
- type: overlay
 text: MATCH THE SCHEDULE TO CAPACITY
 narration: Count the hours you can protect after delivery, sales, and administration. At minimum, keep one helpful post and one response block. At normal capacity, add a second post and a reuse task. Add more only when service work is covered.
- type: overlay
 text: USE REPEATABLE FORMATS
 narration: Choose formats from work you already do. A customer question can become a short answer. A project step can become a photo explanation. A frequent concern can become a demonstration. Give every format one job: teach, explain, answer, or invite a conversation.
- type: overlay
 text: BATCH A WEEKLY PLAN
 narration: On Monday, choose one problem and outline it. On Tuesday, create one clear post. On Wednesday, answer questions and route issues. On Thursday, adapt the idea for another format when it still fits. On Friday, review the record and choose one adjustment. Keep a buffer for timely work.
- type: overlay
 text: REUSE WITH JUDGMENT
 narration: One useful idea can become a checklist, a short answer, and a reminder. Keep the central advice consistent, but change the opening, example, length, and next step. Remove private details unless you have clear permission, and leave out anything uncertain.
- type: overlay
 text: OWN RESPONSES AND MEASURE LEARNING
 narration: Assign response windows and owners for questions, bookings, complaints, and corrections. Track measures connected to your job, such as qualified inquiries, completed bookings, response time, or repeat questions. Review patterns over a month. A thoughtful question can be useful even when visible reactions are small.
