## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable way to get my local business found on Google search results.

## Best Angle
Stop chasing complex SEO jargon. Here is the exact, no-fluff setup for your Google Business Profile that actually brings in calls.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about keywords or technical SEO. They miss the fundamental, easy-to-execute steps of optimizing the core Google Business Profile—the single most important factor for local visibility. They fail to address the owner's fear that this process is too complicated or time-consuming.

## Key Points
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is your free storefront. If it’s set up wrong, you’re invisible.
*   Claim your business name, address, and phone number immediately. This is the foundation of local search.
*   Use high-quality photos of your actual shop, office, or work in progress. Show, don't just tell.
*   Respond to every review—good or bad—in a professional, quick way. This shows Google you care.
*   Start collecting reviews consistently. Focus on asking happy customers for feedback directly.
