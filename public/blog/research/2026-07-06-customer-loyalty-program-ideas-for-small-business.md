## Search Intent
The user wants simple, actionable ideas for creating customer loyalty programs that can actually drive repeat business for their local service-based company.

## Best Angle
Focus on the immediate, tangible steps a local business owner can take *today* to build loyalty, bypassing complex software and endless marketing jargon.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on abstract loyalty concepts or expensive CRM setups. They fail to address the reality that for local businesses, loyalty is built on simple, high-touch interactions and making sure Google sees your local reputation.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **The "Just Ask" Rule:** Implement a simple, personalized follow-up sequence (e.g., a quick email or text after a service completion) asking specifically how they are doing, not just selling.
*   **Review Automation:** Set up a simple system to prompt happy customers for a review *at the right time* (e.g., right after a successful appointment) rather than waiting for a general request.
*   **Tiered, Simple Rewards:** Don't overcomplicate it. Offer small, immediate rewards based on frequency (e.g., "5 visits get a free basic add-on," not complex points systems).
*   **Local Testimonials:** Collect specific testimonials tied to local areas or services ("Thanks to [Business Name], I finally fixed my [specific issue]"). These are far more trustworthy than generic praise.
*   **Offline Connection:** Integrate a small, tangible incentive that encourages in-person visits, like a small, branded item or a handwritten note, making the interaction personal.
