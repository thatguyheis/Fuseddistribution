## Key Points
*   **Consistency over Volume:** Posting once a week consistently beats posting five times a month sporadically for local search signals.
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is Primary:** Focus more on optimizing your GBP (posts, photos) than just the blog for immediate local impact.
*   **Service-Specific Content:** Write about the specific problems your target customer has (e.g., "5 Ways to Fix Leaky Roofs in [Your City]").
*   **Review Integration:** Use blog posts to naturally promote requests for reviews, not just ask for them in a generic way.
*   **Listen to Your Numbers:** If a post doesn't drive inquiries or profile views, cut it. Don't chase volume for volume's sake.
