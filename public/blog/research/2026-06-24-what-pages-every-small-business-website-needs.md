## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable checklist of the absolute essentials to make sure they show up when local customers search for their services.

## Best Angle
Stop building pretty websites. Start fixing the one thing that actually brings customers to your door: your Google Business Profile and reviews.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on the technical SEO jargon or big-picture website architecture. They miss the simple, immediate, and tangible steps—like optimizing your profile and managing local reviews—that deliver actual phone calls today.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup:** You need to claim and fully optimize your profile *this week*. This is free and the first place customers find you.
*   **Review System Overhaul:** Focus on getting genuine, local reviews. Ask happy customers for them, and respond to every single one. This is a direct ranking signal.
*   **Local Keyword Mapping:** Stop trying to rank for "technology solutions." Start optimizing for "plumber downtown [City Name]" or "salon near me."
*   **Website Functionality Check:** Your site needs one clear, easy-to-find phone number and a clear "Call Now" button on every page. If it’s hard to call, you lose the lead.
*   **Review Strategy:** Create a simple, low-effort system for collecting 3-star reviews every month. Consistency beats big, sporadic efforts.
