## Search Intent
They want a simple, actionable checklist to get their local business actually showing up on Google Maps and search results.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on complicated marketing jargon and start focusing on the one thing that actually brings customers in: your Google Business Profile.

## Competitor Gap
Most guides focus on broad SEO theory or complex ad setups. They miss the simple, immediate actions that local businesses need to fix their visibility *right now*. They don't show the exact steps to set up the profile correctly the first time.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Profile Accuracy is King:** Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is more important than your website for local calls. Make sure your hours, services, and address are 100% accurate.
*   **Review Strategy:** Don't wait for customers. Implement a simple, direct ask system to get 5-star reviews quickly. Google rewards active businesses.
*   **Local Keywords Matter:** Focus your profile description and service areas on what customers actually search for (e.g., "Plumber in [Your City]" not just "Plumbing").
*   **Photo Proofing:** Use photos of your actual work, your team, and your storefront. Show, don't just tell, what you do.
*   **Consistency Over Volume:** Showing up consistently across Google Maps and search is better than posting randomly on social media.
