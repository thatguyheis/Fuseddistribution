**Key Points (5 bullets):**
*   **Point 1: Check for Hallucinations:** Look for claims in the summary that don't directly map to specific sentences or data points in the original document.
*   **Point 2: Cross-Reference Key Metrics:** Identify the main data points in the summary and manually check the original source for the exact figures or names.
*   **Point 3: Verify Contextual Integrity:** Ensure the summary doesn't change the *meaning* of a section—only the facts should be extracted, not reinterpreted.
*   **Point 4: Traceability Audit:** For critical information, trace the summary sentence back to the precise paragraph or section in the source document.
*   **Point 5: Tone and Bias Check:** Does the AI summary adopt a tone that differs significantly from the source material? Inconsistent tone often signals a weak summary.
