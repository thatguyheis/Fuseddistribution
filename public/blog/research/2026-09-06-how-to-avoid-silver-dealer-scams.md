## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Demand a Full Price Breakdown:** Insist on a clear, itemized quote showing the cost of the metal, any taxes, and any service fees.
*   **Scrutinize Premium Charges:** Understand exactly what a "premium" is. Ask for the specific justification for that charge.
*   **Verify Product Purity:** Know the difference between the metal content and any added alloys or plating.
*   **Check Dealer History:** Look for independent reviews or established, long-standing local presence rather than new, high-pressure sales tactics.
*   **Understand Storage Protocols:** Ask specific questions about how the dealer handles documentation and the security of the physical product after the sale.
