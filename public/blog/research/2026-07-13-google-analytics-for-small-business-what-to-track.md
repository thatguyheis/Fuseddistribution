## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on "Calls from Website":** Don't just track page views. Set up conversion events specifically for phone number clicks or form submissions directly linked to local service requests. [VERIFY]
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) Data Integration:** Track how many people find you via GBP search and how many of those clicks eventually lead to your website conversion. [VERIFY]
*   **Geographic Performance Split:** Segment your data to see if traffic coming from local search terms (e.g., "plumber near me") is converting better than general search traffic.
*   **Review Velocity Tracking:** Monitor the traffic patterns around review requests. High traffic spikes correlated with review activity suggest a successful local visibility push. [VERIFY]
*   **Time-to-Conversion for Local Users:** Measure how long it takes a user who finds you via local search to actually contact you. Short times mean your landing page is effective.
