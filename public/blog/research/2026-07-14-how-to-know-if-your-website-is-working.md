## Content Brief

**Keyword/Title Candidate:** How to know if your website is working for your local business

**Search Intent**
Local business owners want a simple, actionable checklist to verify if their current website efforts are actually driving local customers (calls, visits, reviews).

**Best Angle**
Stop looking at fancy analytics. Check these 5 simple things right now to see if your website is actually making you money.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus on technical SEO jargon or broad metrics like "traffic." They fail to address the local business owner's core fear: "Is this website actually getting me a phone call or a customer showing up to the shop?" They don't provide a direct, non-technical diagnostic checklist.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Call Tracking Check:** Implement a simple call tracking method. If you can't track a call directly from the website, you can't know if it's working. [VERIFY]
*   **Google Business Profile Audit:** Check your GBP. Is it complete? Are the photos current? Is it getting new interactions (not just passive views)? Link to `google-business-profile-setup`.
*   **Local Search Test:** Search for your exact service + city. Are the first 3 results your business? If not, your local visibility is the problem. Link to `local-seo-near-me`.
*   **Review Source Analysis:** Look at where reviews come from. If reviews only come from direct social media mentions and not from Google, your search visibility strategy is weak. Link to `getting-google-reviews`.
*   **Form Friction Test:** Test your contact form. Does it require too many fields? If so, you are losing leads before they even reach you. Link to `what-a-website-does-for-your-business`.
