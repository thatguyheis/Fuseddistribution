## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable plan to get their business showing up on Google Maps and search results for local customers.

## Best Angle
Stop worrying about complex SEO jargon. Here is the exact, no-fluff checklist you need to get found by the people already looking for your service in your area.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about *what* SEO is. They fail to show the *exact, low-effort steps* a busy contractor or salon owner needs to take *today* to fix their visibility problem. They focus on big, expensive strategies instead of immediate, local fixes.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront.** Set it up correctly—add photos, accurate hours, and use your specific service area code.
*   **Reviews are your new website.** Create a simple, direct request for reviews immediately after a job is done. Ask for a quick 30-second feedback.
*   **Your website needs to talk about local.** Ensure your main page clearly states *who* you help and *where* you serve, not just what you do generally.
*   **Consistency beats complexity.** Focus on showing up consistently in local searches (like "plumber near me") rather than trying to rank for every single keyword.
*   **Don't ignore the basics.** Make sure your phone number, address, and service area are identical across every single platform.
