## Search Intent
They want a simple, actionable step-by-step plan to make sure their website and Google profile actually generate phone calls from local customers.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on complicated jargon. This guide shows you the three non-negotiable steps to get your local business found on Google and start getting calls today.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on general SEO theory or complicated technical setups. They fail to address the specific, practical reality of setting up Google Business Profile and optimizing local content for service businesses.

The gap is practical application. We focus on the immediate, tangible steps a service business owner needs to take, bypassing the confusing, abstract agency promises.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile is the most important tool for local calls. If it’s set up wrong, no website can fix it.
*   Focus on local reviews. Getting 5-star reviews in your city is more powerful than a fancy website design.
*   Use specific local keywords in your website titles and descriptions (e.g., "Plumber in Austin TX" not just "Plumber").
*   Your website needs to clearly tell Google exactly what service you offer and where you serve it.
*   Don't chase vanity metrics like "impressions." Focus only on optimizing for actual phone calls.
