## Content Brief

**Keyword/Title Candidate:** How to get local business found on Google

**Search Intent:** The user wants a straightforward, actionable plan to improve their local visibility on Google search results so potential customers can find their service.

**Best Angle:** Stop guessing what Google wants. This is the exact, no-fluff setup you need to show up when people search for your service right now.

**Competitor Gap:** Most articles focus on vague SEO theory or complex technical jargon. They fail to provide a simple, step-by-step checklist for setting up the essential Google tools that actually drive local calls.

**Key Points (5 bullets):**
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free. Most businesses set it up wrong, which hides you from local searches.
*   Focus on the GBP first. Claim your correct service area and use the right categories so you appear in the first few map results.
*   Reviews are your local ranking fuel. Set up a simple, repeatable way to ask happy customers for feedback.
*   Optimize your website to clearly state *what* you do *where* you do it. This helps Google connect your site to your physical location.
*   Consistency beats complexity. Small, regular updates to your profile and site are more effective than one big, complicated project.
