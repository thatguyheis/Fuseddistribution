## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Verify Field Association:** Confirm that every input field (Name, Email, Message) has a corresponding, clearly visible label directly above or next to it.
*   **Test Submission Flow:** Submit a test message using all fields to confirm the data is being sent to the correct email address.
*   **Check for Typos:** A simple typo in a label (e.g., "Email Address" vs. "Email") can confuse users and cause them to abandon the form.
*   **Ensure Consistency:** Make sure the label style (font size, color) is consistent across all forms on your site so users know what to expect.
*   **Map to Your Inbox:** Confirm that the email address the form is sending to is the correct inbox you use for follow-up.

## H2 Structure
### Why Checking Contact Form Labels Matters for Your Business
*   If your form sends leads to the wrong place, you stop getting calls.
*   Labels are the first thing a customer sees; they dictate if they even fill out the form.
*   Mismatched labels create confusion and cause people to leave before submitting.

### The Quick Checklist: How to Check Contact Form Labels on Your Website
*   **Step 1: Open Your Website:** Navigate to the specific contact page where the form resides.
*   **Step 2: Hover and Inspect:** Hover your mouse over every field. The label should pop up or appear underneath the input box.
*   **Step 3: Cross-Reference:** Check if the label accurately describes the field (e.g., is it labeled "Phone Number" instead of just "Number"?).
*   **Step 4: Test the Submission:** Fill out the form completely and check the email you receive to confirm the data landed in the right spot.

### Common Label Mistakes That Cause Lost Leads
*   **The Missing Label:** Having an input box with no label at all is a huge turn-off for customers.
*   **The Vague Label:** Using generic terms that don't match your business language confuses potential clients.
*   **The Misrouted Label:** Labeling the field for "Phone" but sending the data to your general info inbox.

### Fixing Label Mismatches for Better Lead Flow
*   **Standardize Your Language:** Use clear, simple terms that your local customers understand instantly.
*   **Match the Action:** Ensure the label tells the user exactly what to do (e.g., "Enter your service request" instead of just "Message").
*   **Review Your Setup:** If you use a platform (like Mailchimp or a specific website builder), check its documentation for label placement rules.

### Next Steps After Checking Your Contact Form Labels
*   Once you fix the labels, re-test the entire process with a few colleagues.
*   Monitor your lead flow for the next week to see if submissions are cleaner.
*   Use this small win to build confidence in your website visitors.
