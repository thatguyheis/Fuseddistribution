## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Check the Premium Breakdown:** Demand a clear, itemized breakdown of all costs. Do not accept a single, vague percentage.
*   **Question the Markup:** Ask precisely how the final price compares to the spot price at the time of purchase.
*   **Verify Credentials:** Look for verifiable business registrations or industry certifications. Ask for proof of operation.
*   **Avoid Pressure Tactics:** If a dealer pushes immediate decisions, walk away. A legitimate process takes time.
*   **Compare Apples to Apples:** Compare the total landed cost against established market benchmarks, not just the sticker price.
