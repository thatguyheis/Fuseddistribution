**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Focus on Specificity:** Don't just ask for "customer questions." Focus on scenarios tied directly to your service (e.g., "A contractor asking about scheduling a repair," "A salon client complaining about a specific service").
*   **Capture the Pain Points:** Your test set must include examples of common frustrations your customers have. This teaches the AI to empathize and solve problems, not just recite answers.
*   **Create Negative Examples:** Include instances where customers are confused, angry, or ask off-topic questions. This trains the AI on what *not* to say.
*   **Use Real Language:** Draft your scenarios using the actual slang or terminology your customers use. This makes the resulting AI drafts sound authentic and local, not corporate.
*   **Iterate Fast:** Test the drafts against your test set, get feedback, and immediately update the set. This feedback loop is how you refine the AI until it sounds like your business.
