## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Check the Premium Breakdown:** Demand a clear, itemized breakdown of all associated fees. Do not accept a single lump sum.
*   **Verify Dealer Credentials:** Research the dealer's history and licensing. Look for independent reviews, not just testimonials on their website.
*   **Compare Actual Costs:** Compare the final price per ounce against established market benchmarks, such as the current silver price of $66.96/oz as of 2026-09-04.
*   **Understand Transaction Security:** Ask about the security protocols for transferring ownership and securing your physical asset.
*   **Know Your Storage Plan:** Before buying, confirm the dealer or a third party can advise on secure, appropriate storage methods.
