**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Check for Pressure Tactics:** If a dealer pushes immediate, high-pressure sales, pause. Legitimate sellers do not rush the decision.
*   **Demand a Full Breakdown:** Ask for a clear, itemized quote showing the cost of the metal, premiums, and any fees. No vague totals.
*   **Verify Credentials:** Look for verifiable business registration and established history. Ask for references outside of the immediate sales pitch.
*   **Understand Premium Structure:** Know exactly what the premium covers. Compare their structure against industry norms for better context.
*   **Check Storage Protocols:** A legitimate dealer should offer clear guidance on secure, long-term storage options.
