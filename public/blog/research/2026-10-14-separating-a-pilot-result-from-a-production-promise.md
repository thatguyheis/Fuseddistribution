## Key Points
*   **Metric Divergence:** Clearly define the difference between metrics that proved *feasibility* in the pilot (e.g., user engagement, initial concept validation) and metrics that prove *sustainability* in production (e.g., throughput capacity, cost-per-unit at volume, supply chain stability).
*   **Capacity Stress Testing:** Move beyond pilot-level load testing. Production readiness requires stress-testing the supply chain, infrastructure, and team capacity at 10x or 100x the pilot volume, not just the pilot volume.
*   **Cost Structure Re-evaluation:** Pilot costs are often skewed by learning curve effects. Production promises must account for fixed overhead, economies of scale, and the true marginal cost of goods sold (COGS) under high-volume scenarios.
*   **Failure Mode Analysis (FMEA) for Scale:** Systematically map potential failure points identified in the pilot (e.g., equipment failure, quality control bottlenecks) and develop specific, pre-production mitigation plans, rather than treating them as unknowns.
*   **Decision Framework:** Implement a decision matrix that forces a comparison: "Does the pilot's success metric translate directly into a predictable, repeatable, and cost-effective production mechanism, or is it merely a promising trend?"
