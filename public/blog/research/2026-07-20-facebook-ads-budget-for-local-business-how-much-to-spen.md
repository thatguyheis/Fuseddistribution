## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Start Small and Local:** Begin with a small, consistent weekly budget focused purely on local service areas to test what specific neighborhoods respond.
*   **Focus on Conversion, Not Clicks:** Your primary goal isn't getting clicks; it's getting a phone call or a request for a quote. Measure the cost per lead, not the cost per impression.
*   **Test Service Packages:** Instead of one big ad, run separate ads targeting different services (e.g., plumbing vs. HVAC) to see which service generates the best return on your test spend.
*   **Leverage the Profile:** Ensure your Facebook Business Page and Google Business Profile are perfectly synced, as this boosts ad trust and relevance significantly.
*   **Don't Chase Vanity:** Ignore metrics like "reach" or "impressions." Focus solely on metrics that drive revenue: direct calls, quote requests, and booked appointments.
