# Research Brief: how to map old website urls before a redesign
Date: 2026-09-24 | Brand: tech | Author: Codex recovery

## Search Intent
Help a business owner prepare an old-to-new URL map for a website redesign.

## Best Angle
A practical handover document connects each old address to an intentional content decision and a separately recorded test result.

## Competitor Gap
Avoid assuming a menu or sitemap is a complete inventory. Keep business approval separate from proof that redirects work.

## Topic Contract
The title, URL mapping task, page inventory, destination decisions and launch checks must address redesign preparation. Required terms: old URLs, new URLs, redirects, mapping, testing. Avoid generic SEO rankings advice, pricing promises and unrelated automation.

## Key Points
- Inventory public addresses from multiple sources and preserve the original list.
- Choose relevant destinations; do not send unrelated removed pages to the homepage.
- Record planned decisions separately from tested outcomes.
- Check final content and customer actions as well as HTTP responses.
- Update internal links and monitor after launch without ranking guarantees.

## H2 Structure
Collect the old website URLs from several places
Choose a relevant destination for each old URL
Build a URL map your developer can implement
Test the old address and the final page
Update internal links and monitor after launch

## SEO Title
Map old website URLs before a redesign
