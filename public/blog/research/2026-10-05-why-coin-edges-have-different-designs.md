## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Die Variation:** Each coin uses a unique die. This die dictates the exact shape of the edge.
*   **Striking Force:** The force applied during striking affects how the metal flows into the edge.
*   **Edge Definition:** The edge is a physical record of the minting machine's specific settings.
*   **Coin Type Influence:** Whether it is a round coin or a bar significantly changes the edge profile.
*   **Material Interaction:** The silver itself interacts differently with the tooling used in each production run.
