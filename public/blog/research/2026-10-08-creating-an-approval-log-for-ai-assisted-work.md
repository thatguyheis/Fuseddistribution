## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Define Clear Approval Gates:** Establish mandatory checkpoints *before* and *after* AI generation where human review and sign-off are required, explicitly detailing what the human is verifying (e.g., accuracy, tone, compliance).
*   **Capture the "Input-to-Output" Chain:** The log must meticulously record the initial prompt, the AI model used, the specific parameters set, and the final human edits, providing full traceability.
*   **Establish Risk Triage Criteria:** Define specific criteria within the log to flag work requiring escalated review (e.g., high-stakes content, financial data summarization) to ensure appropriate oversight.
*   **Standardize Log Template:** Develop a standardized template that captures essential metadata (date, task objective, AI source, human revision notes, final sign-off) to ensure consistency across all AI-assisted projects.
*   **Audit Trail for Compliance:** Use the completed log as the primary audit trail to demonstrate due diligence and control when external stakeholders or internal auditors question the accuracy or origin of the final work product.
