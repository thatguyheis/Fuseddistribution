## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Intent, Not Just Clicks:** Retargeting works best when you show an ad to people who already looked at your service page or visited your booking area, not just random strangers.
*   **Start with the Basics (The Profile First):** Before running ads, make sure your Google Business Profile is set up perfectly. Retargeting ads boost the traffic, but a good profile ensures the landing spot is ready to convert.
*   **Use Simple Visuals:** For local businesses, retargeting ads should be short, direct reminders (e.g., "Still thinking about [Service]? Call now for a free quote."). Avoid complicated video or heavy graphics.
*   **Set a Clear Follow-Up Action:** The retargeting ad must push toward a low-friction next step, like a direct phone call or a specific offer, not just a generic website visit.
*   **Test Small Batches:** Don't launch a massive retargeting campaign all at once. Start by retargeting people who visited your pricing page or service menu for a week to see what kind of response you get.
