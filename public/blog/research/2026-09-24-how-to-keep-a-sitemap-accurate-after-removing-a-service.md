## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Identify the Link:** Find every instance of the removed service URL within your existing sitemap file.
*   **Remove the Entry:** Delete that specific URL entry from the XML sitemap file.
*   **Update the Index:** Regenerate or update your sitemap index file to reflect the change.
*   **Check Internal Links:** Scan your site for any remaining links pointing to the deleted service to ensure they are properly redirected or removed.
*   **Verify in GSC:** Use Google Search Console to confirm that Google has successfully re-indexed the remaining pages.
