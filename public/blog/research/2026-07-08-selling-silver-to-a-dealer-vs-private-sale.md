## Key Points
*   **Dealer Markup:** Dealers add a premium to their purchase price. This markup is often unclear upfront. [VERIFY]
*   **Private Sale Control:** You control the entire transaction. You set the terms directly with the buyer.
*   **Transaction Fees:** Dealers charge various fees. These can include administrative costs, insurance, and handling charges.
*   **Purity Verification:** Always verify the silver purity (e.g., 99.99%) before finalizing any dealer agreement.
*   **Trust Assessment:** Assess the dealer's track record. Look for clear documentation regarding their sourcing and quality control.
