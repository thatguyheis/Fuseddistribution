## Search Intent
How do I actually start a customer referral program for my local business so it actually brings in new customers?

## Best Angle
Stop hoping for referrals and start building a simple system that makes getting a new customer easy for your existing happy clients.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on the *idea* of a referral program, not the messy, practical steps a busy local owner needs to take immediately. They miss the crucial link between a referral and a tangible action (like a specific sign-up process).

## Key Points
*   **Define the "Why":** Before building anything, know exactly what action you want the referred person to take (e.g., book a service, sign up for a discount).
*   **Make It Frictionless:** The process for referring must be ridiculously easy. If it takes more than two clicks or a quick phone call, people won't do it.
*   **Tie It to Value:** Reward both the referrer and the referred. This isn't just a thank you; it's a system that makes the act of sharing feel like a win for both parties.
*   **Use Your Existing Base:** Start by asking your current best customers. They are your warmest leads and the best source for your first set of referrals.
*   **Track Everything Simply:** You don't need a complicated dashboard. Track who referred whom, and how many new calls or bookings came in from that source.
