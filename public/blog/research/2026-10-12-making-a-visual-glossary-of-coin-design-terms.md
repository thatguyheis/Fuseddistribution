## Content Brief: Making a Visual Glossary of Coin Design Terms

**Search Intent**
The user wants a clear, easy-to-understand visual guide to define the technical terms used when looking at silver coins.

**Best Angle**
Use clear, direct comparisons to demystify complex numismatic jargon. Show *why* a term matters for a beginner buyer.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles list terms without visual context. They fail to connect the technical term directly to the physical characteristics the buyer sees. They lack a simple, actionable reference tool.
