## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus only on historical price charts or broad economic predictions. They fail to address the specific, practical pain point of understanding the markup and the difference between buying coins versus bars.

The gap is a direct, actionable breakdown of the cost structure, linking premiums directly to the final price you pay, and addressing the skepticism about dealer legitimacy head-on.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Silver premiums are the difference between the spot price and the retail price. This markup is applied by dealers and services.
*   Buying silver coins involves additional premiums related to minting, grading, and storage.
*   Silver bars typically have lower premiums than coins because they bypass some of the complexity of numismatic grading.
