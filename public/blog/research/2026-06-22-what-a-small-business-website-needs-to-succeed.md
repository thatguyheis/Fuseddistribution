## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable plan to get my local business found on Google and start getting calls.

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on fancy websites. Start focusing on the one thing Google actually uses to find you: your Google Business Profile.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about website design or complex SEO tactics. They miss the simple, immediate, and free steps that fix the #1 problem: being invisible on Google Maps and search. They promise big results without showing the exact setup someone can do today.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free. If it's set up wrong, you are invisible to local searches. [VERIFY]
*   Google prioritizes your GBP listing over your website for local results. Optimize your profile completely first.
*   Reviews are the engine. Focus on making it easy for customers to leave a review—this is a direct path to more local calls.
*   Your website's job isn't to rank; it's to support the profile. Make sure your website clearly tells people *what* you do and *where* you are.
*   Forget complicated reports. Focus only on the three things that drive calls: accurate location, consistent hours, and collecting reviews.
