## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable guide on how to use Instagram Stories specifically to help their local business get found or get customers.

## Best Angle
Show the direct, no-fluff steps to use Instagram Stories to solve the immediate problem of local visibility, focusing only on what actually drives calls and visits.

## Competitor Gap
Most guides focus on vanity metrics like follower counts or complex editing tricks. They miss the core action: using Stories as a direct, low-effort way to show *proof* of local service and encourage immediate contact. They don't connect the story directly to Google visibility.

## Key Points
*   **The Story is a Quick Proof Point:** Use Stories to show the *behind-the-scenes* of your actual work (e.g., a quick shot of a finished repair, a friendly face from your team).
*   **Geo-Tag Everything:** Always tag your physical location in the Story. This tells the algorithm *who* in your area you are serving.
*   **Use the Question Sticker:** Ask simple, local questions ("What's your biggest plumbing headache this week?") to start a conversation, not just post updates.
*   **Link Directly to Booking/Call:** Don't make people search for your website. Use the Link Sticker to go straight to your phone number or a specific service booking page.
*   **Consistency Over Perfection:** Post 3-4 times a week showing real work, not highly polished ads. Authenticity beats perfection when you’re trying to get local attention.
