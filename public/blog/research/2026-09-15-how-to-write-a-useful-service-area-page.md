## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Define Boundaries:** Clearly state the specific towns, zip codes, or radius you serve. Don't just say "the whole state."
*   **Service Description Focus:** Describe *what* you fix or provide, not just *where* you are. Use language a local customer would search for.
*   **Keyword Integration:** Naturally weave in terms like "plumber in [City Name]" or "salon near me" into the body copy.
*   **Profile Linkage:** Ensure the service area page clearly points back to your optimized Google Business Profile.
*   **Keep it Real:** Write like you are talking to a neighbor, not a robot. Authenticity beats complexity every time.
