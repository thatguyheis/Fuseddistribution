## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable guide on how to get local business found on Google, specifically focusing on the role of reviews and visibility.

## Best Angle
Stop worrying about fancy SEO. Focus on the one thing that actually moves your business: showing up on Google when people search for your service.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on complex SEO tactics or broad website critiques. They miss the simple, direct link between a complete Google Business Profile setup and getting local customers who are ready to hire *today*. They don't address the frustration of "I have a website but no calls."

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront:** It’s free. Most local businesses set it up wrong—they treat it like a static brochure instead of a tool for showing up in local maps. [VERIFY]
*   **Reviews drive local search:** Google prioritizes businesses with recent, relevant reviews. Don't just ask for reviews; make sure you have a system to respond to every single one.
*   **The "Service + City" formula is key:** People search for "plumber in [City]" or "salon near me." Your profile needs to match that exact language.
*   **Consistency beats intensity:** Showing up for local searches consistently (through your profile, local mentions, and simple local outreach) beats one big, expensive marketing push every time.
*   **Focus on the "Call to Action":** Every piece of your online presence should lead directly to a phone call or a clear direction, not just a generic "Contact Us" form.
