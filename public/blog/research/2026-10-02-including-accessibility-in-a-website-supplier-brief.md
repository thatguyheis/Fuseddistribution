**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Focus on Keyboard Access:** Demand that the supplier proves they can navigate your entire site using only the keyboard (no mouse required). This is the #1 requirement for many users.
*   **Check for Image Descriptions:** Require suppliers to include proper alternative text for every meaningful image. This helps screen readers understand what the visual content is.
*   **Color Contrast is Crucial:** Specify minimum contrast ratios for text and interactive elements. This is vital for users with visual impairments.
*   **Simple Navigation Structure:** Ensure the site map and navigation are logical and consistent. Avoid confusing menus that break accessibility for users relying on screen readers.
*   **Accessibility as a Business Asset:** Frame it not as a compliance burden, but as expanding your potential customer base. Ask suppliers how accessibility can actually improve lead capture.
