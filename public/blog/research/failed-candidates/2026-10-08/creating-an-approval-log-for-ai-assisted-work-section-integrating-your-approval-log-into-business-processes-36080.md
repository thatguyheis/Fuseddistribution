Integrating Your Approval Log into Business Processes

You need a system to track AI-assisted work. You must define clear approval gates. Set checkpoints before and after AI generation. Tell your team exactly what the human needs to check. For example, they verify accuracy, tone, or compliance. This prevents rushed output.

Capture the entire chain of work. Your log must record the initial prompt you gave the AI. Note which model you used. Record the specific settings you chose. Also, document every human edit you make. This gives you a full history of the output. Traceability is key here.

Set rules for when review is mandatory. Create criteria for flagging high-risk items. If a task involves sensitive data or major decisions, the log must show escalation. This ensures the right people review the most important work.

Use a fixed template for your log. This template needs to hold all the basic details. Include the date, the task goal, the AI source, your notes on revisions, and the final sign-off. This keeps everything consistent.

The log becomes your proof. It serves as your audit trail. When someone asks about the work's origin or accuracy, you show the log. This demonstrates that you followed proper steps. It proves you exercised necessary control over the final product.
