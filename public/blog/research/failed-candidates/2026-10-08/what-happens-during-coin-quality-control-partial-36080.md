# what happens during coin quality control

What happens during coin quality control? You'll find that coin quality control checks for various flaws. This process ensures that each coin meets established standards for purity and weight. We look at the specifics of what happens during coin quality control to understand the final product you receive.

## The Foundation of Silver Purity Standards

The Foundation of Silver Purity Standards

You want to know what happens during coin quality control. It starts with assay testing. Labs measure silver content precisely. They use standardized methods to confirm the coin's fineness. This step confirms the coin meets the required purity levels. This is a core part of ensuring quality.

Next, they check the die and the striking process. You'll see checks on the mint dies. They verify the design and the weight. This ensures the coin matches the official specifications exactly. A correct strike is vital for authenticity.

Metal composition analysis follows. Scientists use spectroscopic or chemical tests. These checks verify the exact alloy composition. They confirm the silver meets all mandated purity levels. This ensures the metal is exactly what it should be.

Handling and storage protocols keep things secure. Strict procedures apply during manufacturing and storage. These protocols stop contamination or tampering. They protect the quality control standards you trust.

Authentication trails build trust. We use tracking systems for documentation. This creates a verifiable chain of custody. This trail shows the coin's journey from raw material to finished product. It confirms its authenticity.
