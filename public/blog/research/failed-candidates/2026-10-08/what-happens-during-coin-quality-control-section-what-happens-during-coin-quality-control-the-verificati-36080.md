What Happens During Coin Quality Control: The Verification Process

We check every coin rigorously. First, assay testing is a big part of the process. Labs measure the silver content precisely. This confirms the coin's fineness. Fineness is a key part of quality control. It tells us exactly how pure the silver is.

Next, we look at the die and the strike. Our team verifies the mint dies. They also check the striking process. This ensures the coin's design and weight match the official specs. A mismatch here means it doesn't pass.

We also analyze the metal composition. Spectroscopic tests check the alloy makeup. This verifies the exact silver purity. It makes sure the coin meets all the required standards for silver.

Handling matters too. We have strict protocols for storage. These procedures stop contamination or tampering. This protects the quality of the coins we produce.

Finally, we track everything. Documentation keeps a record. This chain of custody shows where the coin came from. It proves the coin's authenticity from raw material to finished product.
