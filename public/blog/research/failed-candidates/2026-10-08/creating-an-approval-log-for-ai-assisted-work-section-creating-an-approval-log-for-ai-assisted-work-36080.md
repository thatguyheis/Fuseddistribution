You need a system for tracking AI-assisted work. This involves creating an approval log to manage quality control. You must set clear approval gates. Decide exactly when a human needs to check the work. This check happens before the AI generates the content and after it's produced. You need to specify what the human checks for. This could be accuracy, the tone, or compliance with company rules.

You must track the entire process. Record the initial prompt you gave the AI. Note which AI model you used. Specify the exact settings you used for the generation. Then, document every human edit you make. This gives you a complete record of how the final product came about. Traceability is key here.

Define when you need senior review. Create rules within your log for flagging high-risk tasks. For example, flag any content involving financial data or sensitive client information for extra scrutiny. This ensures the right people see the most critical outputs.

Develop one standard form for this log. This template must capture all the necessary details. Include the date, the task goal, the AI source, your revision notes, and the final sign-off. This keeps everything consistent across all your AI projects. This completed log becomes your audit trail. It proves you took the necessary steps to control the final work.
