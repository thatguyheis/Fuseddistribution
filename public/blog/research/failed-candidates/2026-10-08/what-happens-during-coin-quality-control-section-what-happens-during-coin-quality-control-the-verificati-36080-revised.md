What Happens During Coin Quality Control

We check every coin carefully. First, we perform assay testing. Labs measure the silver content with precise methods. This confirms the coin's fineness. Fineness tells us exactly how pure the silver is. This is a key part of our quality control.

Next, we check the die and the strike. Our team verifies the mint dies. They also check the striking process. This ensures the coin's design and weight match the official specifications. If there's a mismatch, the coin does not pass.

We also analyze the metal composition. Spectroscopic tests check the alloy makeup. These tests verify the exact silver purity. This ensures the coin meets all the required standards for silver.

Handling matters too. We have strict protocols for storage. These procedures stop contamination or tampering. This protects the quality of the coins we produce.

Finally, we track everything. Documentation keeps a record. This chain of custody shows where the coin came from. It proves the coin's authenticity from raw material to finished product.
