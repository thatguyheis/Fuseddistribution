Define clear approval gates for your AI work. You must set mandatory checkpoints before and after any AI generates content. Clearly state what you need the human reviewer to check. This includes verifying accuracy, checking the tone, and ensuring compliance with your company rules. This step prevents unapproved output from moving forward.

Capture the entire input to output chain. Your log needs to record the initial prompt you gave the AI. Note which AI model you used and the specific settings you chose. Document every human edit you make to the text. This complete record shows exactly how the final product came to be. Full traceability is essential for accountability.

Set up rules for flagging high-risk tasks. You need criteria in your log to mark work for special review. For example, flag summaries of sensitive financial data or content for external communication. This ensures you focus your human oversight where it matters most.

Create a standard log template. Make a uniform form for every AI-assisted project. This template must capture the date, the specific goal of the task, the AI source, your notes on human revisions, and the final sign-off. Consistency makes tracking much easier.

Use this completed log as your main proof. The log becomes your audit trail. It shows you took the necessary steps to control the work. This documentation proves you followed proper procedure when stakeholders ask about the final product's origin or correctness.
