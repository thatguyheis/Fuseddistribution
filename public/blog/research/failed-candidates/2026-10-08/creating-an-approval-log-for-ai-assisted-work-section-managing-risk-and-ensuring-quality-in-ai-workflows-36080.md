You need a system for tracking AI-assisted work. This is about managing risk and keeping quality high. You must set clear approval gates. You need mandatory checks before and after the AI creates something. Tell the human exactly what they check. They verify accuracy, tone, and compliance.

Record the entire chain of work. Your log must capture the initial prompt you gave the AI. Note the specific AI model you chose. Document the parameters you set. Also, record every human edit you make. This gives you a full history of the output.

Set rules for when to escalate. Define criteria for flagging work. For example, flag anything with high stakes or financial data summarization for extra review. This ensures the right people look at the right things.

Create one log template. This template must capture all the necessary details. Include the date, the task goal, the AI source, your notes on human revisions, and the final sign-off. This keeps everything consistent.

Use this completed log for your audit trail. When someone asks about the work's accuracy or where it came from, the log proves you took the necessary steps. It shows you were careful and controlled.
