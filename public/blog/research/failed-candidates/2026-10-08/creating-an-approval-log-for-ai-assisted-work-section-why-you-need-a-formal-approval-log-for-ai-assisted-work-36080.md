You must set clear checkpoints for your AI work. Decide exactly when a human needs to review something. This review happens before the final output and after the AI generates it. You need to state what the human checks. For example, the human verifies the accuracy of the facts, checks the tone of voice, and confirms compliance with your company rules.

Keep track the entire process. Your log must record everything that happened. Note the initial prompt you gave the AI. Record which AI model you used. Specify the exact settings you chose for the AI. Also, document every edit you made by a person. This gives you a clear path from the starting idea to the finished product.

Define when to escalate. Create rules for flagging certain tasks. For instance, flag work involving sensitive financial data or high stakes content. These items need extra attention. This ensures you apply the right level of oversight to the most critical outputs.

Use a consistent format for your log. Build a template that captures all the necessary details. Include the date, the goal of the task, the AI source, your human notes, and the final approval signature. This consistency makes tracking much easier.

The log serves as your proof of work. When someone questions the accuracy of a document, you show the log. It proves you took the necessary steps. It shows you exercised due diligence in creating the final result.
