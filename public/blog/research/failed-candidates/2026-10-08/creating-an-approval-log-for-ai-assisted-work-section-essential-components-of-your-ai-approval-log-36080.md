Essential Components of Your AI Approval Log

You need clear approval gates. Set mandatory checkpoints for human review. Decide exactly what the person reviewing must check. Verify accuracy, check the tone, or confirm compliance with your standards. This prevents errors from passing through unchecked.

You must track the entire work journey. Record the initial prompt you gave the AI. Note which AI model you used and the exact settings you selected. Capture every human edit you make afterward. This creates a full history of the content's creation. Traceability is key here.

Define when you need extra oversight. Create rules for flagging work for higher-level review. For example, flag any content involving sensitive data or major decisions. This ensures the right people look at the most critical tasks. Standardize your log template. Use the same format for every project. Include the date, the goal of the task, the AI source, human notes, and the final sign-off.

This log becomes your proof of process. Use it as your main record. When someone asks about the work's origin or accuracy, the log shows you followed your procedures. It proves you took the necessary steps to control the output.
