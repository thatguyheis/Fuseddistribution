Making Sure Your Website Meets Basic Web Standards

You need a website that everyone can use. This starts with keyboard access. Ask your web supplier if they can navigate your entire site with just the keyboard. This is a top requirement for many people. If they can't do this, your site is locked out for a lot of users.

Next, check the images. Make sure every important picture has alternative text. Screen readers read this text aloud. It tells them what the picture is about. This helps people with visual impairments understand your content. Don't skip this step.

Color matters too. You must check the contrast. Make sure your text stands out clearly against the background. This is especially important for people with low vision. Poor contrast makes your site hard to read.

Your site structure should be simple. The navigation needs to make sense. Avoid confusing menus. If users can’t find what they need easily, they leave. A clear path to your contact info is essential.

Treat accessibility as a way to get more customers. People with disabilities are a large part of the market. Making your site accessible opens up new potential clients for your business. Ask your supplier how better accessibility can actually help you get more calls.
