Silver comes from mining. Mines extract raw silver from the earth. This is the first step. You need to know where the silver is coming from.

Refining takes that raw material. Refiners purify the silver. They separate it into specific products. This purification sets the final quality of the silver. Purity matters for the value you hold.

Production numbers show where the silver is first found. Refining statistics show where the processed silver goes. These figures show the supply chain. They help you see the full picture.

These steps create different costs. Production and refining both add to the final price. Understanding this difference helps you compare silver products. It lets you see the actual cost structure.
