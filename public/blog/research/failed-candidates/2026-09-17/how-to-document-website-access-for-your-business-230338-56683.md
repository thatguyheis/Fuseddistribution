## Setting Up Your Website Link for Google

Setting Up Your Website Link for Google

First, check your homepage. Make sure the page people search for you with your business name is easy to find. Google needs to see that main page to know what you do. If it’s buried, Google won't find you easily.

Next, think about your services. You need to tell Google exactly which pages show up for what. Use sitelinks to point Google straight to your plumbing page or your salon page. Don't just have one big website. Break it down so Google knows exactly what you offer.

Then, double-check your map connection. Your Google Business Profile has to point to the right place on your website. If that link is broken, you're missing out on local searches. Make sure the map location matches the address on your site.

Keep it simple. Don't try to build a complicated structure. Google just wants one clear path to your main business info. Focus on that primary link that Google can read right away.

Use Google Search Console. This tool shows you exactly what Google sees on your site. It points out if you’re missing anything. Use it to fix the simple stuff that stops people from finding you.
