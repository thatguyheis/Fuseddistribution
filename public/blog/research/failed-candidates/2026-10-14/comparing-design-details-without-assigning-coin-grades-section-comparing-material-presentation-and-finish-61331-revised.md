Comparing Material Presentation and Finish

You'll see how silver and gold coins look different. Look at the mint mark. These marks appear different on silver coins than on gold ones. This is a visual clue. They tell you where the coin came from.

Die variation shows up too. See the design of the coin. Slight changes in the die work reveal different production runs. This tells you which mint made that specific piece.

The surface finish is another thing to check. How silver is struck differs from how gold is struck. You can see textural changes just by looking. These differences are visible to the naked eye.

Check the edge and rim. The way the coin's edge is finished reflects the process used for that metal form. This detail points to the specific metal form.

Finally, look at the symbols. Compare how figures look on silver versus gold designs. The mint makes aesthetic choices. These visual elements show their design preferences.
