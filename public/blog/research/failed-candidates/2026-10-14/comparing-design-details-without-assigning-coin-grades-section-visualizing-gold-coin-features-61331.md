Visualizing Gold Coin Features

You can see how silver coins look different from gold coins. The mint mark is a good place to start. See how the mark on a silver coin sits compared to a gold one. They look distinct. This difference shows you something about the mint.

Look at the die work. Small changes in the design show you different production runs. Some coins have slightly different details in the engraving. This variation tells a story about where and when the coin was made. You can spot these subtle shifts in the artwork.

The surface finish is another thing to check. Silver has a certain way of being struck. Gold has a different texture. You can often feel the difference when you look closely at how the metal has been formed. This striking technique leaves visible marks on the coin.

Check the edge and rim. The way the coin is finished on the edge varies. This finish shows the specific process used for that metal. A silver edge will look distinct from a gold edge.

Symbolic designs also show differences. See how the figures or symbols look on silver compared to gold. The mints make choices about how these elements appear. These visual choices reflect the metal they are working with.
