Defining the Pilot Success Metrics vs. Production Viability

You need to clearly separate what worked in the pilot from what will work in full production. Pilot success metrics prove feasibility. You might see high user engagement or initial concept validation. These show the idea is sound. These metrics tell you if people will use it. They do not prove you can make it reliably at scale.

Production viability demands a different view. You must test capacity stress. Don't just test the pilot volume. You need to test your supply chain and infrastructure at ten or one hundred times that volume. This checks if your systems can handle real demand without breaking. You need to see if your team can actually maintain quality when things get busy.

Also, re-evaluate your cost structure. Pilot costs often look different. They might be skewed by learning curves. Production promises must factor in fixed overhead and the true cost per unit at high volume. You need to see the actual marginal cost of goods sold. This tells you if the profit model holds up when you ramp up.
