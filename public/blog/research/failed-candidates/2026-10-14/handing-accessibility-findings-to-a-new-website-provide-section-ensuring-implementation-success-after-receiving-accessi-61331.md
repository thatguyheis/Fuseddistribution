Ensuring Implementation Success After Receiving Accessibility Findings

You need to give your new website provider a clear map. First, define the target standard. Tell them exactly which WCAG version you expect them to meet. This sets the boundaries for what you're looking at. This tells them the exact rules they must follow for your site.

Next, organize your findings by severity. Sort the issues into Critical, Serious, and Minor categories. This lets the provider know where to focus their immediate attention. Critical items need fixing fast. Serious issues require careful planning. Minor items can be addressed later in the development cycle.

For each finding, show them what you mean. Don't just send a link or an error code. Send specific screenshots or short videos. This makes the problem concrete. Show them the exact spot on the site where the issue occurs.

Provide clear next steps. Don't just state the problem. Tell them how to fix it. Give them technical suggestions or small code examples. This helps their developers jump straight to a solution.

Finally, set up a check-in system. Establish a regular time for them to send you their plan. Agree on a format for these updates. This makes the remediation a cycle. You work together to confirm the fixes are correct before moving on.
