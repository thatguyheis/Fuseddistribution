You need to figure out who owns what on your online presence. Trying to spread the workload across the team just creates confusion. When everyone does a little bit of everything, nothing gets done right.

Designate one person to be the main owner of your Google Business Profile. This person handles the daily updates and the core profile. They are the one responsible for making sure the information is accurate and current. If you wait for everyone else to do it, the profile will get messy.

Next, assign someone to manage reviews. This person checks in on customer feedback and replies to comments. Make sure this person knows exactly what to do once the profile owner has made a change. It stops tasks from getting lost in the shuffle.

Find a second person to check your consistency. This person looks at other places online. They make sure your business name, address, and phone number are the same everywhere. This is a simple weekly check. It keeps things looking solid.

Create a short checklist. Give your team one page to look at each week. Tell them to focus only on the owner's tasks. This keeps accountability clear. You see who is doing what and when.
