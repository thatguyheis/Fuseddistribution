You need someone on your team to own the local visibility. Don't try to make everyone the expert. That gets messy and nobody takes responsibility. Pick one person to be the profile owner. This person handles the Google Business Profile. They update it daily. They manage the main content. Make it their job.

Next, assign someone to manage reviews. This person checks for feedback. They respond to reviews. They make sure the owner knows what’s happening. This keeps the profile looking good. Reviews matter a lot for local search. Don't let that fall apart.

Find someone to check consistency. This person looks at your name and address everywhere online. They check for typos. They make sure your details match up across all listings. This is visibility checking. It keeps things clean and stops you from showing up wrong.

When the profile owner needs to pass something on, write it down. Document the steps. Tell the reviewer exactly what to do. This stops confusion. It keeps the hand-off clear.

Make a simple list for your team. A one-page checklist. It should only focus on what the profile owner needs to do. This keeps accountability simple. Your team knows their part. You keep the focus on what actually gets you calls.
