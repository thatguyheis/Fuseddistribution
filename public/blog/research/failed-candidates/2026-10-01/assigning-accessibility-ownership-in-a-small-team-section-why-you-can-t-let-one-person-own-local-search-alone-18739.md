Why you can't let one person own local search alone

You're busy. You're running the business. Trying to manage your website, your calls, and your Google presence all by yourself is a recipe for burnout. Local search isn't something you just set up and forget. It needs consistent attention.

Designate one person to own the Google Business Profile. This person handles the daily updates. They post new photos, answer initial questions, and keep the information accurate. This person is the main point of contact for Google.

Then, assign someone else to manage reviews. Monitoring and responding to customer feedback is critical. This person watches for new reviews and replies to them. They keep the customer connection strong.

You need someone else to check consistency. This person looks at listings across the web. They make sure your name, address, and phone number match everywhere. This keeps things clean.

Set clear handoffs. Know exactly when the profile owner needs to pass a task to the reviewer. Don't leave it open. Document the steps. This stops confusion and keeps the work moving.

Finally, make a simple weekly check. Give your team a one-page list. Tell them to only look at the profile owner's tasks. This keeps accountability clear. You don't need to manage everything yourself. You just need a clear system.
