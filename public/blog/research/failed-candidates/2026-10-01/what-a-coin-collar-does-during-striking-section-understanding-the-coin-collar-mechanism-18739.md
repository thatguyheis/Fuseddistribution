The coin collar happens during the striking of a silver coin. It's a specific step in the manufacturing process. This coating spreads a thin layer of silver evenly over the coin. You see this layer on the coin's surface.

This process keeps the metal pure. It ensures consistent purity throughout the coin. The collar stops uneven metal from spreading during striking. This means the coin's quality stays consistent.

You can look at the coin to see this work. Understanding the collar helps you check the production quality. It shows how the coin was made. This detail matters when you look at a coin.
