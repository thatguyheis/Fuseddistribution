You need clear ownership for your Google Business Profile. Don't let it just float around. Assign one person to be the profile owner. This person handles the setup and the daily updates. They are the main point of contact for everything related to that profile.

Next, you need someone to watch the reviews. Assign a specific person to monitor customer feedback. This person responds to reviews directly, keeping the tone consistent. Link this review management back to the profile owner. They work together on keeping the profile strong.

Find someone to check consistency. This person checks your Name, Address, and Phone number across the web. They check other listings, not just your main profile. This person ensures everything matches up everywhere.

Make the hand-off simple. Write down exactly when the profile owner needs to pass a task to the reviewer. Be clear about what needs to happen next. This stops confusion.

Create a simple weekly check. Make a one-page list. Only focus on what the profile owner needs to do. This keeps accountability clear for your whole team.
