You need someone in charge of your Google Business Profile. Don't leave it floating around. You pick one person, maybe it's you right now, and make them the main owner of that profile. They handle the daily stuff. They post updates, they answer simple questions. This keeps things consistent.

Then you need someone to watch the reviews. Assign a specific person to monitor what customers are saying. They respond to reviews. This shows you care. Make sure that person knows exactly what to do. That person links back to the profile owner for big changes.

You also need a second set of eyes. Pick someone to check things weekly. They look at all your online listings. They check if your name, address, and phone number match everywhere. This stops confusion.

When the profile owner has to pass something on, make it clear. Write down the exact steps. Who does what. This stops confusion and makes sure no one misses a step. You can make a simple checklist. Focus it just on the owner's job. That keeps accountability clear.
