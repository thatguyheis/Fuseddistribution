Your Google Business Profile is free. Most businesses set it up wrong, which hides you from local searches. You gotta fix that first. Claim your correct service area. Pick the right categories so you show up in those first few map results. That’s where local customers look.

Reviews are your local ranking fuel. They matter more than a fancy website for local search. Set up a simple way to ask happy customers for feedback. Don't wait for big projects. Just make it a habit.

Your website needs to tell Google exactly what you do and where you do it. Be clear. State your services and your city plainly. This connects your site to your physical place of business. Consistency beats complexity. Small, regular updates to your profile and site are what actually work.
