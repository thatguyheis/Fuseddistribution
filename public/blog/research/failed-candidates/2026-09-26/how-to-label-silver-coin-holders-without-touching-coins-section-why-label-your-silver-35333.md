
You need a way to track your silver. Keeping inventory clear is important. You can use passive RFID tags. Attach these tags to your holder or container. This lets you scan items remotely. You track them without touching the silver.

Another option is a barcode or QR code. Print a unique code on a sturdy card. Use a phone app to scan the code. This updates your inventory instantly. You log where each piece is kept.

Color coding works too. Pick a simple color scheme. Use it to mark different metal types. This is a low-tech way to see what you have. It makes finding things much easier.

Link your physical items to a digital list. Use a spreadsheet for this work. Connect your asset locations to the digital labels. This keeps your tracking separate from the actual metal.

Choose good materials for your labels. Select tags that handle storage well. Make sure they stay dry. Your labels need to survive where you keep your silver.
