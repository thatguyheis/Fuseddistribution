
You need to know what you're handling. Look closely at the packaging for your coins. It tells you something important about the material. See if it's PVC. PVC can cause chemical issues with silver over time. This is a risk you need to see.

Control the environment around your coins. Temperature swings stress the packaging. Direct sunlight can damage paper materials. Humidity can lead to mold growth. Keep your storage area steady. Avoid places with sudden heat or cold changes.

When you archive your coins, choose the right box. Use acid-free, lignin-free materials. These keep the silver safe. Standard cardboard boxes can release gases. They can harm your collection. Use sleeves that protect the packaging. This keeps everything stable.

Handle the packaging with care. Always use clean, dry hands. Do not touch the coin or the box with your bare skin. Inspect the packaging often. Look for any dampness or color changes. This checks for damage before it happens.
