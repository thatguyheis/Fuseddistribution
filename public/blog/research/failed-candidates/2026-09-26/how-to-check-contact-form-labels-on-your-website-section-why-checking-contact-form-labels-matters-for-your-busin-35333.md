Checking your contact form labels is simple. You need to make sure everything works right before customers try to reach you.

First, check every input field. Make sure there's a clear label right above or next to the box for the name, email, and message. If a label is missing, people get confused. They won't know what to type.

Next, test the whole thing out. Send a test message using all the fields. Confirm that the data actually lands in the right inbox. You want to know the message gets to you fast.

Watch for small mistakes. A simple typo in a label, like writing "Email Address" instead of "Email," messes things up. Users get frustrated and leave the form. Consistency is key here. Use the same style for all your forms so visitors know what to expect every time.
