You need to train your AI with real customer talk. Don't just ask for generic questions. Think about the actual work you do. For a plumber, ask about scheduling a repair or what to do when the water suddenly stops. For a salon, ask about a specific service complaint or how to book a cut.

You have to feed the AI the frustration. Include examples where customers get confused or get angry. Show it what not to say. This teaches the AI to actually help solve problems, not just repeat canned responses.

Use the language your customers actually use. If your local clients use specific slang or terms, use them in your test set. This makes the AI sound like a local, not some big corporation.

Test the drafts right away. See how the AI handles the examples. Get feedback fast. Change the test set based on what you learn. This loop is how you make sure the AI drafts sound exactly like your business.
