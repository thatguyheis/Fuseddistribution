## Why Indexing Matters for Local Visibility

When you update a service page, Google doesn't know about it right away. You gotta tell it. Think of it like sending a package to the post office. You put the new page in, but you need to send a note saying, "Hey, this address changed, please check it now."

You use Google Search Console for this. It's your direct line to Google. After you make that change on your site, go to the URL Inspection tool. Find the specific service page you just updated.

Then, click "Request Indexing." That tells Google, "I changed this, please look at it now." This isn't magic. It just signals Google to come check the new version.

Before you ask, check for mistakes. See if GSC flags any errors on that page. Fixing those issues first keeps Google from getting confused by the old version of the page.

After you request indexing, you need to wait a bit. Google crawls your site. Watch the Coverage report in GSC. You'll see if the page status moves from "Not Indexed" to "Indexed." That means Google has found your update and is starting to show it.
