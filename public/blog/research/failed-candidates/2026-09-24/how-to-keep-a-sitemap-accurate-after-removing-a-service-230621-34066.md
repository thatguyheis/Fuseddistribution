## Why Does Removing a Service Break Your Sitemap

Removing a service means you gotta clean up your sitemap. If you just delete the page, Google still sees a link pointing to it. Your sitemap is a list of all the pages Google should look at. If you don't tell Google about the page you took down, it gets confused.

You need to find where that old service URL is listed in your sitemap file. Look through that file and spot every link to the service you removed. Then, you gotta delete that specific URL entry from the sitemap. Don't just delete the page; delete the reference to it in the sitemap.

After you edit the sitemap, you gotta make sure Google knows about the changes. You'll need to regenerate or update your sitemap index file. This tells Google where to look next. It’s a simple fix, but it keeps Google focused on the pages you still want them to see.

Finally, you check your site for any other links pointing to that old service. Make sure those links either redirect somewhere useful or just get taken out. Use Google Search Console to confirm Google has re-indexed everything else correctly. That’s how you keep things clean.
