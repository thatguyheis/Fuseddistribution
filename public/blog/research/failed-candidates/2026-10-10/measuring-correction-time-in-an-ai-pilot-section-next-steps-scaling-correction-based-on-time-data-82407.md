You need to know what a real fix looks like. A correction isn't about getting a vague ranking. It's about a specific outcome. Are you talking about getting more calls from your website? Or maybe a specific service request form filling up? That's what you need to define first.

You can't measure improvement without a starting point. Figure out your current lead flow. Track how many calls you get now. See what your current website actually does for you. That's your baseline. You can't see if the change works if you don't know where you're starting from.

Then you track the time it takes. See how long it takes from making a change to getting that first real sign of improvement. Look for that moment when phone calls actually start coming in. Ignore the noise. Focus on those calls. That proves the change is connecting people to your business.

Use that timing to make quick moves. If it takes too long, you adjust the AI prompt or the website layout right away. Don't wait for a big report. Use the time data to fix things fast. This keeps you moving forward, not stuck waiting for promises.
