Silver isn't found all at once. It sits inside various forms within ore deposits. Miners start by crushing the ore. This process frees the silver from the rock.

Next comes the refining stage. They separate the silver from base metals. This uses chemical or physical methods. The goal is to get pure silver. Purity standards set the final grade you see.

The supply chain sets the final price. This chain shows where the silver comes from. It shows how much is available. This affects the cost and what you can buy. You need to see this path to understand the metal.
