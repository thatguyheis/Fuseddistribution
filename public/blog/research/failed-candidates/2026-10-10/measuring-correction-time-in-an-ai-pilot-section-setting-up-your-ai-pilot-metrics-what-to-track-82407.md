You need to know what a real improvement looks like. Don't just look at big numbers. Define what a correction means for your business. Is it getting a specific service keyword higher in search? Is it seeing more people fill out your contact form? Be clear about what success looks like before you start.

You have to start with where you are now. You can't measure a fix if you don't know the starting point. Track your current phone calls, your actual service inquiries. That’s your baseline. You need that number to see if anything changes later.

Don't wait for the end result. Watch the time between making a change and when you see the first real lift in calls. That lag time tells you if the change is working fast or slow. Focus on calls and inquiries, not just likes or views. Those metrics don't mean anything for your bottom line.

Use that timing to make a move. If you see no change in a week, you adjust the prompt or the website setup right away. Don't let things drag on. Measure the correction time so you know when to pivot.
