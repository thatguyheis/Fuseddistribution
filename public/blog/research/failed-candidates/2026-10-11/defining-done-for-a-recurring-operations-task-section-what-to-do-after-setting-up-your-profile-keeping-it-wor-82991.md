Claim your Google Business Profile. It’s free. Most local businesses set it up wrong. You miss the basic steps that tell Google exactly what you do and where you are. Get it right, and you start showing up when people search for your service in your area.

Keep your Name, Address, and Phone number exactly the same everywhere online. This is called NAP consistency. Google checks this to make sure you’re the same business across the internet. If it’s different, it confuses Google and hurts your chances. Be consistent.

Use the Services section. Don't just write a general description. List the specific jobs you do. If someone searches for "HVAC repair near me," you need to show up for that exact phrase. Be specific about what you fix.

Respond to every review you get. Reply to customers. Google sees this activity. It shows them you’re paying attention. It tells Google you care about your local customers.

Use the Posts feature for quick updates. Post about a new service, a special offer, or your new hours. This is low effort. It’s high impact. It keeps your profile fresh and shows you’re active. That’s how you keep getting found.
