Your Google Business Profile is free. Most businesses set it up wrong. You gotta get that profile set up right, or you’re invisible when people search for your service nearby. It’s not rocket science. It’s just making sure your name, address, and phone number are exactly the same everywhere online. Google checks for that consistency. If it’s messy, you lose credibility fast. Fix that NAP consistency now. It builds trust with searchers.

Don't just list everything vaguely. Use the Services section to tell Google exactly what you do. Instead of a long paragraph, list your specific offerings. If you do plumbing, list "Drain Cleaning," "Water Heater Repair," and "Sewer Backup." That way, someone searching for drain cleaning finds you. They find you for what you actually offer.

Reviews matter more than your website for local search. You need to engage with every review. Reply to positive feedback, and handle any complaints. Google sees this activity. It signals that you’re a real business that cares. Don't ignore them.

Keep things moving with posts. Use the "Posts" feature for quick updates. Announce a new service, a holiday hours change, or a special offer. This is low effort for you. It keeps you visible. It shows Google you’re active. It helps you get seen when people are looking for exactly what you provide.
