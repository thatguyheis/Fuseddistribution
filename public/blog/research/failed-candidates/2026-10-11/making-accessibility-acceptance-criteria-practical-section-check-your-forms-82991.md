Check your forms now. Your contact page isn't just for you. It needs to work for everyone. If you use a contact form, make sure the labels are clear. A screen reader needs to know what each box is asking for. If it’s confusing, people leave before they even try to send it.

Test your inquiry process. Can someone easily find the way to submit a message? Think about the steps. Does the form flow logically? If it’s a maze, potential customers won't finish. Make sure the submission feels simple. You want people to send you a message, not get frustrated by a bad form.

Every picture on your site needs description. Alt text is not optional. When you use a photo of your work or your shop, tell Google and users what the picture is about. This helps search engines understand your business. It’s a basic fix that shows you care about how people find you online.

Your menu needs to be easy to use. Test your navigation. Can someone using a screen reader jump from your homepage straight to your plumbing service page? Keep it simple. Clear links mean people can find what they need fast. Fixing these small things improves your site’s health. Google likes sites that are easy to use.
