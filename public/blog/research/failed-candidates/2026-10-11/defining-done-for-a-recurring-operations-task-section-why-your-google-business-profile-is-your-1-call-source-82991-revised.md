Your Google Business Profile is your first stop for local customers. It’s free. But most people set it up wrong, so you're missing out on calls.

You gotta make sure your Name, Address, and Phone number are exactly the same everywhere online. Google checks this. If they don't match, it messes up your local ranking. Consistency builds trust with the search engine.

Use the Services section. Don't just write a general description. List exactly what you do. If someone searches for "plumber near me," you need to show up for "emergency pipe repair," not just "plumbing services." Be specific so people find you for the job they need right now.

Engage with reviews. Don't just ignore them. Reply to every review. This shows Google you care. It signals that you're an active, real business.

Finally, use the Posts feature. It’s a quick way to share a new offer or your updated hours. Low effort. High impact. Keep that profile active, and you start getting seen by people ready to call you.
