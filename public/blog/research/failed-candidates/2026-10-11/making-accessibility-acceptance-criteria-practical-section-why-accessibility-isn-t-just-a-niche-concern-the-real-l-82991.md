Accessibility isn't just for big tech companies. It’s about making sure the local customer who needs your service can actually find you when they search online. If your website is hard to read for someone using a screen reader, that customer leaves. They won't call you.

You gotta focus on the content first. Make sure your service descriptions and contact forms are structured so a screen reader can follow them easily. If someone can’t read what you offer, they won't even see your pricing.

Check your forms. Are the labels clear? Can someone navigate the submission process without getting lost? Test it. This is basic.

Every photo you use needs alt text. Tell Google and users what the picture is about. This helps search engines understand your business and helps people find you when they look at your photos.

Keep your navigation simple. Test your main menu. Can someone easily jump from your homepage to your specific service page? Simple navigation helps Google see your site as easy to use. Fixing these small things builds a better foundation for local search.
