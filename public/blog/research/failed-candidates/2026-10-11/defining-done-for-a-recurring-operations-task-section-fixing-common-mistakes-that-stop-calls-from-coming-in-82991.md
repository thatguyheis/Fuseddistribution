Your Google Business Profile is free. Most businesses set it up wrong. You're invisible because of a simple setup error.

First, claim your free profile. Google Business Profile is how local people find you. Most owners miss the basic steps that actually drive local searches. You need to fill out every section completely. Don't just list your business name. List exactly what you do. This profile is your first impression. Get it right, and you start showing up when people search for your service in your area.

Next, make sure your details match everywhere. This is called NAP consistency. Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be the same on your website, your profile, and every other listing. Google checks this. If the details jump around, Google gets confused. It loses trust in your business. Keep those three things identical everywhere online.

Don't just list what you do generally. Use the Services section. Tell people exactly what you offer. Instead of saying "We do plumbing," list "Drain Cleaning," "Water Heater Repair," or "Sewer Line Installation." This lets searchers find you for the specific fix they need right now.

Engage with what people say about you. Reviews matter for local search. When someone leaves feedback, respond. A simple thank you shows Google you care. It signals to the search engine that you are an active, real business serving your community.

Finally, keep things current with simple posts. Use the update feature for quick things. Post about new hours, a special offer, or a quick tip. This is low effort for you. It gives searchers a reason to click on your profile right now. Stop waiting for big changes. Start fixing these small, direct steps today.
