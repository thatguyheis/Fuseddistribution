How Practical Criteria Boost Your Google Visibility

You need to make sure your website actually works for everyone searching for your services. Google needs to see that your site is easy to use. Start with your main pages. Make sure your service descriptions and contact forms are readable by screen readers. Don't just make them look nice. Make sure the text is clear and logical for people using assistive technology.

Test your contact forms right now. Are the fields clearly labeled? Can someone using a screen reader follow the steps to send a message? If the form is confusing, people leave. This directly hurts your chances of getting noticed. Google looks at how easy it is to interact with your site.

Every photo you put on your site needs alt text. This text tells Google what the picture is about. It also helps people who can't see the picture understand your work. Don't skip this step. It's not optional.

Your navigation needs to be simple too. Check your main menu. Can someone using a screen reader easily jump from your homepage to your plumbing page? A complicated menu scares people away. Simple navigation keeps visitors on your site longer.

Fixing these small things makes your site healthier overall. When your site is easy to use, Google sees that. This basic fix helps your local business show up better in search results. Focus on these concrete steps first.
