**The 5 Simple Steps to Optimize Your GBP for Local Calls**

Claim your Google Business Profile. It’s free. Most businesses set it up wrong, and that’s where you lose calls. Google sees your profile as your storefront. You need to get the basics right first.

Make sure your Name, Address, and Phone number are exactly the same everywhere online. This is called NAP consistency. If it’s different on your website and on Yelp, Google gets confused. It erodes trust fast. Keep it locked down.

Use the Services section. Don't just list "plumbing." List "Drain cleaning," "Water heater repair," and "Sewer line installation." People search for specific fixes. Show Google exactly what you fix.

Engage with your reviews. Don't ignore them. When someone leaves feedback, reply. This shows Google you care and proves you're a real, active local business. It moves you up in search results.

Keep things fresh with Posts. Use the update feature for quick notices. Post about a new service you started or a special offer. It takes little time, but it keeps you visible when people are looking right now.
