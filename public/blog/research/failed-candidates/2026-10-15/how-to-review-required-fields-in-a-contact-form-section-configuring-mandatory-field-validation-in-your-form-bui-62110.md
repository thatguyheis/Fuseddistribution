Configuring Mandatory Field Validation in Your Form Builder

First, you must define why you need certain information. Think about your business process. What specific data do you need to move forward? For example, you need an email address so you can follow up with the customer. Clearly state this need for each field. This documentation helps you make smart decisions about what information is truly necessary.

Next, you'll set the requirement within your form builder. Look at the specific settings for your platform. Gravity Forms or Typeform have their own ways of setting field requirements. You need to find the exact control that tells the system a field must be filled out. This setting is what actually enforces the rule.

You must test the form yourself. Visit the form as if you were a new visitor. Try submitting it with one field left empty. You need to confirm the system stops the submission. This check ensures that your setup works exactly as you intend it to.

Finally, make sure you know the difference between mandatory and optional items. Label your fields clearly. Some data is essential for your next step. Other data is just extra information. Review your list to spot any duplicate questions. Asking for the same thing twice slows down your users.
