Audit your existing sitemap first. Generate a full, current sitemap. This gives you a baseline of all URLs and service entries. You'll see everything currently listed.

Next, find the service you plan to remove. Cross-reference that service with your sitemap. Pinpoint every URL, category, or entry that belongs to that service. Be precise about what you need to take out.

Then, follow a clear removal protocol. Delete the specific XML entries from the sitemap file. Or, use your sitemap management software to mark those entries for exclusion. Make the deletion direct and unambiguous.

You must update internal redirects. Make sure any old links pointing to the removed service go to the correct, existing pages. Use a 301 status code for these redirects. This keeps your sitemap links intact.

Finally, run a post-removal validation. Use an XML sitemap validator tool. Check the resulting file to confirm it is clean and syntactically correct. Verify it only shows the services you want to keep.
