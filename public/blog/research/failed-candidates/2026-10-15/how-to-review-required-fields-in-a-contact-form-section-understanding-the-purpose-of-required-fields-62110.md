Setting up required fields tells your visitors exactly what information you need. You must first define the reason for each field. Explain to yourself why you need an email address. You need it for follow up. This clarity helps you build a better form.

Your form builder has specific settings for this. Look at your platform's tools. You need to know how to set the requirement level for each field. This is how you make sure the system enforces the rule. You can control this setting directly within your form builder's configuration.

You must check the user experience. Test the form yourself. Try filling it out without providing a required piece of data. See if the system stops the submission. This confirms that the requirement works as intended for your users.

Be clear about what is mandatory. Mark fields that need an answer as required. Separate these from optional details. Some information is vital for your business. Other information is just extra data. This distinction guides the user experience.

Check for overlapping questions. Do you ask for the same information twice? Having redundant fields makes the form feel long. It increases the chance that someone will stop filling it out. Keep your required fields focused and necessary.
