Define why you need each field. Don't just make things required. Write down exactly what you need the email for. You need it for follow-up. You need it to send the quote. This documentation tells you what information is truly essential for your business.

Check your form builder settings. Each platform has its own way to set field requirements. You'll need to look in the builder's options. Find the specific setting for making a field mandatory. This is how you enforce the rule that users must fill it out before submitting.

Test the form yourself. Pretend you are a new visitor. Fill out the form intentionally leaving one required field empty. See if the system stops the submission. You must confirm that the form stops working correctly when a field is missed. This checks your enforcement works as expected.

Tell users what matters. Label fields clearly. Use words like must have or required. Keep other fields optional. This helps visitors know what information they can skip. Don't ask for the same data twice. This stops people from filling out the same thing multiple times.
