The Core of Citation

You need to show where your silver supply chart comes from. Simply pointing to a graph isn't enough. You must state the exact year the data was gathered. For example, you should write, "This chart shows silver supply data collected in 2023." This direct attribution tells your reader exactly when the information was recorded.

Next, you link the visual to the original source. Tell people where you got the chart. Did you pull it from a specific financial database? Cite that database name. This shows your work is based on verifiable information. You can use APA or MLA styles for this. Check your guide for the right format for referencing historical silver supply documents.

The year gives context to the supply and demand you show. Knowing the year helps explain market shifts. A change in supply during 2024 tells a different story than a change in 2021. Keep the factual year separate from your own thoughts. Don't mix up when the data was collected and what you think it means.
