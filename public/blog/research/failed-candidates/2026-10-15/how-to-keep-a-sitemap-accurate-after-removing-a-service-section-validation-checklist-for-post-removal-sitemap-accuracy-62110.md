Validation Checklist for Post Removal Sitemap Accuracy

First, you must audit your current sitemap. Generate a full, complete sitemap now. This gives you a baseline. You need to see every URL and every service entry present before you start making changes. This step sets your starting point for accuracy.

Next, find the service you are taking down. Look through that sitemap. You need to spot every URL, category, or entry that belongs to that specific service. Pinpoint exactly what you need to remove from the file.

Then, you execute the removal. Delete the XML entries for the service you removed. If you use software, mark those entries for exclusion instead. Be precise with your deletion.

After deletion, you must check your redirects. Make sure any old links pointing to the service you took down go to the right new pages. Use 301 status codes for these redirects. This keeps your site structure sound.

Finally, run a validation tool. Use an XML sitemap validator. Check the resulting file immediately. Confirm that it is clean. It must only show the services you still offer. This confirms your work is correct.
