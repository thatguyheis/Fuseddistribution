To keep your sitemap accurate after taking a service down, you need a clear, step-by-step process. First, audit your current sitemap. Generate a full, up-to-date sitemap. This sets a baseline showing every URL and service entry you have right now. You'll see everything currently listed.

Next, find the service you plan to remove. Compare that service against your sitemap. Identify every URL, category, or entry that is specifically linked to that service. Be precise about what you need to take out.

Then, follow a specific removal protocol. Delete the exact XML entries for the removed service from the sitemap file. Alternatively, use your sitemap management software to mark those entries for exclusion. Make the deletion direct and clear.

You must update internal links. Make sure any old links pointing to the service you removed go to the correct, existing pages. Use a 301 status code for these redirects. This keeps the links in your sitemap working.

Finally, run a check after removal. Use an XML sitemap validator tool. Check the resulting file to confirm it is clean and correct. Verify that it only shows the services you intend to keep.
