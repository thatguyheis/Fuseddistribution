To keep your sitemap accurate after taking a service offline, you need to follow a few specific steps. First, audit your current sitemap. Generate a full, complete sitemap right now. This creates a baseline showing every URL and service entry you currently have. This baseline tells you exactly what your sitemap looks like before you make any changes.

Next, you need to find the specific links related to the service you are removing. Look through the sitemap. Find every URL, category, or entry that points to that old service. Pinpoint exactly which entries need to be taken out.

Then, you remove those entries. Delete the XML parts from your sitemap file. If you use sitemap management software, mark those entries for exclusion instead. This cleans up the file right away.

You must update any old links pointing to the removed service. Any page linking to that old service needs to go to the correct, existing page. Set up a 301 redirect to the right location. This keeps your internal links working properly.

Finally, you check the result. Use an XML sitemap validator tool. Run the check immediately after deletion. Make sure the new sitemap file is clean. It should only show the services you still offer.
