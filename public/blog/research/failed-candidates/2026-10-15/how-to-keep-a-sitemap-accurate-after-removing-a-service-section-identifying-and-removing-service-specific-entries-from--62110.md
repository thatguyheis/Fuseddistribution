Identifying and Removing Service Specific Entries from the Sitemap

First, you gotta check your current sitemap. Generate a complete sitemap to get a full list of all your URLs and service entries. This gives you a baseline. You need to see exactly what's in there before you start making changes.

Next, you find the service you plan to take down. Compare that service to your sitemap. Pinpoint every URL, category, or entry that specifically points to that service. Be thorough in this search. You want to find every single reference.

Then, you remove those entries. Delete the relevant XML entries from the sitemap file. If you use software, mark them for exclusion instead of deleting them outright. This is how you manage the removal process cleanly.

After deletion, you must fix internal links. Change any old links pointing to the service you removed. Make sure they point to the correct, existing pages using a 301 redirect. This keeps your sitemap links intact.

Finally, run a check. Use an XML sitemap validator tool right away. Confirm the resulting file is clean. It must be syntactically correct and show only the services you still offer.
