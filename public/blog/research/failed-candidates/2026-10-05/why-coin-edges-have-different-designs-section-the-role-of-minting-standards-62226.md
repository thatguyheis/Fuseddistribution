The shape of a coin's edge tells a story. Each coin gets a unique die. This die sets the exact line. It shapes the edge you see.

The force used to strike the coin matters too. This force affects the metal. It shows how the silver flows. This makes the edge a record. It shows the machine's settings.

The coin type changes the edge profile. A round coin has a different edge. A bar has a different look. The metal reacts to the tooling. Different runs mean different finishes.

The silver itself plays a part. It interacts with the tooling. This interaction makes the edge unique. You see this difference when you look closely.
