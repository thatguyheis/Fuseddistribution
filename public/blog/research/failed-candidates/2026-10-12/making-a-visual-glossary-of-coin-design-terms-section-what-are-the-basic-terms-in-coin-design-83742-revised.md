You need to know these terms before you look at a coin. Understanding these labels helps you avoid confusion. It keeps you focused on what you actually buy.

A mint mark shows who made the coin. It tells you the issuing authority. This marks the country or entity that created the coin. It’s a key piece of identification.

Purity tells you how much metal is in the coin. Fineness is the standard. It shows the exact metal content. This is important for knowing the actual value. Purity is what you need to check.

Denomination is the face value. This relates to the coin's physical size and weight. It shows the amount of money the coin represents. This helps you match the coin to your purchase plan.

Design affects collectibility. Specific features matter. They influence how others view the coin. Knowing this helps you decide what you are looking for.

Knowing these terms reduces risk. You see the details clearly. You make a smarter choice.
