## Brand Identity Summary

**Name:** Fused Distribution Technology Solutions
**Sub-line:** Technology Solutions
**CTA Target:** /#contact
**Site:** fuseddistribution.com
**Positioning:** We build websites and digital marketing that actually get local businesses found on Google. No jargon, no retainer traps, no vague promises.
**What this brand is NOT:** Not a national agency. Not an SEO farm. Not a tool subscription service. Not complicated.

---

## Audience Profile

**Primary Segment:** Local business owners, ages 35-60, service-based businesses (contractors, salons, restaurants, clinics, trades). They are not tech experts and are frustrated by websites that get zero calls. They feel competitors are ranking better despite doing worse work.

**Pain Points (What they say):**
* "I have a website but nobody calls from it."
* "I don't even show up when people search for me."
* "I don't know what I'm paying for with my current service."
* "I asked Google how to get more reviews and got a wall of text."
* "I don't have time to post on social media every day."

**Objections (What they distrust):**
* Agencies promising page-one rankings without explaining the method.
* Long contracts they can't easily break from.
* Dashboards and reports they can't understand.
* Anyone who talks about "impressions" or "reach" instead of phone calls.

---

## Voice & Tone

**Register:** Second person (you/your). Active voice. Present tense. Direct.
**Sentence Style:** Short. Plain. Written like a busy business owner who just needs to know what to do.
**Energy:** Straight to the point. No fluff, but no condescension. You sound like a trusted friend who actually knows the answers.

**On-Brand Examples:**
* "Your Google Business Profile is free. Most people set it up wrong."
* "Reviews matter more than your website for local search. That's just how Google works."
* "If someone searches your service plus your city and you don't show up, you're invisible."

**Off-Brand Examples (Never Use):**
* "Harness the power of digital transformation to elevate your brand's online presence." (Too much buzzword)
* "In today's competitive digital landscape, having a robust online ecosystem is paramount." (AI filler)
* "Leverage our comprehensive suite of solutions to streamline your marketing efforts." (Agency-speak)

---

## Content Pillars & UGC Principles

**Preferred Formats:** Founder stories, Demos (best for skeptical small business owners).

**Authenticity Cues for Local Audience:**
* Speak like a peer, not a distant expert. Use "I used to make this mistake too."
* Use concrete business examples in the hook ("If you run a plumbing business...").
* Show the actual steps in a demo, not just theory.
* Explain frustration with vague agency promises as your origin story.
* Avoid technical terms unless you immediately explain them.

**Forbidden Words (Agency-Speak to Avoid):**
* synergy, ecosystem, paradigm, digital transformation
*
* Guaranteed results, page one guaranteed (Avoid any illegal/undelievable claims)
*
* Disrupt, innovate (Startup clichés)

## Visual Identity Focus: Setting Up Your Google Business Profile The Free Foundation

**Goal:** Provide specific, useful, actionable advice on setting up the Google Business Profile.

**Tone Check:** Plain, direct, second person (you/your), short sentences, use contractions. No em dashes or en dashes. No hedging. No filler transitions. No filler openers.

Your Google Business Profile is your free storefront. Set up accurate hours, services, and photos today. This is where people find you when they search locally. Get it right now, or you’re leaving money on the table.

Reviews fuel your local ranking. You need them. Ask happy customers for reviews on Google and Yelp. Don't wait for them to find you. Systematically get those reviews. They tell Google you're a trusted local.

Consistency beats intensity. Show up for local search every week. Keep updating your profile. This keeps you visible. It shows you’re active in your community.

Focus on what customers search for. Think "Plumber near me" or "Best dentist in [City]." Put those exact phrases into your profile. Optimize your profile answers for those search terms. Ignore the noise. Don't chase vanity metrics. Focus only on calls and shows up.
