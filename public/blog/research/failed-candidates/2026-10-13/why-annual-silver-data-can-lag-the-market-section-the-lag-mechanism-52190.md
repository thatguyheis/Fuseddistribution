Annual silver data often falls behind what's happening in the market. Official reports are compiled looking back at past performance. This creates a reporting frequency mismatch. The market, however, reacts to daily news and instant changes in supply and demand. These real-time shifts move prices quickly.

Annual statistics usually summarize historical production or inventory. This can hide current, rapid changes in physical silver availability. These physical stock levels directly impact spot prices. The numbers you see are old snapshots. They don't show the immediate reality of the silver market.

Market pricing includes speculative elements. It absorbs immediate news cycles and trading activity. This speculative behavior doesn't fit into slow, annual statistical releases. The lag shows that market sentiment matters now. It shows the difference between slow, long-term trends and fast, short-term volatility.
