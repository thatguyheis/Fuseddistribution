Ensuring Accessibility Compliance Through Task Management

You need a clear way to handle every accessibility repair request. Start by segmenting customer requests. Sort each job into specific categories. Think about physical barrier removal, integrating assistive technology, or making environmental changes. This separation lets you see what you're dealing with.

Next, you must score the complexity of each task. Make a scoring chart. This chart weighs factors like how hard the repair is. It also looks at the skills your team needs. Finally, consider your team's capacity. Map out which tasks fit your current skills and time. This stops you from taking on too much work at once.

Align the customer's goal with the repair queue. Document exactly what the customer wants to achieve. Place that goal first in the queue. This means urgent fixes that affect access get attention right away. After assigning a task, check your process. Review your prioritization logic. Adjust your workflow for the next set of customer tasks.
