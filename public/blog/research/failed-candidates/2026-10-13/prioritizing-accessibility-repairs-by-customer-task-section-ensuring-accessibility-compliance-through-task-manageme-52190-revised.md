You need a clear method for managing every accessibility repair request. First, you must sort customer requests. Put each job into specific categories. Think about physical barrier removal, integrating assistive technology, or making environmental changes. This separation lets you see what you're dealing with.

Next, you'll score the complexity of each task. Make a scoring chart. This chart weighs how hard the repair is. It also checks the skills your team needs. Finally, map out your team's capacity. Show which repair tasks fit your current skills and available time. This stops you from taking on too much work at once.

You must align the customer's stated goal with the repair queue. Document exactly what the customer wants to achieve. Place that goal first in the queue. This means urgent fixes that affect access get attention right away. After assigning a task, check your process. Review your prioritization logic. Adjust your workflow for the next set of customer tasks.
