You need a system for sorting accessibility repair requests. Start by segmenting every customer request into specific repair types. Think about physical barriers, assistive technology setup, or environmental changes. This clear classification lets you see exactly what kind of work you have.

Next, you must score each task. Create a matrix to judge the difficulty of the repair. Consider the skill needed. Also, think about how much impact this fix has on the customer's ability to use a space. This scoring helps you see which issues demand immediate attention.

Map your team's skills to the tasks. Draw a visual chart showing which repairs your team can handle right now. This prevents you from taking on too much work at once. Match the task to the person with the right expertise and the available time.

Finally, link the customer's stated goal directly to the repair order. If a customer says they need a ramp, that task gets placed at the top. This ensures the most urgent, high-impact fixes get started first. After each assignment, check your logic. Adjust the process for the next set of requests.
