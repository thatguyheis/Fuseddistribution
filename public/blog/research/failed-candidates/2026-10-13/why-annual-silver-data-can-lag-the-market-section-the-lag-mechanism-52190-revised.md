Annual silver data often falls behind what's happening in the market. Official reports look back at past performance. This creates a mismatch in timing. The market reacts to daily news and instant shifts in supply and demand. These real-time changes move prices fast.

Annual statistics usually summarize historical production or inventory. This can hide current, rapid changes in physical silver availability. These physical stock levels directly affect spot prices. The numbers you see are old snapshots. They don't show the immediate reality of the silver market.

Market pricing includes speculative elements. It absorbs immediate news cycles and trading activity. This speculative behavior doesn't fit into slow, annual statistical releases. The lag shows that what traders feel now matters. It shows the difference between slow, long-term trends and fast, short-term price swings.
