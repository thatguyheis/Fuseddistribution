Your Google Business Profile is your first stop. It’s free. Most people set it up wrong. Get it right. This is the first place folks look when they need your service. Make sure your hours, services, and photos are accurate. This is the base layer.

Don't just ask for a review. Ask for specific feedback. Instead of a general request, ask about a particular part of the service. For example, after a plumbing job, ask, "How was the speed of the repair?" or "Was the explanation of the cost clear?" Specific answers are better than vague praise. They tell Google exactly what you do well.

You have to answer everything. Reply to every review. Good reviews get you seen. Bad reviews show you care. When you respond, show you read what they said. This shows you’re open and responsible. That builds trust with potential customers. Reviews directly change how people choose you over the other businesses on the map.
