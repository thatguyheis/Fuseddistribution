AI summaries aren't always accurate. They can make things up. You need to check what the AI says against the real source. Look for claims that don't match any specific sentences or data points in the original document.

You must check the main numbers in the summary. Find the figures or names the AI mentions and go back to the source to confirm them. Don't just trust the numbers the AI pulls out.

Make sure the summary keeps the original meaning. The AI shouldn't change what a section actually means. It should pull facts, not rewrite the idea.

Trace the summary back to the exact spot in the document. See which paragraph or section the AI pulled that specific sentence from. This shows you where the information came from.

Watch the tone. Does the summary sound different from the original writing? If the tone shifts a lot, it signals that the summary isn't solid.
