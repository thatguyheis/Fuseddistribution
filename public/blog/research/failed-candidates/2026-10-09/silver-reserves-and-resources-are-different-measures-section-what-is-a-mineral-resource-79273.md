A mineral resource shows the total silver potential in a geological area. This is the entire amount of silver that could be found there. It is just a potential estimate.

A silver reserve is different. It is a specific, extractable quantity of silver. This number reflects what you can actually get from the ground. Reserves are set by economic feasibility. They show where silver extraction makes financial sense.

You need to look at reserves. They tell you about reliable inventory sources. This helps you assess where to focus your acquisition efforts. Don't just look at the total resource estimate. Focus on the actual reserve figure. That figure tells you what is realistic for you.
