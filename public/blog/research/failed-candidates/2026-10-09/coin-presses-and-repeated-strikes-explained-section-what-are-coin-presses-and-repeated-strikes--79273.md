Coin presses use massive force to shape the metal. This pressure is what forms the coin. The tool applies intense force to the silver. This process sets the metal into its final form.

Repeated strikes keep the metal flowing smoothly. Each strike builds the coin's shape. The die plan guides the pattern. This plan shows you exactly how the coin will look.

The number of strikes matters for quality. Too few strikes cause weak shapes. Too many strikes can create flaws. Proper striking minimizes these flaws. Good striking keeps the coin strong.
