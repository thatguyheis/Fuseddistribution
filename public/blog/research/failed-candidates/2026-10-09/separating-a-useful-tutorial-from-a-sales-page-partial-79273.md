## The Free Way to Start Getting Real Google Reviews

Your Google Business Profile is your first stop. It’s free, and most people set it up wrong. This is the starting point. Make sure your business name, address, and phone number are 100% correct. If you miss a step, you’re losing customers right now.

Don't just wait for people to find you. You need to get reviews where local people are looking. Stop asking for a generic review. Ask specific questions. For example, after a plumbing job, ask the customer what they thought about the repair time. Specific feedback is better. It tells Google exactly what you do well.

You gotta reply to every review. Whether it’s five stars or four stars, respond. A quick thank you shows you care. It also tells Google you’re a real, active business. This builds trust with potential customers. Steady, honest responses matter when people are choosing a local service.

More reviews mean you show up more often in local searches. A few good reviews help you beat out the competition. Focus on getting those specific, helpful comments. That’s how you start seeing more people call your business.
