## Search Intent
The user wants a clear, unbiased method for comparing the pricing and premiums offered by different silver dealers.

## Best Angle
Show the reader the math behind the premiums so they can evaluate value directly, eliminating the need to trust a single dealer's narrative.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on the general concept of silver value. They fail to provide a practical, step-by-step framework for calculating and comparing the actual cost of silver across various dealer quotes. They do not address the specific, often hidden, costs of premiums and fees.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Understand that premiums are not fixed. They change based on the metal type, storage location, and dealer volume.
*   Compare the cost per ounce
