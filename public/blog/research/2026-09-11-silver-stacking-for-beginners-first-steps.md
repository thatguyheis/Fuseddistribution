## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Define Your Goal:** Decide if your goal is immediate price stability or long-term preservation. This shapes your initial purchase size.
*   **Focus on Purity:** Look only for certified purity (99.99% or higher). Avoid unverified sources to prevent future confusion.
*   **Start Small:** Begin with a manageable amount. Test the buying process and understand the market flow before committing significant capital.
*   **Understand Premiums:** Learn how premiums affect the final cost. Knowing this prevents being surprised by dealer markups.
*   **Storage Matters:** Identify secure, climate-controlled storage options before you buy. Protect your asset from environmental factors.
