## Search Intent
The user wants a clear, unbiased comparison of the benefits and drawbacks of holding 100 oz silver bars versus other investment options.

## Best Angle
Focus on demystifying the cost structure and storage logistics, showing the reader how to manage the physical asset without relying on dealer advice.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on market predictions or speculative value. They miss the practical, real-world concerns of physical ownership, such as managing premiums, understanding storage requirements, and navigating the trust issues with dealers.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   The cost of a physical bar is determined by the spot price and the dealer's premium, which varies widely.
*   Storage costs (insurance, security, location) are a significant, often overlooked expense for physical bullion.
*   100 oz bars offer diversification away from traditional financial assets, but they are not guaranteed portfolio protection.
*   The actual value of the metal is separate from the cost of acquisition and storage.
*   Understanding the supply chain and storage methods is more important
