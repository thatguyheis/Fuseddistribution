## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Use a high-resolution camera setup. Aim for at least 12 megapixels for clear detail.
*   Employ controlled, diffused lighting. Avoid harsh shadows that obscure coin details.
*   Utilize a scale reference. Include a ruler or a known object in the frame for accurate size comparison.
*   Ensure a neutral, dark background. This minimizes distractions and makes the silver stand out clearly.
*   Take multiple angles. Capture the coin from different perspectives for a complete inventory record.
