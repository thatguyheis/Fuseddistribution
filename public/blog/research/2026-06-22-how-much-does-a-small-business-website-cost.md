## Search Intent

## Best Angle

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about website design or SEO theory. They fail to address the immediate, tangible problem: visibility and phone calls. They avoid telling you exactly what to check on Google Business Profile without sounding like a salesperson.

## Key Points
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free. Most local businesses set it up wrong, which keeps you invisible.
*   People search for "plumber near me," not "best plumbing services." Your profile needs to match those exact searches.
*   Reviews are your new website. If you aren't actively getting them, you're losing customers to competitors.
*   Your website isn't just a brochure; it needs to be set up to capture phone calls instantly from search results.
*   Stop worrying about complex SEO jargon. Focus only on showing up when someone searches for your service in your area.
