## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable explanation of NAP consistency and how fixing it directly impacts their local search visibility and calls.

## Best Angle
NAP consistency is the single most important, free step you can take right now to stop being invisible to Google and start getting local calls.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles define NAP consistency academically, focusing on theory rather than the practical, immediate consequence of inconsistent data on local ranking and citation building. They miss explaining exactly how small inconsistencies destroy trust and visibility for local businesses.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **NAP is a Trust Signal:** Google uses NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data to verify your business's legitimacy. Inconsistency tells Google your business is unreliable.
*   **Citation Impact:** Inconsistent data across directories (Yelp, Facebook, etc.) dilutes the authority of your business, making it harder to rank locally.
*   **Local Search Penalty:** When data conflicts, Google struggles to determine which location is the correct one, resulting in poor visibility for your specific city or service area.
*   **Review Linkage:** Inconsistent NAP
