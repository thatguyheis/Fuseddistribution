## Search Intent
Local businesses want a simple, actionable way to capture customer contact details at the exact moment a customer is making a purchase or interacting with them.

## Best Angle
Stop hoping customers call you. Show them how to get their phone number or email right when they buy something, turning a transaction into a lead.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on complex CRM software or big, expensive POS systems. They miss the simple, low-tech, and immediate ways local service businesses can collect contact info right now, which is what they actually need.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **The Immediate Ask:** Train staff to ask for an email or phone number *before* finalizing a sale, framing it as a way to send a follow-up tip or offer.
*   **The Receipt Trick:** Design your receipt (digital or paper) to include a clear, single field for "Leave a Note/Email" rather than just a thank you message.
*   **Simple QR Codes:** Use a simple QR code linked directly to a mobile form asking for contact info immediately after a service completion.
*   **POS Integration Check:** Ensure your current Point of Sale system can easily log this captured data and send it to your email or contact list automatically.
*   **The Value Exchange:** Clearly state *why* you need the info (e.g., "We want to send you a discount code for your next service").
