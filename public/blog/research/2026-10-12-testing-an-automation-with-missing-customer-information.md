## Content Brief: testing an automation with missing customer information

**Search Intent**
How do I test if an automation process I set up is actually working when I don't have the right customer data?

**Best Angle**
Stop wasting time testing fancy automation. Here’s the simple, real-world checklist to see if your system is actually capturing the leads you need.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus on the technical setup of the automation itself. They miss the crucial, practical reality: the data gaps that break the system in a real local business scenario. They don't show the common mistakes business owners make when testing without knowing what data is actually missing.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Point 1:** Test automation based on *action*, not just *data*. If the automation doesn't trigger the desired next step (like a call), the data quality is irrelevant.
*   **Point 2:** Focus on the "hand-off." The biggest failure point is the moment the automation tries to move a lead to a sales agent without the necessary contact details.
*   **Point 3:** Before testing, map the exact data fields required for the next step. If the automation skips a field, it’s a data gap, not a software bug.
*   **Point 4:** Use manual checks alongside automated runs. Have a team member manually review 5 failed automation attempts to see *exactly* where the data breaks down.
*   **Point 5:** Treat missing customer info as a process flaw, not a technology flaw. Fix the data collection point first before you fix the testing script.
