## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable plan to get more local phone calls through their website.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting money on fancy websites. Focus on the one thing Google actually uses to find you: your local presence.

## Competitor Gap
Most advice is too technical or promises impossible rankings. They talk about complex SEO when the owner just wants to know how to set up their Google Business Profile correctly today.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is your free storefront. If it’s not set up right, you are invisible on Google Maps and Search. [VERIFY]
*   Review management is a direct revenue stream. Focus on asking happy customers for reviews, not chasing vanity metrics.
*   Local search is simple: Search Term + Your City = Visibility. If you aren't showing up for "plumber near me," you aren't working.
*   A website without a clear call-to-action (phone number) is just an online brochure. It’s not a sales tool.
*   Stop worrying about complex rankings. Start focusing on showing up for the exact phrases your customers type into Google.
