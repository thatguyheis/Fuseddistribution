## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Define Your Need:** Determine if you need small accumulation or a larger reserve. Start small to understand the process.
*   **Source Vetting:** Focus on transparency. Look for suppliers who show clear pricing structures, not hidden markups.
*   **Cost Clarity:** Compare bar versus coin costs. Understand the difference between the spot price and any associated premiums.
*   **Storage First:** Before buying, plan where you will keep your silver. Secure, dry, and safe storage is the first practical step.
*   **Actionable Next Move:** Identify the first small purchase you can make this week. Start building your stack now.
