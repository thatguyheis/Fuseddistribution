## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Mobile First is Non-Negotiable:** Test your site on a phone. If it lags on mobile, Google ignores it. [VERIFY]
*   **Image Compression is Key:** Large photos slow everything down. Compress images before uploading them to your site.
*   **Minimize Bloatware:** Remove unnecessary plugins or large scripts. Every extra line of code slows down the load time.
*   **Optimize for Local Keywords:** Use your city and service area naturally on your homepage and service pages.
*   **Profile Consistency:** Ensure the address, hours, and services listed on your website match *exactly* what is on your Google Business Profile.
