## Search Intent
The user wants to know the exact, simple steps needed to optimize their Google Business Profile to attract local customers and calls.

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on website design. Focus on fixing your Google Business Profile first, because that is the only place local customers actually find you.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on general SEO theory or complex technical setup. They miss the crucial, practical steps for optimizing the GBP for immediate local results. They fail to explain the direct link between GBP details and actual phone calls.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free and is the single most important tool for local visibility.
*   Optimize your service area precisely. Don't just list your city; define the exact neighborhoods and towns you serve.
*   Reviews are the single biggest ranking factor for local search results. Focus on getting recent, specific reviews.
*   Use your GBP posts to show what you do today, not just what you used to do.
*   Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) are 100% consistent across every online platform.
