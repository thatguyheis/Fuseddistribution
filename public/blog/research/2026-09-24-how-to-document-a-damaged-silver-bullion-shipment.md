## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Inspect Before Acceptance:** Note all visible damage (cracks, dents) before signing the receiving manifest.
*   **Photo Evidence is Key:** Take clear, dated photos of all damaged items and the packaging immediately.
*   **Use Manifest Forms:** Complete any provided shipment forms precisely. Do not skip fields.
*   **Quantify the Loss:** Record the exact weight and description of every damaged item.
*   **Secure Copies:** Keep a physical and digital copy of the complete documentation file.
