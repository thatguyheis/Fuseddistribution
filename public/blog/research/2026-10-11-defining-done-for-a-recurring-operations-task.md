**Search Intent**
The user wants a clear, actionable, step-by-step method to improve their visibility on Google Maps and Search so that local customers can call their business directly.

**Best Angle**
Stop focusing on fancy websites. Focus on the single, free tool that brings customers to your door: your Google Business Profile.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus on *what* a GBP is, or list generic tips. They fail to show the specific, non-jargon steps a local business owner needs to take *today* to actually see more calls, often missing the crucial difference between a profile and a marketing vanity project.

*   **Accurate NAP Consistency:** Stress the critical importance of ensuring your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across every online listing to build trust with Google.
*   **Use the "Services" Section:** Show how to list specific services clearly, not just general descriptions, so searchers find you for exactly what you offer.
*   **Respond to Every Review:** Explain that engaging with reviews directly impacts local search ranking and shows Google you are active.
*   **Post Updates Regularly:** Advise on using the "Posts" feature for quick updates, offers, or new hours—this is low-effort, high-impact content.
