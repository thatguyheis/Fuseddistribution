## Content Brief: Short Form Video for Local Business Complete Guide

**Search Intent**
The user wants a simple, actionable step-by-step video guide on how to create and use short-form video content specifically to help their local business get found on Google.

**Best Angle**
Stop wasting time on fancy ads. Learn the 3 simple video moves that get local customers calling your service today.

**Competitor Gap**
Most guides focus on complex editing software or broad social media strategies. They miss the crucial link between short-form video and the specific, actionable steps needed to optimize for local search visibility (like Google Business Profile integration). They fail to show how to use video to directly solve the "I don't get found" pain point immediately.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Focus on Service, Not Sales:** Show a contractor finishing a job or a salon interior, not just a product pitch. This builds trust instantly.
*   **Geo-Tag Everything:** Ensure your videos clearly show your location or service area so Google knows exactly who to show them to.
*   **Use Local Keywords in Captions & Subtitles:** Don't just talk about your service; explicitly mention your city and the specific service you offer in the text overlay.
*   **Repurpose, Don't Recreate:** Take one strong video idea and chop it into 5-10 short clips. Use the same core message across different platforms (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).
*   **Direct Link in Bio/Video Description:** Every short video must end with a clear instruction: "Call us now" or "Click the link to book."
