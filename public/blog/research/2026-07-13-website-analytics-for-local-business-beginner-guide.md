## Key Points
*   **Focus on Local Search Signals:** Understand that analytics should prioritize metrics related to local search (e.g., map clicks, searches originating from local keywords) over general website traffic volume. [VERIFY]
*   **Identify Conversion Points:** Pinpoint exactly where users leave your site (e.g., contact form abandonment) to see if your website structure is failing to capture local leads.
*   **Map Traffic to Calls:** Learn how to connect website sessions to actual phone calls or form submissions to prove the website's direct impact on local business revenue. [VERIFY]
*   **Google Business Profile Correlation:** Use analytics to see if users who interact with your website are also engaging with your Google Business Profile, or vice versa.
*   **Actionable Insights, Not Reports:** Stop reading charts. Focus only on the one thing you can change this week based on the data (e.g., improving the clarity of your service page).
