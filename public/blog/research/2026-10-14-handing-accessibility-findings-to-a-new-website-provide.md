## Content Brief

**Search Intent**
The user wants to know the specific, structured process and best practices for effectively delivering accessibility findings to a new website provider.

**Best Angle**
Focus on providing a clear, actionable checklist and communication template that ensures the new provider understands the technical requirements of accessibility standards, minimizing back-and-forth.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus either broadly on accessibility basics or on the technical audit itself. Few provide a specific, step-by-step framework for the *handover* phase—the crucial communication bridge between the auditing team and the development team.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Define the Target Standard:** Clearly state which specific WCAG version (e.g., 2.1 AA) the new provider must adhere to, as this dictates the scope of the findings.
*   **Prioritize Findings by Severity:** Structure the handover document to clearly categorize findings into Critical, Serious, and Minor issues, allowing the provider to triage their remediation efforts effectively.
*   **Provide Contextual Examples:** For every finding, include specific, reproducible screenshots or video clips demonstrating the issue, rather than just abstract error codes.
*   **Include Actionable Recommendations:** Do not just report the problem; for each finding, provide a suggested technical solution or code snippet to guide the provider's development team.
*   **Establish a Feedback Loop Protocol:** Define the expected cadence and format for the provider to submit their remediation plans, ensuring the audit process is a collaborative cycle, not a one-time delivery.
