## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable guide on how to effectively use Instagram to find local customers.

## Best Angle
Show a quick, step-by-step process for setting up Instagram to attract local customers, focusing only on what actually drives calls and visits.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on vanity metrics like likes or follower counts. They miss the crucial step: using Instagram to directly prompt a phone call or a physical visit. They don't address the fear that social media is too complicated or time-consuming for a busy local owner.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Location Tags:** Always tag your actual service area or city in every post. This tells the algorithm exactly who to show your content to. [VERIFY]
*   **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Post photos or short videos of your work *in action* at a local job site or with a local client. This builds immediate trust over text.
*   **Use the Right Calls to Action:** Don't just ask for a "follow." Ask for a specific action: "Call this number to book your consultation," or "DM us for a quick quote."
*   **Respond Fast:** Treat DMs and comments like potential calls. A quick, personalized response proves you're local and responsive.
*   **Consistency Trumps Volume:** Post 2-3 times a week consistently, rather than trying to post daily and burning out. Quality over quantity when you’re busy.
