## Key Points
*   **Focus on Proximity:** Google Ads for local businesses works best when you target people searching *right now* for your specific service in your immediate area.
*   **Cost vs. Conversion:** Don't focus on clicks. Focus on the cost per *qualified lead* (a call or quote request), not just the click itself.
*   **Profile First:** Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free and is the foundation. Ads work much better when you have a solid, verified profile to point to.
*   **Start Small and Test:** Don't throw a big budget at it. Test a small, focused campaign to see what services actually generate calls for you before committing more.
*   **Direct Call Tracking:** Set up tracking so you know exactly which ad spend translates into actual phone conversations, not just website visits.
