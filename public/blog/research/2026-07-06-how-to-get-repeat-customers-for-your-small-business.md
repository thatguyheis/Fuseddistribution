## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable plan to get my local business showing up on Google so I get more calls.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on fancy marketing. Focus only on the three things that actually get customers to call you right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about SEO theory or complex technical setups. They fail to show the exact, step-by-step setup for the Google Business Profile and how to use local reviews to drive immediate foot traffic or service bookings. They promise big changes without showing the small, concrete actions needed today.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is your free storefront.** Set up your hours, services, and photos correctly.
*   **Reviews are your new website.** Focus on asking happy customers for reviews, not just getting them.
*   **Local searches are simple.** Make sure your name, address, and phone number match exactly across the web.
*   **Show up where people search.** You need to be visible on Google Maps when someone searches for your service near you.
*   **Don't ignore the basics.** A clear, simple website tells people what you do. If it doesn't get calls, it's not working.
