## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Inspect Immediately:** Document all physical damage, including cracks, dents, or corrosion, before moving the shipment.
*   **Photography is Key:** Take clear, timestamped photos of every damaged area. Use a ruler for scale reference.
*   **Detailed Inventory:** Create a manifest listing the quantity and specific nature of the damage for each unit.
*   **Contact Carrier First:** Notify the shipping company immediately regarding the condition upon arrival.
*   **Consult Experts:** Seek advice on formal documentation if you are pursuing insurance or a claim against the supplier.
