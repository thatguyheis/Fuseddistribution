## Key Points
*   **Direct Review Ask:** Don't just ask for a review; give them a specific, easy-to-follow instruction on *how* to leave a review on Google (link included).
*   **Personalized Value:** Include a small, specific reference to their service or interaction to show you paid attention, not just sending a template.
*   **The "Next Step" Focus:** The card should always lead to a clear, immediate next step (e.g., "Book your next service here," or "Check out our latest trade guide").
*   **Low-Friction Delivery:** Ensure the card is tangible and delivered promptly. Slow or flimsy cards signal a lack of professionalism.
*   **Track the Conversion:** Measure how many cards lead to actual Google reviews or new service bookings to prove the system works for your business.
