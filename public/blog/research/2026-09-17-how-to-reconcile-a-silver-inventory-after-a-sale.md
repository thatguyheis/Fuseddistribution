## Key Points
*   **Record Everything:** Document the exact purchase price, date, and weight for every piece of silver you acquire.
*   **Calculate True Cost:** Determine the actual cost basis for each ounce, accounting for any premiums or fees paid at the time of sale.
*   **Use Current Spot Rates:** Compare your recorded cost basis against the current market price, such as the $63.33/oz for silver as of 2026-09-17.
*   **Inventory Management:** Implement a simple system to track the location and quantity of your silver holdings.
*   **Understand Premium Impact:** Identify if any premiums paid significantly altered the final cost basis of your silver.
