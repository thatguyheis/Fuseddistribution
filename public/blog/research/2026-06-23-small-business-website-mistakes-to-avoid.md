## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable checklist to fix their website and local visibility so that potential customers actually call or show up to their business.

## Best Angle
Stop worrying about fancy SEO jargon. We’re going straight to the three things you can fix today to get noticed by people searching for your service right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on technical SEO tactics or vague advice about content. They miss the core problem: the disconnect between a website and local search visibility. They don't address the specific, low-effort, high-impact fixes for local service providers.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront:** Most owners set it up wrong—missing photos, wrong hours, or not responding to reviews. Fix this first. [VERIFY]
*   **The "Call-to-Action Gap":** Your website doesn't have one single, clear button telling people what to do next (Call Now, Book Appointment). If it’s not obvious, people leave.
*   **Local Keywords vs. General Words:** Stop trying to rank for "plumbing." Start using "plumber in [Your City]" or "emergency leak repair near me."
*   **Review System Fix:** Don't just ask for reviews. Set up an easy, automated system (even a simple text message prompt) to encourage happy customers to leave Google reviews.
*   **Website Speed & Mobile:** If your site takes more than three seconds to load on a phone, you are losing customers immediately. Make sure it works perfectly on mobile.
