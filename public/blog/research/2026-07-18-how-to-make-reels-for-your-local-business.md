## Content Brief: how to make reels for your local business

**Search Intent**
What do you want to know when you search "how to make reels for your local business"? You want a simple, step-by-step guide on creating short, local videos that actually get local customers.

**Best Angle**
Stop trying to make fancy, complicated videos. Focus on showing your local customers *exactly* what you do and why they should call you, using simple phone-friendly video ideas.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus on fancy editing software or high-production value. They miss the point that local businesses need short, authentic videos that solve an immediate customer problem (e.g., "Here's how I fix leaky pipes," not a 5-minute tour). They don't address the fear that they can't make it look professional.

**Topic Contract**
*   **Slug:** how-make-local-business-reels
*   **SEO Title:** How to Make Local Business Reels That Get Customers
*   **Search Intent:** To learn practical, no-fluff methods for creating short video content for local marketing.
*   **H2 Plan:** 1. Why Reels Work for Local Trades/Services. 2. The Simple 3-Step Reel Formula. 3. What to Film: Real Service Examples. 4. Quick Tips for Local Growth Reels.
*   **Core Topic Terms:** local business reels, making reels for local business, short video marketing, local service promotion, Google Business Profile video.
*   **Forbidden Drift Topics:** Advanced video editing software tutorials, complex algorithm changes, national social media trends.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Show the "Before & After":** Film a quick clip of a common local problem (e.g., a messy job site, a customer's frustrated face) followed immediately by your clean, successful solution.
*   **Use Voiceover, Not Just Text:** Keep your script short. Talk directly to the camera like you're talking to a neighbor. This builds trust faster than fancy graphics.
*   **Focus on the "Why," Not the "What":** Don't just show you paint. Show the *result*—a beautiful, finished room. Show the *feeling*—the relief of having a problem solved.
*   **Use Local Tags in Your Reels:** Tag your city, neighborhood, or specific local landmarks in the video. This helps people searching locally find you instantly.
*   **Repurpose Your Best Work:** Take a successful customer testimonial or a quick "day in the life" of your business and turn it into a reel. It’s authentic and shows you’re real.
