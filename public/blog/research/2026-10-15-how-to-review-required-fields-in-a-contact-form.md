## Search Intent
A small business owner searching this keyword wants a clear, step-by-step guide on the specific methods and tools to ensure their contact form collects all necessary, required information from potential customers.

## Best Angle
Focus on a practical, troubleshooting approach, showing exactly *why* certain fields are required and how to configure the form settings to enforce that requirement effectively.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus only on *what* required fields are, but they fail to provide specific, actionable solutions for common form builders (e.g., Mailchimp, HubSpot, Gravity Forms) on *how* to implement the validation rules for those fields. They often miss the technical configuration details needed to make the requirement mandatory for the end-user experience.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Define Necessity First:** Before setting a field as required, clearly document *why* that piece of information is essential for your business process (e.g., "We require an email address for follow-up").
*   **Implement Builder-Specific Logic:** Understand the unique settings within your form platform (e.g., Gravity Forms, Typeform) to set the field level requirement, as this is the primary mechanism for enforcement.
*   **Test the User Journey:** Always test the form from the perspective of a visitor to confirm that the system correctly prevents submission if a required field is left blank.
*   **Differentiate Required vs. Optional:** Clearly label fields to distinguish between information that *must* be provided and supplementary data that is welcome but not essential for the initial contact.
*   **Review for Redundancy:** Audit your required fields to ensure you aren't asking for the same data in multiple places, which increases friction and reduces the likelihood of a complete submission.
