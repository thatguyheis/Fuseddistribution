## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile is Key:** Your free Google Business Profile is the first place people find you; set up call notifications directly here. [VERIFY]
*   **Use Simple Forms:** Don't build a complex system. Use a basic contact form that automatically routes to a specific phone number or triggers a call notification.
*   **Focus on Mobile:** Most local searches happen on phones. Ensure your call tracking feature works flawlessly on mobile devices.
*   **Define Your Trigger:** Decide *exactly* what action triggers the tracking—is it a form submission, a button click, or a direct phone number click?
*   **Review the Process:** Check that the tracking data flows clearly. If you can't see the call logged, the system isn't working, regardless of how good your website looks.
