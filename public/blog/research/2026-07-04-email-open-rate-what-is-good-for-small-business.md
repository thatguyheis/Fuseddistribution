## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront:** Set up your profile completely—photos, hours, services—in the next 30 minutes.
*   **Review strategy beats website copy:** Focus 80% of your energy on getting local customers to leave reviews on Google first.
*   **Local keywords matter:** Stop optimizing for broad terms. Focus on "Plumber in [City Name]" or "Salon near [Neighborhood]."
*   **Website isn't just a brochure:** Ensure your site clearly states *who* you serve and *where* you serve. It needs to match what you tell Google.
*   **The connection:** A strong GBP profile signals to Google that you are a legitimate, local business, which helps your website rankings too.
