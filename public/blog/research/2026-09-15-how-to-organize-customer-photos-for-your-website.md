## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable guide on how to properly organize customer photos for my website so that potential customers can easily find and trust my business.

## Best Angle
Stop uploading random pictures. Show the *right* photos that prove you do the job and build trust, not just decoration.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on *what* photos to take (e.g., "take a picture of your tools"). They miss the crucial step of *organizing* them—how to tag, categorize, and structure them so Google and customers can actually use them to find you.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Proof, Not Pretty Pictures:** Use photos showing the *results* of your work (before/afters, finished projects) over generic stock images.
*   **Tag Everything:** Use descriptive, local keywords in your photo tags (e.g., "Plumbing repair [City Name]", "Salon cut before and after").
*   **Create Clear Categories:** Group photos logically (e.g., "Before & Afters," "Teamwork," "Client Testimonials"). This makes navigation easy for visitors.
*   **Optimize for Mobile:** Ensure your organized photo gallery loads fast on a phone, as most local searches happen on mobile.
*   **Consistency Builds Trust:** Keep the style of your photos consistent. A professional, organized look signals a professional business.
