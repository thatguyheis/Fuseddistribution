## Search Intent
They want to know exactly which words and phrases they should target to get calls from people searching for their specific service in their local area.

## Best Angle
Stop guessing what customers search for. Learn the simple, step-by-step process for finding the exact local keywords that drive calls for your business.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on general SEO theory or complex tools. They miss the practical, local-specific application of keyword research—how to find the low-competition, high-intent terms specific to their neighborhood or trade.

The gap is practical application. They don't teach you how to use local intent to win local search, only how to fill out a form.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your local keywords are not just your city name. They are the specific services and problems your customers are typing into Google right now.
*   Focus on "near me" intent. People searching for local services are looking for immediate solutions, not long-form blog posts.
*   Use your competitors' actual service descriptions to find gaps. See what keywords your successful local rivals are ranking for.
*   Don't chase volume. Target long-tail, specific phrases (e.g., "emergency HVAC repair downtown Austin") over broad terms (e.g., "HVAC").
