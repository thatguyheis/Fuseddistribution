## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable guide on how to get my local business found on Google without feeling overwhelmed by complex marketing jargon.

## Best Angle
Stop trying to build a fancy website. Focus 80% of your energy on the simple, free tools that actually bring local customers to your phone.

## Competitor Gap
Most guides focus on website design or complex SEO jargon. They fail to address the immediate, tangible problem: how to get calls *today* using the free tools available to local service providers. They skip the practical, step-by-step execution that a busy contractor needs.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront. If it's not 100% complete, you are invisible to local searches.
*   Reviews are the new website. Focus on getting consistent, direct reviews from happy customers, not just generic praise.
*   Google Maps visibility is more important than ranking on the map. Ensure your service area is perfectly defined in your profile.
*   Use simple, local keywords in your service descriptions. Don't just list services; list *where* you work.
*   Don't chase vanity metrics. Track how many calls or service inquiries came directly from Google Search or Maps.
