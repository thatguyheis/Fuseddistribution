## Key Points

*   Sulfur is common in industrial processes and certain atmospheric conditions.
*   The specific type of tarnish depends heavily on the silver's purity level.
*   Proper storage, especially regarding humidity and air circulation, is critical.
*   Cleaning methods should be gentle. Harsh chemicals can damage the metal surface.
