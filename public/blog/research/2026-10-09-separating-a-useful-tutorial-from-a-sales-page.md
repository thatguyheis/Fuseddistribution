**Keyword/Title Candidate:** Getting Google Reviews

## Search Intent
What specific, actionable steps does a local business owner need to take *right now* to start getting genuine reviews on Google?

## Best Angle
Stop chasing fancy SEO. Focus 80% of your energy on this single, free tool that directly impacts how local customers find you.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles just talk about "getting reviews." They fail to address the skepticism of local owners—specifically, they don't explain the difference between fake reviews and real customer feedback, nor do they show the exact, low-effort setup needed for a business owner who isn't a marketer.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your #1 priority:** It's free, and it’s the first place customers look. Set up your profile correctly—this is the foundation.
*   **Focus on the Right Platform:** Don't just ask for reviews everywhere. Focus on getting reviews directly through your service area (e.g., asking satisfied customers to leave feedback after a service completion).
*   **The Power of Specificity:** Instead of asking, "Leave us a review," ask for a specific type of feedback. "We'd love to know what you thought about the repair speed." This encourages better, more useful input.
*   **Respond to Everything:** Reply to every review, positive and negative. Showing you care builds trust, and it signals to Google that you are an active, responsible local business.
*   **Reviews Drive Visibility:** A steady stream of 5-star reviews directly affects your local search ranking and how people decide to choose you over the competition.
