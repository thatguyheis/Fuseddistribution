## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable guide on how frequently they should post on social media to get more local business visibility.

## Best Angle
Stop posting daily. Focus on the one thing that actually drives calls: optimizing your Google Business Profile.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on vanity metrics like likes or follower counts. They fail to connect social activity directly to tangible actions, like showing up in a local search result. They never address the time constraint of a busy service owner.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile is your #1 priority.** Treat it like your physical storefront.
*   **Consistency beats volume.** One strong, optimized profile update per week is better than five rushed posts a week.
*   **Focus on local proof, not promotion.** Post photos of your work, customer testimonials, or behind-the-scenes—not just sales pitches.
*   **Use social to drive traffic to your main goal.** Every post should have a clear path to "Call Now" or "Book Appointment."
*   **Time is scarce.** Pick one platform where your ideal client hangs out and focus your energy there, and ignore the noise elsewhere.
