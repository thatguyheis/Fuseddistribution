## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Marking/Stamps:** Check for official mint marks. Look for consistent, sharp engraving.
*   **Weight and Density:** Compare the weight to known standards for that specific denomination.
*   **Surface Quality:** Genuine silver shows specific luster. Fakes often have dull, uneven, or overly polished surfaces.
*   **Alloy Indicators:** Be wary of unusual color variations or mixed metals that don't match the expected silver composition.
*   **Source Verification:** Buy from established distributors who provide documentation, not just a single vendor promise.
