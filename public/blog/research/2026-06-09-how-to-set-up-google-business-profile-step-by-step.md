## Search Intent
The user wants a clear, actionable, step-by-step guide to successfully create and optimize a Google Business Profile to improve local visibility and attract customers.

## Best Angle
Focus on the immediate, tangible benefits of a complete profile—specifically how optimizing the profile directly impacts local foot traffic and phone calls, rather than just focusing on the technical setup.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus heavily on the technical setup (filling out forms) but fail to address the crucial, often overlooked step of ongoing optimization, including the strategic use of photos, service descriptions, and managing reviews to maximize local SEO impact. They often neglect the "after setup" strategy necessary for growth.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Verify Business Category Selection:** Choose the most specific primary category that accurately reflects your core service, as this heavily influences local search ranking.
*   **Optimize for Local Keywords:** Integrate your primary service keywords naturally into the business description and service listings to signal relevance to search engines.
*   **Utilize High-Quality Visuals:** Upload professional photos of your storefront, products, and team; visual content significantly increases engagement rates on the profile.
*   **Implement Consistent NAP:** Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across the Google Business Profile and all other online directories to build trust.
*   **Proactive Review Management:** Establish a system for politely requesting and responding to customer reviews, as this is a key ranking factor for local businesses.
