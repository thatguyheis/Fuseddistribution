## Search Intent
What is the simplest, most direct way for a local business to start getting found on Google searches?

## Best Angle
Stop worrying about complex SEO. Focus only on the three things that actually move customers: your Google Business Profile, local reviews, and making sure your website talks to Google.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about technical SEO or broad content strategies. They fail to address the immediate, tangible actions local owners need to take *today* to see calls and shows up in local maps. They skip the practical, no-fluff setup steps.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) First:** Treat your GBP like your storefront. Ensure your hours, photos, and service areas are 100% accurate. [VERIFY]
*   **Review Strategy Over Everything Else:** Focus on getting 5-star reviews specifically mentioning your service and location. This is the #1 local ranking signal. [VERIFY]
*   **Local Keywords, Not Big Words:** Use the exact phrases customers use (e.g., "Plumber in [Your City]") on your website and GBP, not vague terms.
*   **Website Action:** Make sure your website has one clear, easy-to-find "Call Now" button visible on every page. Nothing else matters for the first 30 seconds.
*   **Consistency Beats Volume:** Showing up consistently in local searches is more important than posting daily on social media. Focus energy on showing up where customers search.
