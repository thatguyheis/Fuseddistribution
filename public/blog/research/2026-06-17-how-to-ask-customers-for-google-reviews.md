## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable step-by-step guide on how to effectively ask customers for Google reviews to improve their local search visibility.

## Best Angle
Stop trying to trick Google. Focus only on making it easy for your happy customers to leave a review.

## Competitor Gap
Most guides focus on the technical setup of the Google Business Profile. They miss the human element: the actual script, the timing, and handling the response. They don't address the fear or hesitation local business owners have about asking for feedback.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Make it easy, don't make it a task.** Send a simple, direct text or email link immediately after a positive service completion, not a generic follow-up weeks later.
*   **Target the right platform.** Don't just ask for a review. Ask for a review directly tied to the specific service they just received (e.g., "If you enjoyed your haircut, leave a quick note on Google").
*   **Use a simple, low-friction ask.** Instead of a long survey, use a short, direct link that goes straight to the Google review prompt.
*   **Respond to every single review.** Thank them publicly and privately. This shows you care and signals to Google that you are an engaged business.
*   **Track what works.** See which methods (text, email, in-person) get the most responses. Stop wasting time on methods that don't generate feedback.
