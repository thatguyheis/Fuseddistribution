## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable answer on whether focusing on email marketing or social media is the best immediate step to get more customers.

## Best Angle
Stop choosing between email and social. You need to know which one drives actual calls *right now* for your local service.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles treat email and social as separate, competing priorities. They fail to explain how to use one channel to directly feed the other for local lead generation. They don't focus on the immediate ROI for a service-based business owner.

## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is the foundation:** If you don't optimize your GBP first, neither email nor social will solve your immediate problem. [VERIFY]
*   **Email is for retention, not initial discovery:** Use email to nurture existing customers, not to find the first new client.
*   **Social is for visibility, not conversion:** Don't post random updates. Post content that answers a direct local need (e.g., "How to fix X problem in your area"). [VERIFY]
*   **The "Call to Action" must be local:** Every piece of content, email, and social post must lead to a clear, immediate local action (a call, a visit, a quote request).
*   **Measure what matters:** Stop tracking likes. Start tracking how many inquiries come from your online activity.
