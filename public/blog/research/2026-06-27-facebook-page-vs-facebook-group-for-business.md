## Competitor Gap

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Facebook Page:** Best for a professional storefront. It's where you look official and direct people to your main booking/contact info.
*   **Facebook Group:** Best for building community and getting direct feedback. Great for service businesses needing local tips or quick Q&A.
*   **Local Search Focus:** A Page works better when you want to be found on Google Maps. Groups are harder to rank directly for "near me" searches.
*   **The Real Goal:** Don't just post. Use the Page to drive traffic to a simple contact form, and use the Group for building trust *after* they find you.
*   **Actionable Check:** If your main goal is getting a phone call for a specific service, start with a clean Page setup.
