## Key Points
*   **Define Stacking Simply:** Silver stacking means systematically acquiring physical silver. It is about building a tangible asset base.
*   **Premium Clarity:** Understand that premiums are costs. Know exactly what you pay for the silver you receive.
*   **Asset Choice Matters:** Start small. Decide between bars, coins, or bullion based on your storage needs.
*   **Tracking is Essential:** Keep a simple log. Know what you own and where it is stored.
*   **Patience is Key:** Accumulation is a process. Focus on consistent, low-pressure steps rather than quick gains.
