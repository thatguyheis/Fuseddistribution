## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Audit for Content Gaps:** Identify pages that lack seasonal relevance (e.g., outdated statistics, seasonal calls-to-action) rather than focusing on rewriting everything.
*   **Adopt a Modular Content Framework:** Design content blocks (e.g., infographics, short guides, updated FAQs) that can be easily swapped out for seasonal versions.
*   **Prioritize "Thin" Updates:** Target small, high-visibility areas—like featured snippets or meta descriptions—first, as these offer quick wins without major page restructuring.
*   **Leverage Existing Assets:** Re-purpose older, relevant content by adding seasonal context, rather than creating entirely new pieces from scratch.
*   **Use Content Mapping:** Create a simple map showing which pages need which specific seasonal tweaks, ensuring targeted effort instead of blanket rewriting.
