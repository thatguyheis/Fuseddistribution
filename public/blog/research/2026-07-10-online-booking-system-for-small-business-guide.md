## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Simplicity:** Start with a system that requires minimal setup. Overly complex software wastes time and money.
*   **Check Local Features:** Ensure your chosen system supports local search visibility and easy integration with Google Business Profile.
*   **Test the Flow:** Before launching, test the entire booking path—from the customer clicking to you receiving the confirmation—to eliminate friction points.
*   **Prioritize Reviews:** Make sure the booking system automatically prompts customers for a review after service completion.
*   **Keep It Simple:** Don't try to build the perfect system. Pick a tool that handles the core function (scheduling) perfectly first.
