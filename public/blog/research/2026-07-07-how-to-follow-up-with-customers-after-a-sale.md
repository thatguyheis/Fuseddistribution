## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Be Immediate:** Send a personalized thank you within 24 hours. Don't wait for a generic email; reference the specific service they just bought.
*   **Ask for Input, Not Just a Review:** Instead of just asking for a review, ask a specific, low-effort question about their experience. This opens a dialogue.
*   **Map the Next Service:** Identify the logical next step for them. If they just bought a cleaning service, suggest a maintenance check-in in 30 days.
*   **Use Simple Channels:** Stick to phone calls or a simple text message for initial follow-ups. Avoid overwhelming them with complex messaging.
*   **Reward the Repeat:** Create a small incentive (e.g., a small discount code for their next service) to encourage the second purchase.
