## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable way to figure out who in their team is responsible for making sure their local search visibility is handled correctly.

## Best Angle
Stop blaming the website. Figure out exactly which person on your team owns the Google Business Profile and the local calls, and fix that one thing first.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about *what* a Google Business Profile is. They miss the practical, team-based accountability—the messy reality of assigning ownership so no one drops the ball when it comes to local visibility. They focus on the tool, not the human process.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **The Profile Owner:** Designate one person, even if it’s you initially, as the primary owner of the Google Business Profile setup and daily updates.
*   **The Review Manager:** Assign a specific person the task of monitoring and responding to customer reviews, linking it directly to the profile owner.
*   **The Visibility Checker:** Identify a secondary person responsible for weekly checks—ensuring NAP consistency across all online listings, not just your main profile.
*   **Clear Hand-off:** Document the exact steps for when the profile owner needs to hand off a task (like content updates) to the reviewer, making the process explicit.
*   **Simple Audit:** Create a one-page checklist for your team to review weekly, focusing only on the profile owner's tasks to ensure accountability remains clear.
