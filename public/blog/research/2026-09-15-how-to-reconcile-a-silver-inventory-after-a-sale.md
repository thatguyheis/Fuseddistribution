## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Document the exact purchase price and weight immediately. This is your starting point for reconciliation.
*   Calculate your initial cost basis per ounce. Use the live price data, e.g., at $62.99/oz as of 2026-09-15, to establish a baseline.
*   Create a simple ledger to track every transaction, including any premiums or fees paid.
*   Determine the current market value of your inventory. This helps you reconcile the difference between what you paid and what it is worth now.
*   Establish clear rules for future tracking. Decide how often you will review your silver inventory reconciliation process.
