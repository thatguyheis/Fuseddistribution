## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   The spot price is the true market value. It is the price you get from the primary exchange.
*   The premium is the additional cost added by the seller. This covers their operational expenses.
*   Premiums are not fixed. They change based on supply and demand dynamics.
*   A high premium means a lower spot price for you. This is the direct cost impact.
*   Understanding the premium helps you assess the true cost of entry into silver.
