## Search Intent
They want a simple, actionable way to stop bad Google reviews from hurting their business.

## Best Angle
Stop trying to fight negative reviews with fancy PR. Focus on a simple, direct response strategy that actually builds trust with local customers.

## Competitor Gap
Most advice focuses on "replying quickly." They miss the crucial step of using negative feedback as a chance to demonstrate your service quality to *new* potential customers. They also fail to connect review management directly to local search visibility.

## Key Points
*   **Respond to *all* reviews, good or bad.** Ignoring them signals to Google and customers that you don't care.
*   **Address the complaint publicly and professionally.** Don't get defensive. Show empathy and your solution.
*   **Turn negativity into a trust signal.** A thoughtful response proves you handle issues well, which is a huge differentiator.
*   **Use your response to subtly showcase your service.** Briefly mention how you fix things or what makes your service better than the competition.
*   **Focus on the *next* interaction.** End the response by inviting them to try your service or ask a specific question. [VERIFY]
