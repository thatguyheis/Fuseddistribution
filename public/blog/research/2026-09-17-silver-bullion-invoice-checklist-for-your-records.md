*   **Capture Transaction Details:** Record the exact date and time of every purchase.
*   **Specify Itemized Costs:** List the exact weight, purity (e.g., .999 fine), and price paid for each lot.
*   **Document Dealer Information:** Keep a clear record of the seller, their contact, and any specific terms agreed upon.
*   **Note Shipping and Handling:** Include costs associated with transport and insurance for every shipment.
*   **Maintain Secure Storage Records:** Link your checklist to where physical or digital proof of purchase is stored.
