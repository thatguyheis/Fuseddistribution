**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   Identify the packaging material: Determine if the packaging is PVC, paper, or plastic. PVC poses a significant chemical risk to silver over time.
*   Environmental control is key: Store packaging away from extreme temperature fluctuations and direct sunlight. Humidity control is crucial to prevent mold or paper degradation.
*   Use inert materials for secondary storage: When archiving, use acid-free, lignin-free boxes or archival sleeves. Avoid standard cardboard boxes that can off-gas.
*   Handling protocols: Always use clean, dry hands when moving the packaging. Avoid touching the coin or packaging with bare skin.
*   Regular inspection: Periodically check the packaging for signs of moisture, discoloration, or physical stress.
