## Key Points
*   **Manual Check:** Use browser developer tools (Inspect Element) to see the actual path the browser takes when clicking a link.
*   **Loop Detection:** Look for the same page appearing repeatedly in the URL bar after clicking a link, which indicates a redirect loop.
*   **404 vs. Redirect:** Understand the difference between a true broken link (404) and a redirect that isn't working correctly.
*   **The Fix:** Use a simple link checker tool or manually trace the path to ensure every important page points to the right place.
*   **Impact:** Fixing these chains directly improves user experience and ensures potential customers find your services.
