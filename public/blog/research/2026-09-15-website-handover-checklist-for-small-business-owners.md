## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile First:** Set up your Google Business Profile correctly. This is your free, most important local listing. Most owners set this up wrong, leading to zero local visibility. [VERIFY]
*   **Mobile-First Website Check:** Verify your website loads fast and looks perfect on a phone. If it doesn't work on mobile, you are losing local customers immediately.
*   **Local Keywords on Pages:** Ensure your main service pages clearly state *what* you do and *where* you do it (City/Service). Simple language wins over complex keywords.
*   **Review System Setup:** Create an easy, direct way for customers to leave reviews. Reviews are crucial for local search ranking and trust.
*   **Clear Call-to-Action:** Every page must have one clear instruction: Call us, Visit our site, or Book now. No confusion about what the next step is.
