## Key Points
*   **PPC is immediate:** It gets you calls fast, but the budget runs out when you stop spending. [VERIFY]
*   **SEO is long-term:** It builds authority, but it takes time—you won't see results overnight.
*   **Local focus matters:** For local businesses, optimizing your Google Business Profile (GBP) is often the fastest, cheapest organic win. [VERIFY]
*   **The hybrid approach wins:** The best results come from using PPC to test things out while SEO builds the foundation.
*   **Don't confuse traffic with leads:** Focus on keywords that lead directly to a phone call, not just website views.
