## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is Your Front Door:** Treat your GBP like a local listing, not just a directory entry. Claim and fully optimize it first. [VERIFY]
*   **Consistency Over Complexity:** Update your business hours, services, and photos weekly. Google rewards active profiles.
*   **NAP Consistency is Non-Negotiable:** Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across every single online platform. Inconsistency confuses Google.
*   **Reviews Drive Local Traffic:** Focus on asking happy customers for reviews. Reviews are the primary signal for local search ranking. [VERIFY]
*   **Make the Call Easy:** Ensure your contact form or phone number is immediately visible and functional on your website and GBP.
