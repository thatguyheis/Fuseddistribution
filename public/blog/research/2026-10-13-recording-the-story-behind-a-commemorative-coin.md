**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Design Phase:** The initial stage involves conceptualizing the historical or cultural event that the coin will commemorate, which directly dictates the narrative elements to be included.
*   **Die Engraving:** The story is physically transferred onto the coin through the process of die engraving, where the specific details of the event are meticulously etched or stamped into the metal blank.
*   **Material Selection:** The choice of metal, such as silver, dictates the durability and aesthetic quality of the final recording, impacting how the story is preserved for future generations.
*   **Quality Control:** Rigorous quality control ensures that the narrative elements recorded accurately reflect the intended historical or cultural message before the coin enters the production stream.
*   **Authentication:** The final recording process is validated by the minting authority to ensure the historical integrity of the narrative embedded in the coin.
