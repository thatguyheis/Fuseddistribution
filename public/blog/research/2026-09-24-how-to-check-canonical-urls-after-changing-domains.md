## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Check Your Code:** Use Google Search Console to see if Google is still pointing to the old URL or the new one.
*   **Test Direct Links:** Manually type in the new domain and the old domain to ensure both load correctly and point where you expect.
*   **Verify Search Console:** Look for any "URL Inspection" reports that flag a 404 or a redirect loop immediately after the change.
*   **Focus on User Experience:** Ensure your main business pages load fast and look normal for customers, not just for search engines.
*   **Don't Wait:** Check these steps within 24 hours of the domain switch to catch any immediate indexing issues.
