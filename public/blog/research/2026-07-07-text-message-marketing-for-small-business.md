## Key Points
*   **Permission is everything:** You must get explicit consent before texting anyone. This is the only way to avoid being blocked and seen as spam. [VERIFY]
*   **Focus on utility, not sales:** Use text messages to send quick, helpful updates (e.g., appointment reminders, special service offers) rather than just hard sales pitches. [VERIFY]
*   **Integrate with your existing system:** Don't start a new tool. Use a simple system that links text messages to your existing booking or customer management software. [VERIFY]
*   **Start small and test:** Begin by texting existing, happy customers about a new service or a limited-time offer. Measure the response rate, not just the number of texts sent. [VERIFY]
*   **Keep it human:** Your tone must sound like a helpful neighbor, not a corporation. Avoid overly formal language; be direct and friendly. [VERIFY]
