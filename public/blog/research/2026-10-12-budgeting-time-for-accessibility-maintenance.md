## Content Brief: Local SEO for Service Businesses

**Search Intent:** I want to know the simplest, most effective steps to improve my local visibility on Google so that potential customers can find my service.

**Best Angle:** Stop wasting time on complicated SEO jargon and focus on the three non-negotiable things that get local customers to call you right now.

**Competitor Gap:** Most articles either give vague advice about "optimizing your site" or focus too much on technical SEO. They fail to address the immediate, tangible actions a busy contractor or salon owner needs to take this week to start showing up in local search results.

**Key Points (5 bullets):**
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is your free storefront. Set up accurate hours, services, and photos *today*.
*   Reviews are your local ranking fuel. Systematically ask happy customers for reviews on Google and Yelp.
*   Consistency beats intensity. Showing up for local search every week is better than one big push.
*   Focus on what customers search for: "Plumber near me" or "Best dentist in [City]." Optimize your GBP answers for these exact phrases.
*   Ignore the noise. Don't chase vanity metrics like "impressions." Focus only on calls and shows up.
