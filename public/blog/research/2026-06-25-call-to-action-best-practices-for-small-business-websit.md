## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable checklist to fix my website so local customers actually call me.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on fancy website design and focus on the one thing Google cares about most: getting local customers to find you on Google Maps and search.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on technical SEO jargon or broad website audits. They miss the crucial, practical step: correctly optimizing your Google Business Profile and getting real customer reviews, which directly impacts calls. They don't show the simple, step-by-step fix for this immediate problem.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup is Free:** Most local businesses set this up wrong, missing crucial details that show up in local searches.
*   **Review Strategy is Key:** Focus less on complex marketing and more on getting consistent, genuine reviews in the right places (Google, Yelp, etc.).
*   **Local Keywords Beat Broad Terms:** You need to target searches like "plumber in [Your City]" not just "plumbing services."
*   **Website Action, Not Just Pages:** Your website needs one clear, easy-to-find button for calling, not just a pretty menu.
*   **Consistency Over Volume:** Showing up reliably on Google Maps is better than posting randomly on social media.
