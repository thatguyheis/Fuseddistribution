## Content Brief

**Keyword/Title Candidate:** how online booking reduces no-shows

**Search Intent**
The user wants a simple, actionable method to use online booking to lower the rate at which their customers miss appointments.

**Best Angle**
Stop losing money to missed appointments. Show you how setting up a simple online booking system cuts down on cancellations and lets you fill your schedule faster.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles talk about *setting up* booking software. They fail to show the direct, measurable link between a simple online booking process and the actual financial benefit of reducing no-shows for a local service business. They focus too much on the tech and not enough on the immediate cash flow impact.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Direct Cost Savings:** Detail the average cost of a single no-show (lost service time, wasted preparation, lost revenue). [VERIFY]
