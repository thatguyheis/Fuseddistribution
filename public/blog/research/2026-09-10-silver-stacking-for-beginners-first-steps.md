## Key Points
*   **Start Small:** Begin with a manageable quantity. Do not try to buy the largest amount immediately.
*   **Source Verification:** Check dealer legitimacy before committing funds. Look for transparency on pricing structures.
*   **Premium Clarity:** Understand the difference between the spot price and the premium you pay. Know what you are paying for.
*   **Storage Basics:** Research secure, climate-controlled storage options for your initial holdings.
*   **Plan Before Purchase:** Define your buying goal. This helps you decide between bars, coins, or other formats.
