## Search Intent
Local business owners want a simple, actionable way to get found on Google so customers can call them.

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on fancy website features and start focusing on the one thing that actually brings calls: your Google Business Profile.

## Competitor Gap
Most advice focuses on technical SEO jargon or complex website redesigns. They miss the simple, immediate fix that local businesses often overlook, which is optimizing their local listing and review system.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free, but most local businesses set it up wrong—it's the first place customers look.
*   People search for "plumber near me," not "best plumbing services." You need to match the search language.
*   Reviews are your direct line to customers. Focus on a simple, repeatable process for getting them.
*   A fast, mobile-friendly website isn't a luxury; it's the minimum standard for showing up on Google Maps.
*   Ignoring local search means you are invisible to the people actively looking for your service right now.
