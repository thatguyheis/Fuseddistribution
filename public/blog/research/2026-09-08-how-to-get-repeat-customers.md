## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable plan to get more local customers coming to my business through Google.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting money on fancy websites. Focus on the one thing that actually brings people to your door: getting listed correctly on Google Maps and getting real customer feedback.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about complex SEO strategies or expensive software. They miss the simple reality: if you aren't optimized for local search terms and reviews, you are invisible to the customers who are ready to hire you right now.

## Key Points
*   **Master Your Google Business Profile (GBP):** Treat your GBP like a storefront. Ensure your hours, services, and photos are 100% accurate. [VERIFY]
*   **Focus on Review Velocity:** Ask every happy customer for a review immediately after a service. Reviews are the #1 driver for local search visibility.
*   **Systematize Follow-Up:** Create a simple, non-intrusive system to follow up with past clients. This is how you build repeat business without constant selling.
*   **Address Feedback Loops:** Use the feedback you get from reviews to fix service gaps. Showing you listen proves you care, which encourages repeat visits.
*   **Leverage Referral Networks:** Make asking for referrals easy. A simple, direct ask at the end of a successful service is often more effective than any marketing post.
