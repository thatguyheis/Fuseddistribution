## Content Brief: evergreen content ideas for small business website

**Search Intent**
The user wants actionable, practical ideas on how to use content to improve their small business website and get found locally on Google.

**Best Angle**
Stop writing vague blog posts. Start creating website content that answers the exact questions your local customers are typing into Google right now.

**Competitor Gap**
Most articles focus on general content marketing theory or broad SEO strategies. They fail to provide concrete, low-effort, high-impact content ideas tailored specifically to service-based businesses that need to show up locally. They skip the practical "what to write next" part.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Answer "How-To" Questions:** Focus content on solving immediate customer problems (e.g., "How to fix a leaky faucet in [City Name]").
*   **Localize Everything:** Every piece of content must explicitly mention your service area, city, or neighborhood.
*   **Leverage Customer Language:** Write exactly how your local customers talk about their problems, not how you talk about your features.
*   **Repurpose Ruthlessly:** Turn one service explanation into 5 different pieces of content (a blog, a short video script, a Google post, a FAQ page).
*   **Focus on Trust Signals:** Content that shows expertise and local connection builds the trust needed for local calls.
