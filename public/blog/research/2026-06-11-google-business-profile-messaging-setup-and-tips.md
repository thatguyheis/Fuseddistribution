## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, step-by-step guide to setting up their Google Business Profile and getting more calls from local customers.

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on your website. Your Google Business Profile is the single most important thing for getting local calls.

## Competitor Gap
Most guides focus on general SEO theory or complex ranking factors. They miss the critical, actionable steps for optimizing the Profile itself. They don't address the frustration of setting it up correctly and seeing zero results.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile is free. It is the #1 way local customers find you, not your website.
*   Focus on filling out every section completely. Details like service areas and business hours are critical for local search.
*   Reviews are the most powerful ranking factor for local businesses. Getting reviews is more important than fancy website design.
*   Use your Profile to post updates about new services or promotions. This tells Google you are active and in business.
*   Ensure your business name and address are 100% accurate across all platforms. Inconsistencies kill local trust.
