## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Experience, Not Sales:** The best word-of-mouth happens when a customer has a great experience, not when you push a deal.
*   **Create a "Referral Trigger":** Design a specific moment (e.g., a follow-up call, a thank-you note) that prompts the satisfied customer to think about telling a friend.
*   **Make It Easy to Refer:** Don't make it complicated. Give your customers a simple, pre-written script or a quick email template they can copy/paste to share.
*   **Systematize the Ask:** Instead of hoping for referrals, schedule a quick, genuine check-in a few weeks after a big win to ask, "Who else in your network could benefit from [Your Service]?"
*   **Reward the Loop:** Formalize a simple, non-monetary way to thank your referrers (e.g., a free consultation, a small gift card) to encourage them to keep the loop going.
