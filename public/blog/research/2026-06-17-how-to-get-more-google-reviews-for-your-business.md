## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable plan to get more genuine, high-quality reviews for their local business on Google.

## Best Angle
Stop chasing vague marketing tactics and focus on the simple, direct steps that Google actually rewards with better visibility.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles list generic review-asking tips. They miss the crucial, often overlooked step of optimizing the Google Business Profile *before* asking for reviews, and they don't address the difference between fake volume and real customer trust.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Profile Audit First:** Check your Google Business Profile. Is your service area, hours, and basic information 100% accurate? Google prioritizes profiles that look professional first. [VERIFY]
*   **Review Strategy Over Volume:** Focus on asking for reviews *after* a positive interaction, not just randomly. Make the request specific: "If you found this helpful, a quick 30-second review on Google would help other local customers find us."
*   **The "Review Gap":** Identify where you are missing reviews. Are you missing reviews on specific service categories? Target those specific keywords when asking for feedback.
*   **Respond to Everything:** Reply to every review—good or bad. Showing you care builds trust faster than asking for one. This shows Google you are an engaged business owner.
*   **Consistency Beats Intensity:** A steady stream of small, genuine reviews is better than a flood of fake ones. Focus on building a consistent habit, not a one-time push.
