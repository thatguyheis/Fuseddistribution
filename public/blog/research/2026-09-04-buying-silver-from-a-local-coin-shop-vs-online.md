## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Markup Visibility:** Local shops show premium costs upfront. Online platforms often hide these fees in the final price.
*   **Authentication:** Local shops offer physical inspection. This helps confirm the purity of coins or bars.
*   **Transaction History:** You can ask about the shop's history. This builds trust regarding their legitimacy.
*   **Accessibility:** Online offers 24/7 access. Local shops require scheduling and in-person visits.
*   **Storage Clarity:** Local dealers can offer immediate advice on secure, local storage options.
