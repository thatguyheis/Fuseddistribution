## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Proof silver coins are often high-relief and susceptible to surface abrasion.
*   Use a soft, dry cloth for initial dusting. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive powders.
*   For deeper cleaning, consult a professional coin conservator.
*   Store proof silver coins in airtight, acid-free, and inert containers.
*   Keep coins away from moisture and direct, harsh light sources.
