## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Primary Service:** Select the main service you offer first (e.g., "Plumber," "Salons," "Restaurant"). This is your biggest signal to Google.
*   **Use Location-Specific Terms:** Ensure your categories reflect the exact service you do *in your city* (e.g., not just "Plumber," but "Emergency Plumbing Services in [City Name]").
*   **Prioritize High-Intent Categories:** Choose categories that people actively search for when they need your service right now, not broad, vague terms.
*   **Don't Overstuff:** Stick to 3-5 strong, relevant primary categories. Too many categories confuse Google and dilute your signal.
*   **Check Competitors:** Look at what your direct, local competitors are using. See what categories they have selected to see what signals they are using.
