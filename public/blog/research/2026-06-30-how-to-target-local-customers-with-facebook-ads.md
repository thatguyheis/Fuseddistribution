## Search Intent
What is the simplest, most direct way for a local business to get found by people searching for their specific service in their area?

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on complicated marketing. Here is the exact, no-fluff setup you need to get found on Google, right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on expensive ad platforms or complex SEO jargon. They miss the simple, foundational steps—like optimizing your Google Business Profile—that actually drive immediate, local traffic. They talk about big strategies instead of the small, actionable fixes that deliver calls.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile is Your Front Door:** Set up your profile correctly with accurate hours, photos, and services in your specific city. This is the single most important, free step. [VERIFY]
*   **Review Strategy Over Everything Else:** Focus 80% of your effort on getting consistent, genuine reviews on Google. People trust local businesses based on what other local customers say.
*   **Local Keywords Beat Broad Terms:** Stop trying to rank for "plumbing." Start targeting "emergency plumber downtown [City Name]." Be specific about what you do and where you do it.
*   **The Website's Job is to Support, Not Drive Traffic:** Your website needs to clearly state *what* you do, *where* you do it, and provide a clear way to call. It’s a directory, not a sales pitch.
*   **Don't Chase Vanity Metrics:** Ignore vague promises about "impressions." Focus only on calls, appointments booked, and foot traffic that comes directly from your search.
