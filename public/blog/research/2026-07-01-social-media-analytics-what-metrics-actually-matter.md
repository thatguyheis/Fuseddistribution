## Search Intent
I want to know exactly which Google metrics actually lead to more phone calls for my local business.

## Best Angle
Stop tracking vanity metrics. Focus only on the three Google signals that drive local customers to your door.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles list vague vanity metrics like "impressions" or "engagement." They fail to connect those numbers directly to the crucial action: a phone call or a visit. They don't explain the simple, actionable steps to fix the profile that gets ignored.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Call-to-Action Density:** Your Google Business Profile (GBP) must have a clear, one-click "Call Now" button visible on every page. If it's hidden, you lose the click. [VERIFY]
*   **Review Velocity Over Volume:** Focus less on the total number of reviews and more on the *speed* of getting new, recent reviews (within the last 90 days). Google favors recent activity. [VERIFY]
*   **Citation Consistency:** Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) are identical across every single online listing—Yelp, industry directories, and your main website. Inconsistency confuses Google.
*   **Local Intent Signals:** Track searches for your specific service + your city (e.g., "plumber downtown [City]"). If you aren't showing up for those exact phrases, you aren't visible.
*   **Review Response Time:** Reply to every review, positive or negative, within 24 hours. This signals to Google that you are an active, responsive business, which boosts local trust signals. [VERIFY]
