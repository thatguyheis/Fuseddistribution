## Search Intent
I want a simple, actionable plan to get local customers to find me on Google and call me directly.

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on fancy website design and start focusing on the one thing that actually brings customers to your door: Google visibility.

## Competitor Gap
Most advice focuses on complex SEO jargon or expensive software. They miss the simple, direct steps—like fixing your Google Business Profile—that immediately impact phone calls. They promise big changes without showing the small, easy fixes that work for a local plumber or salon.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free. Most local businesses set it up wrong, making you invisible to local searches.
*   Reviews beat your website for local search. Focus on getting consistent, simple reviews, not complex marketing campaigns.
*   If you don't show up when someone searches your service + your city, you don't exist to them. This is basic search mechanics.
*   Don't worry about complex algorithms. Focus on the three things Google cares about: Name, Address, Phone number (NAP), and consistent local mentions.
*   Start with your Google Business Profile. It’s the fastest way to get local customers to call you today. [VERIFY]
