## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Check Your Homepage:** Ensure your main business page is easily accessible and indexed by Google.
*   **Use the "Sitelinks" Strategy:** Document the specific pages on your site that directly relate to your services (e.g., "Plumbing Services," "Salons").
*   **Verify Map Integration:** Confirm that your Google Business Profile is correctly linked to the specific URL of your main business website.
*   **Keep It Simple:** Don't overcomplicate this. Focus on the primary link Google needs to crawl and verify your business presence.
*   **Use Google Search Console:** This is your free tool to see exactly how Google is reading your site and where documentation is failing.
