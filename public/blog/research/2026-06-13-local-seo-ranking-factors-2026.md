## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on broad, national SEO theories. They miss the critical, hyper-local factors that service businesses actually need to focus on. They fail to connect the dots between a website, Google Business Profile, and local reviews.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is the #1 ranking factor for local searches.** It is free, and setting it up correctly is more important than your website design.
*   **Review Velocity matters more than website content.** Google prioritizes recent, relevant reviews from local customers over the sheer amount of text on your site.
*   **Local Citations and Consistency are essential.** Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across all online directories, including Yelp and industry-specific sites.
