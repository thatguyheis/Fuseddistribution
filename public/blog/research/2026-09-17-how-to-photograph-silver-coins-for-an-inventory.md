## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Lighting is Key:** Use soft, diffused, indirect light. Avoid harsh shadows that obscure details on the coin's surface.
*   **Scale Reference:** Always include a known, measurable object in the frame. This helps viewers gauge the actual size of the silver coin.
*   **Focus on Detail:** Use a high-resolution camera setup. Ensure sharp focus on the mint marks and any specific identifying features.
*   **Consistent Backgrounds:** Use a neutral, uniform background. This prevents visual clutter and keeps the focus entirely on the silver coin.
*   **Metadata Matters:** Label your files clearly. Include date, coin type, and any relevant measurements in your file naming convention.
