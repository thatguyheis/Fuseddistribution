## Search Intent
What simple, actionable steps can I take *today* to get found by customers searching for my service locally?

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on fancy marketing. Focus on the one thing that actually brings customers to your door: making sure Google knows you exist right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most advice focuses on complex SEO tactics or expensive software. They miss the fundamental truth: local visibility is about optimizing your Google Business Profile and getting real, local reviews. They talk about *doing* things; they don't show the simple, direct path to getting calls.

## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront:** Set up your profile completely. Use photos of your actual location and your team.
*   **Reviews are currency:** Focus on asking happy customers for reviews. Don't just wait for them; ask them after a great service.
*   **NAP Consistency matters:** Make sure your Name, Address, and Phone number are exactly the same everywhere online. This builds trust with Google.
*   **Local search is simple:** When someone searches "plumber near me," Google looks for businesses that are physically near them and have accurate local info.
*   **Don't chase vanity metrics:** Ignore vague promises about "reach." Focus only on calls and appointments you book directly from Google.
