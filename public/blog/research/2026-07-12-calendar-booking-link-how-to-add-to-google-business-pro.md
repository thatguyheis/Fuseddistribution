## Key Points
*   **Use a dedicated booking tool:** Don't just use a generic link; use a tool that integrates well with Google's system to ensure smooth tracking. [VERIFY]
*   **Profile Link Placement:** The booking link should be clearly visible in your website description or directly in the "Services" section of your Google Business Profile.
*   **Mobile Check:** Test the link on a phone. Most local customers search on mobile, and the booking flow must be flawless there. [VERIFY]
*   **Consistency is Key:** Make sure the link you use on your website is the *exact* link you put in Google Business Profile. Inconsistency confuses the system.
*   **Review the "Contact" Section:** Ensure your profile's contact section points directly to the booking widget, not just a generic email address. [VERIFY]
