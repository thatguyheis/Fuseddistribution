## Content Brief: Customer Testimonial Video How-To

**Search Intent**
The user wants a clear, actionable guide on how to film and use a customer testimonial video to improve their local business visibility.

**Best Angle**
Stop writing vague testimonials. Film a short, simple video that shows a real customer talking about your service, and use it immediately to fix your Google search results.

**Competitor Gap**
Most guides focus on *what* a testimonial is, not the practical, no-fluff steps for filming and integrating it into local SEO. They miss the crucial part about framing the message to solve the local business owner's immediate pain point.

**Key Points (5 bullets)**
*   **Focus on the "Before & After":** Don't just ask "How was your experience?" Ask them to talk about how your service *changed* their business (e.g., "Before Fused, I had zero calls; now I get 10 new clients a week").
*   **Keep it Under 60 Seconds:** Local business owners are busy. Short, punchy videos get watched and retained far better than long stories.
*   **Shoot in Good Light:** Use natural light (facing a window). A messy room kills credibility. A clean, professional look is enough to show you take your service seriously.
*   **The "Ask for Permission" Rule:** Never film a testimonial without explicit, written permission. Make it easy for the customer to say yes.
*   **Direct Integration:** Show exactly where to upload the video—specifically linking it to your Google Business Profile or your website's service page.
