**Key Points (5 bullets):**
1.  Start by identifying the personal significance of the coins. This is the core of your family narrative.
2.  Select coins with a specific history. Look for pieces with personal connections or notable dates.
3.  Document the journey. Include photos, notes, and stories about where you found them or why you chose them.
4.  Keep the language simple. Focus on the tangible memories, not just the metal's worth.
5.  Create an accessible format. This could be a simple binder, a digital album, or a short written history.
