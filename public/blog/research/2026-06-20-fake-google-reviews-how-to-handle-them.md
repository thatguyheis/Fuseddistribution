## Search Intent
What is the simplest, most direct way to fix a bad review situation so it actually helps your local business get found?

## Best Angle
Stop worrying about fake reviews and start focusing on the real stuff Google cares about: showing up right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles focus on *how* to get fake reviews, which is irrelevant. They miss the crucial step of understanding what Google *actually* prioritizes for local searches—which is consistent, real customer interaction, not manufactured praise.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Prioritizes Activity:** Google doesn't just count the number of reviews; they look for recent, genuine interactions (calls, website visits, actual map views).
*   **The "Fake" Trap:** Manipulating reviews usually triggers Google's spam filters. Focus on building volume through legitimate, consistent service.
*   **Profile Ownership:** Ensure your Google Business Profile is 100% owned and verified. This is the foundation; fake reviews on a weak profile mean nothing.
*   **Response Strategy:** How you respond to *all* reviews—good and bad—signals professionalism. A quick, human reply builds trust faster than any review score.
*   **Local Signals Beat Vanity Metrics:** Focus on local signals: consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone number) across the web and local directories.
