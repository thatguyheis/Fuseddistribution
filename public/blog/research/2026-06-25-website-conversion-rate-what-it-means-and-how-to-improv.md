## Search Intent
The user wants a simple, actionable guide on how to improve their website's ability to turn visitors into actual phone calls or visits from local customers.

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on fancy website design. Focus on the one thing that actually gets you calls: making sure Google knows exactly what you do, right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about technical SEO fixes or expensive software. They miss the simple, foundational steps—like optimizing your Google Business Profile and making sure your website clearly tells a local customer how to hire you. They focus on *traffic*, not *conversions*.

## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile is your storefront:** Set up your profile correctly with photos, hours, and services. This is free and is how people find you when they search "plumber near me." [VERIFY]
*   **Your website needs a clear instruction:** Every page must tell a local business owner, in plain language, exactly what service you offer and how to call you. No fluff.
*   **Reviews beat fancy design:** Focus on getting consistent, simple reviews on your Google profile. This is the #1 signal Google uses to trust you. [VERIFY]
*   **Local search is about proximity:** Optimize your site for local searches (e.g., "best HVAC repair in [Your City]"). This tells Google you serve a specific area.
*   **Don't chase clicks; chase calls:** Measure success by phone rings, not by how many people looked at your homepage.
