## Best Angle

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Focus on Immediate Value:** Frame the chatbot not as a fancy tool, but as a 24/7 assistant that answers basic questions, freeing up your time.
*   **Start Simple:** Recommend no complex coding. Suggest using existing, user-friendly platforms that require minimal technical setup (e.g., specific website builder integrations).
*   **Integrate Local Info:** Show how the bot can pull up your hours, service areas, or a quick contact form, linking directly to your Google Business Profile.
*   **Train for Conversions:** Emphasize that the bot’s job isn't just answering FAQs; it's designed to smoothly hand off qualified leads to a human when necessary.
*   **Measure What Matters:** Instruct the owner to track if the bot interaction leads to an actual phone call or a submitted quote, not just the number of messages received.
