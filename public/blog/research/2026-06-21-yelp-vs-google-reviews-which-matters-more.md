## Key Points
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is your storefront:** It’s free, and 80% of local searches start here.
*   **Review volume beats page rank for local:** Google prioritizes businesses with active, recent reviews over vanity metrics.
*   **The "Service + City" search is the real winner:** Focus your efforts on optimizing for hyper-local searches, not broad industry terms.
*   **Consistency beats complexity:** Showing up consistently on Google Maps and local packs is more important than one big, expensive marketing push.
*   **Website is a tool, not the main attraction:** Your website just needs to point people to the place where they can call you *right now*.
