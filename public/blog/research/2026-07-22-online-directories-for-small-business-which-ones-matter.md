## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Google Business Profile (GBP) is #1:** This is the single most important listing. Focus 80% of your directory effort here first.
*   **Niche Directories Beat General Ones:** Look for directories specific to your industry (e.g., plumbing directories, local salon listings) over massive, generic ones.
*   **Consistency is Key:** Ensure your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are *identical* across every single directory you list. Inconsistency confuses search engines.
*   **Review Aggregators Matter:** Directories that facilitate and showcase reviews (like Yelp or industry-specific review sites) are crucial for local trust signals.
*   **Focus on Citation Building:** Use directories to ensure you appear consistently across the web, which builds authority, not just vanity metrics.
