## Key Points
*   **Profile Verification is Key:** You must verify your Google Business Profile in Search Console first; this is the foundation for all local search data. [VERIFY]
*   **Error Monitoring is Free:** Search Console immediately flags technical issues (like incorrect mapping or listing errors) that are actively hurting your local ranking.
*   **Query Insights Show Real Demand:** Don't just look at traffic. Use the Query Insights report to see the exact phrases local customers are using to find you right now. [VERIFY]
*   **Fixing Map Errors:** Search Console helps you identify and correct discrepancies between your listing and what Google actually displays on the map.
*   **Focus on Local Signals:** Use the console data to confirm if your listing is showing up for terms like "[Your Service] near me" or "[Your Service] [Your City]."
