## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   **Use Google Search Console (GSC):** This is your direct line to Google. You must use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing immediately after the update.
*   **Verify the Update:** Ensure the service page is live, has the correct title tag, and contains unique, valuable content specific to your local service area.
*   **Submit the Request:** Go into the URL Inspection tool, select the updated page, and click "Request Indexing." This tells Google, "Hey, I changed this, please look at it now."
*   **Check for Technical Errors:** Look for any errors reported in GSC. Fixing these errors *before* requesting indexing ensures Google doesn't get stuck on old versions of the page.
*   **Be Patient, But Active:** While the request is submitted, Google crawls the site. Monitor GSC for the "Coverage" report to see if the page status moves from "Not Indexed" to "Indexed."
