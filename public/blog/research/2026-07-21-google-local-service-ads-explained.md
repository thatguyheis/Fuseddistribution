## Key Points
*   **It’s not just a listing:** Google Local Service Ads are paid lead generation tools, not just free profiles. You pay for visibility to customers actively searching for your service *right now*.
*   **Focus on the right profile:** Getting your Google Business Profile set up correctly is the foundation. Misspelling your service or location kills your chances immediately.
*   **Targeted Ads matter:** You don't just show up; you show up when people are actively looking for your specific trade or service in your city.
*   **It’s about the call, not the click:** These ads are designed to get you a direct phone call or message, not just a website visit. Focus on the conversion point.
*   **Keep it simple:** Don't try to master every feature. Focus on setting up the basic profile and ensuring your service area is 100% accurate.
