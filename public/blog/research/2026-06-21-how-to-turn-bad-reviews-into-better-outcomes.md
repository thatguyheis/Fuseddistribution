## Search Intent
What is the simplest, actionable way for my local business to get discovered on Google Maps and get more phone calls?

## Best Angle
Stop wasting time on fancy marketing. We show you the one thing most local businesses mess up that’s costing them customers right now.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about SEO theory or complex website redesigns. They fail to focus on the immediate, tangible fixes—like optimizing your Google Business Profile and handling reviews—that actually drive calls this week. They offer advice that requires a degree, not a checklist.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free, but 90% of businesses set it up wrong. Check your service areas and respond to every review within 24 hours.
*   Reviews matter more than your website for local search. Focus on getting 5-star feedback consistently, not just chasing vanity metrics.
*   If you don't show up when someone searches your service + your city, you are invisible. This is the most basic step to visibility.
*   Stop worrying about complex algorithms. Focus on local signals: consistent phone calls, quick replies, and accurate location data.
*   A basic, functional website isn't enough. You need a system that captures those local searchers and turns them into actual customers.
