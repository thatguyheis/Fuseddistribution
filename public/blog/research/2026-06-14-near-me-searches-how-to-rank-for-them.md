## Search Intent
What specific, actionable steps do I need to take right now to get my local business found on Google maps and search results?

## Best Angle
Stop focusing on website design and start focusing on what Google actually cares about: local proof and visibility.

## Competitor Gap
Most articles talk about general SEO theory or complex technical setups. They miss the simple, immediate actions local business owners need to take to fix their visibility today. They fail to connect the dots between a website and actual phone calls.

## Key Points (5 bullets)
*   Your Google Business Profile is your single most important asset. It is free, and most local businesses set it up incorrectly, missing critical details.
*   Google prioritizes proof. Getting real, local reviews is far more effective for ranking than fancy website features.
*   Local search is not about keywords; it’s about proximity and relevance. You must optimize your profile for your specific service area.
*   Your website needs to be a call machine, not just an online brochure. It must clearly tell people exactly how to hire you.
*   If you don't show up when someone searches your service + your city, you are invisible to potential customers.
