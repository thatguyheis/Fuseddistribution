## OVERLAY: TELL ONE CUSTOMER STORY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: To make a useful customer testimonial video, record one customer's real experience, capture clear sound, and edit the answer around a specific question a potential customer has. Start with the story and the permission to use it. A better camera cannot rescue a vague answer, a misleading edit, or an unclear agreement about where the footage will appear. You can begin with a phone, a stable support, and a quiet room.

---

## OVERLAY: AGREE ON VIDEO USE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Keep approval records with the footage rather than in a separate inbox that will be hard to search later. Offer a review of the proposed edit to catch factual mistakes and unexpected context. Do not make a positive testimonial a condition of helping with an unresolved service problem. The customer should be able to describe their experience honestly and decline participation without being pressured into a performance.

---

## OVERLAY: ASK AND LISTEN

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Ask one question at a time. Let the customer finish rather than speaking over the last words. If an answer refers to something the viewer will not hear, ask a neutral follow-up that helps the customer explain the context. Keep the conversation focused without insisting on a particular choice of words. It is fine to record another take when noise interrupts or the customer loses their place.

---

## OVERLAY: REVIEW CAPTIONS AND DISCLOSURES

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Review the FTC endorsement guidance for your situation. Keep any necessary disclosure visible and understandable in the format people will actually see. Captions and a platform label are not a reason to skip reviewing the final presentation. Keep a master edit, then export versions for the places where you have permission to use the testimonial. A website player and a vertical social post may need different framing or text placement.

---

## OVERLAY: MEASURE WITHOUT OVERCLAIMING

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 26s minimum

Narration: Use what you learn to improve a later interview or edit. An increase in calls after publication does not by itself prove that the video caused the increase. Other campaigns, seasonal changes, and unrelated business activity can affect the same measures. Keep performance claims proportionate to the evidence. Begin with one approved customer story, publish it where it fits, and use the result to refine the next recording.
