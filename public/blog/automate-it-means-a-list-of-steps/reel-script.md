# Reel Script: automate-it-means-a-list-of-steps
format: fused-tech-short-v3

## Segment 1
Narration: Automation is not a robot. It's a list of steps you stop doing by hand.

## Segment 2
Narration: Before you buy an AI tool, write down the steps you already do.

## Segment 3
Narration: Teaching example. A repair shop gets an estimate request by email.

## Segment 4
Narration: Step one, read the request. Two, check what's missing. Three, draft a reply. Four, approve it. Five, send it. Six, log it.

## Segment 5
Narration: Now mark each step. Which ones are the same every time?

## Segment 6
Narration: Checking for a missing size or material is a rule. Drafting is pattern work. Logging is copy and paste. Those can be automated.

## Segment 7
Narration: Approving the reply, and anything with a price, stays with a person.

## Segment 8
Narration: Automate the boring steps first, one at a time, and keep the approval step yours.

## Segment 9
Narration: Want the step list template I use before automating anything?

## Segment 10
Narration: Comment the word grow and I'll send you the example SOPs so you can reference and use them in your AI systems.
