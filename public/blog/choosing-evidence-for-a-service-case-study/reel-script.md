# Reel Script: choosing-evidence-for-a-service-case-study
format: long-form
engagement-policy: 1
content-kind: educate

---

## HOOK

**Visual:** Show the work. Prove the claim.
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Choose evidence for a service case study by starting with the claim you want a reader to assess. Then show the relevant work, the measurement method and the limits of the result. A polished screenshot can demonstrate a design change. It cannot, on its own, demonstrate additional sales or explain why customer behavior changed.

---

## OVERLAY: MATCH CASE STUDY EVIDENCE TO A SPECIFIC CLAIM

**Visual:** Match case study evidence to a specific claim
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: Write the proposed headline before collecting screenshots. If the headline claims that a task became easier, identify what would support that statement: an observed completion, a documented customer comment or a comparable task measurement. If the only evidence is that a new page was delivered, narrow the headline to describe that delivery. Make a simple claim register with a source beside each statement.

---

## OVERLAY: KEEP THE CASE STUDY BASELINE AND PERIOD VISIBLE

**Visual:** Keep the case study baseline and period visible
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: For a before and after comparison, record the date range, metric definition and collection method on both sides. If those changed, explain the change rather than treating the results as directly equivalent. Preserve the original export or report so the comparison can be checked later. The GOV.UK Service Manual discusses measuring service benefits against a baseline and using case studies to communicate value.

---

## OVERLAY: SEPARATE MEASURED RESULTS FROM POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS

**Visual:** Separate measured results from possible explanations
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: A result can be real while its cause remains uncertain. Record other relevant changes during the observation period, such as a promotion or a different posting schedule, when you know about them. Do not invent missing context. Explain whether your evidence establishes a comparison, a customer opinion or a causal conclusion. For an early project, a clear statement might report views over a named period and separately state that revenue has not yet been measured.

---

## OVERLAY: PUBLISH EVIDENCE THE CUSTOMER CAN UNDERSTAND

**Visual:** Publish evidence the customer can understand
**Duration:** 24s minimum

Narration: Choose a small number of examples that support the central claim. Label screenshots with the relevant state and date, remove private material and obtain the necessary permission before identifying a client or quoting their feedback. Keep internal source records even when the public example needs redaction. End with the remaining question or next measurement, rather than a promise that another customer will receive the same result.
