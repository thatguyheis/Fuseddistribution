## segments
- type: overlay text: CHECK 401K ELIGIBILITY narration: Ask the plan administrator whether this balance is eligible for a distribution or rollover. Current employees can face plan limits. Confirm pretax, Roth, after tax, stock, and loan components before you choose a destination. Moving pretax money to a Roth IRA generally creates taxable conversion income, and required minimum distributions cannot be rolled over.
- type: overlay text: DIRECT ROLLOVER narration: A direct rollover sends money from the 401(k) trustee to the receiving IRA trustee. Employer plan distributions paid directly to an eligible plan or IRA generally avoid mandatory 20 percent federal withholding.
- type: stat text: 60 DAY ROLLOVER narration: If the distribution is paid to you, the IRS generally gives you 60 days to deposit eligible funds. Employer plans generally withhold 20 percent. To roll over the full amount, you must replace that withheld money from another source.
- type: overlay text: ONE ROLLOVER RULE narration: The one rollover per year rule generally concerns IRA to IRA distributions. It does not prohibit a qualified employer plan to IRA rollover or a trustee to trustee transfer. Have the institutions classify your exact transaction.
- type: overlay text: CUSTODIAN AND SILVER narration: Before moving cash, read the receiving custodian’s fees, approved asset list, purchase process, and storage agreement. Get written approval for the exact coin or bar. Do not assume a generic one ounce or .999 description is enough.
- type: overlay text: STORAGE AND RECORDS narration: IRA metals remain in the trustee or custodian’s storage arrangement. A home safe is not automatically an IRA depository. Keep the plan notice, transfer receipt, invoice, shipping record, and custody statement together.
- type: question text: Is a direct rollover available? subtext: CHECK THE PLAN FIRST narration: Compare the existing plan, a new employer plan, and the IRA’s complete costs. Ask a tax professional to review the transaction before requesting money. Is a direct rollover available in your plan?
