## OVERLAY: DEFINE THE PURCHASE PROCESS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: Silver stacking means accumulating physical silver and keeping track of what you own. For a beginner, the useful first step is a written purchase process: decide what money is available, identify the exact product, compare complete costs, arrange storage and keep records. There is no required starting ounce count, monthly spending amount or allocation that suits every reader.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE BUDGET AGAIN

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Set a maximum total outlay for the proposed purchase, including delivery and any other stated charges. Separately identify money needed for ordinary expenses and foreseeable commitments. A recurring purchase schedule is optional. It can organize a budget, but it does not guarantee a profit or make an unsuitable purchase suitable. Review affordability again when circumstances change.

---

## OVERLAY: COMPARE DELIVERED COST

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: Here is a hypothetical worksheet, not a current quote. Imagine five items, each containing one troy ounce of silver. Their combined price is 200 dollars and delivery is ten dollars, with no other charges. The total outlay is 210 dollars and the cost is 42 dollars per ounce. Recalculate using the actual final checkout total before confirming an order.

---

## OVERLAY: VERIFY BEFORE REPEATING

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Make sure the seller identity and contact information are independently verifiable. Read the offer, delivery terms and return conditions before paying. On receipt, reconcile the package against the invoice and product specification. Keep records connected to the purchase. A simple visual check or home screening observation is not a definitive assay.

---

## OVERLAY: CALCULATE NET SALE PROCEEDS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Breaking even means net sale proceeds equal the costs included in your calculation. In our five-ounce hypothetical example, the purchase total was 210 dollars. If selling later costs ten dollars, a gross offer of 220 dollars produces 210 dollars net. That gross offer is 44 dollars per ounce. This does not predict what a buyer will offer.

---

## OVERLAY: REVIEW BEFORE THE NEXT PURCHASE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: Before repeating a purchase, review what the transaction actually cost and whether storage and records worked as planned. Compare a current offer using fresh inputs. A repeatable process should preserve the checks, while allowing the decision to change. Building a stack does not require buying whenever a calendar reminder appears.
