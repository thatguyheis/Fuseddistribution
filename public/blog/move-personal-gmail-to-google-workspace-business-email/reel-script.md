---
## OVERLAY: Identify your account path
**Visual:** The answer depends on the account you already have.
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: The answer depends on the account you already have. A Workspace account that started with a Gmail sign-in has a specific Google domain-verification path. A free personal Gmail account and a newly created company Workspace account are separate identities. First identify which case describes you before moving anything.

---
## OVERLAY: Domain verified Workspace
**Visual:** This is a specific Workspace signup path.
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: For the Gmail-based Workspace path, Google documents a transition where verifying your domain adds a custom address and administrator features. Google says email, Drive files, and other account data remain with that account on this specific path. Some personal services or billing arrangements can change, so read the current instructions first.

---
## OVERLAY: Separate accounts stay separate
**Visual:** A separate personal account is not merged automatically.
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: That is not a general button for converting any free Gmail account. If you create a separate work account, personal mail and Drive data do not automatically merge into it. List the items you need for work, such as messages, contacts, calendars, or files, and plan any import as a separate step.

---
## OVERLAY: Check account transfer
**Visual:** Check eligibility and get owner consent.
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: An organization may also find an unmanaged personal account using an address that conflicts with its domain. Google provides a transfer process for eligible accounts, and the account owner must accept. Some accounts are ineligible, including family-group members. Transfer is different from importing mail, so check the exact rules.

---
## OVERLAY: Plan migration carefully
**Visual:** Keep the source until the destination is checked.
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: Before starting, inventory the source account, confirm who owns each item, and select the documented route that matches your account. Keep the source available. Afterward, check that the intended mail and files are accessible in the destination. Do not assume everything moves automatically just because both accounts use Gmail. Keep a note of what remains personal and who can access the work account. Confirm the owner can still sign in.
