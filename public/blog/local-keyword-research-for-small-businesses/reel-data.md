Not Tracking Your Results: Use Google Analytics and Google Search Console to monitor your website’s performance and identify which keywords are driving traffic. - type: overlay
  text: UNDERSTANDING BALANCE BETWEEN KEYWORD
  narration: Understanding the balance between keyword volume and keyword difficulty is key to successful local SEO. Keyword volume refers to how many people are searching for a particular keyword. High volume means lots of potential traffic. Keyword difficulty refers to how competitive a keyword is - how many other websites are already ranking for it. If you target a keyword with extremely high difficulty, it will be very difficult to rank, even with a large volume of searches.
