## OVERLAY: CRYSTAL CLEAR ABOUT DESIRED

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Be crystal clear about the desired action. A vague request is likely to be ignored. Keep the process as simple as possible. The fewer steps involved, the more likely people are to participate. If it takes more than two clicks or a quick phone call, people will likely abandon the process. A simple link to a landing page with a clear call to action is often the best approach.
