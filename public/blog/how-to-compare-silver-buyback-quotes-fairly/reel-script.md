## HOOK

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 13s minimum

Narration: The biggest silver buyback quote does not always leave you with the most money. Compare the same items, the stated costs and the conditions attached to the offer. Keep an estimate separate from a sale you have actually accepted.

---

## OVERLAY: MATCH THE ITEMS

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: Send each buyer the same product list. Include quantities, stated weight and purity, the maker when known and relevant condition details. Keep different products on separate lines. If a buyer quotes for only part of the collection, mark that difference before comparing the total with another offer.

---

## OVERLAY: RECORD THE TERMS

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Save the quote time and ask when the price expires. Identify whether the figure is an estimate or a price available to lock. Read the acceptance and cancellation terms before committing. Do not accept several binding sales for the same items just to compare prices. Ask for refreshed estimates instead.

---

## OVERLAY: COMPARE THE PROCEEDS

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: Subtract the sale costs you would pay from the gross offer. Count each charge once and label missing costs as unknown. A supplied shipping label is not automatically free. Confirm the charge, the protection and the instructions for your shipment before treating the estimate as complete.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE ARITHMETIC

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 16s minimum

Narration: Here is an invented example, not a market quote. One buyer offers a thousand dollars with thirty dollars in sale costs, leaving nine hundred seventy. Another offers nine hundred ninety with ten dollars in costs, leaving nine hundred eighty. The second leaves ten dollars more under these assumptions.

---

## OVERLAY: PLAN THE HANDOFF

**Visual:** Original buyback comparison diagram
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: Ask how inspection affects the offer and what happens if the buyer disagrees with your description. Record any return costs. Separate delivery, inspection, payment issue and funds availability. Keep the accepted terms and shipment reference, then reconcile the actual payment. This comparison does not calculate your after tax profit. Before accepting an offer, compare the same inventory and keep unresolved costs visible. Save the comparison alongside the final sale record so an estimate is never mistaken for a completed result.
