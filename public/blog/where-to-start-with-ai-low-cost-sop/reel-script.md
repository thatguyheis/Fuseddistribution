## HOOK
**Duration:** 4s minimum
Narration: Want to learn AI without a big bill?

---

## OVERLAY: Start with one task
**Duration:** 10s minimum
Narration: Pick a repeatable job you already understand. Define its input, useful result, and what a person must check.

---

## OVERLAY: Test a free tool
**Duration:** 8s minimum
Narration: Try a free AI tier with safe examples. Check current limits, then compare its draft with your own result.

---

## OVERLAY: Keep one SOP
**Duration:** 8s minimum
Narration: Set steps, privacy rules, output format, checks, and what to do when details are missing. Keep it where each tool can read it.

---

## OVERLAY: Pass a clear handoff
**Duration:** 7s minimum
Narration: For each handoff, pass the SOP version, completed step, verified facts, open questions, and next action. Tools may not share memory.

---

## OVERLAY: Review before use
**Duration:** 5s minimum
Narration: Check names, facts, privacy, and format before anything goes out.
