- type: overlay
  text: WEBSITE ANALYTICS TELLS LOCAL
  narration: Website analytics tells a local business how people find the site, which pages they enter through, and whether they take a useful next step. You do not need dozens of reports to begin. You need a working analytics tag, a small list of customer actions, and a weekly habit of turning one finding into one improvement. A plumber may want estimate requests and phone calls.
- type: stat
  text: 4, SUCCESSFUL REQUEST FORM USE
  narration: Give each important action a clear name. In Google Analytics 4, a successful request form can use Google's recommended generatelead event. Important events can then be marked as key events so they appear alongside traffic and landing-page data. Google's guides explain recommended events and how key events work. Before interpreting any chart, make sure data collection works. Visit the site from your phone and computer.
- type: overlay
  text: ALSO LOOK DUPLICATE TAGS
  narration: Also look for duplicate tags, which can inflate counts and make performance appear stronger than it is. Document who owns the account, property, tag manager container, and website access. Use individual accounts with appropriate permissions instead of a shared password. That small step lowers the risk of losing access when an employee or vendor changes.
- type: overlay
  text: TRAFFIC ACQUISITION REPORT GROUPS
  narration: The Traffic acquisition report groups visits from sources such as organic search, paid search, referrals, email, social, and direct traffic. Google's Traffic acquisition documentation explains that it is session-scoped and can include sessions, engagement, key events, and session key event rate. Compare sources using both traffic and outcomes.

For a local business, it may be the home page, a service page, a location page, or a blog article. The Google Analytics Landing page report shows sessions and key events by entry page. - type: overlay
  text: TEST ONE LIKELY CAUSE
  narration: Test one likely cause, such as a confusing headline, a hidden call button, or a form that asks for too much information. Measure the final action and the steps immediately before it. For a form, track successful submission rather than a click on the submit button. A button click can occur even when validation fails.
- type: overlay
  text: GOOGLE SUPPORTS LINKING BUSINESS
  narration: Google supports linking a Business Profile with Google Analytics. Available shared metrics can include website clicks, calls, directions, messages, bookings, menus, and total interactions. The official connection guide notes that the data is aggregated and limited to the most recent six months. Use profile and website data as related signals, not as a perfect person-by-person journey. A customer may see the profile on one device and visit the site later on another.
- type: overlay
  text: MOBILE RESULTS TRAIL DESKTOP
  narration: Mobile results trail desktop: test page speed, tap targets, forms, and phone links on an actual phone. Do not declare a winner from a tiny sample. Compare consistent date ranges and account for promotions, holidays, weather, or other events that change local demand. First, verify that important events still work. Next, record sessions, key events, session key event rate, top landing pages, and top sources.
