## OVERLAY: MESSAGE SHORT ENOUGH READ

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Is the message short enough to read quickly? Did the draft add anything you did not approve? If the answer is no on any one of those, revise before you send. This review step is what keeps AI useful instead of risky. Do not judge AI by whether the draft sounds impressive. Judge it by whether it saves time and improves results.

## OVERLAY: DOES REMOVE JUDGMENT ADVANTAGE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 27s minimum

Narration: That does not remove the need for judgment. Your advantage is still knowing the customer, the offer, and the right next move. AI just helps you get the words on the page faster. Start with one email type this week, such as estimate follow ups, appointment reminders, or proposal replies. Build one prompt that works, improve it over a few real messages, and keep the final review human. That is how you use AI to write better business emails without sounding like a machine.
