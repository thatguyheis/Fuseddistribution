# Coin flips: check before buying

hook: Coin flips: check before buying

## segments
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK PRODUCT DETAILS
 narration: Open the actual product page and look for a material description tied to the exact item. A store can sell several types of flips with similar photographs. Check the product number on the listing, the package, and the receipt. A general statement elsewhere on the seller's website is less useful when you cannot connect it to the product being ordered.
- type: overlay
 text: CHECK HOLDER FIT
 narration: Record the coin's diameter from its issuer's specifications or existing paperwork before choosing a holder. A listing's outside flip dimensions are not a promise that every coin will fit comfortably inside. Check the usable pocket size and any stated coin size limits. Review the closure and how you would insert the coin without forcing it against a seam. Start with a small quantity when you have not used a product before. Inspect an empty holder under good light for rough edges, loose debris, or damage. Keep questionable pieces out of the usable supply. Do not test a tight fit with your most delicate coin.
- type: overlay
 text: LABEL THE RECORD
 narration: Plan the inventory label before opening anything. Give each coin an identifier that connects its holder to the purchase record. Keep paper notes in the designated label pocket, if the holder has one, rather than placing loose writing materials against the coin. Follow the manufacturer's directions for the holder you selected.
- type: overlay
 text: SAVE SUPPLY DETAILS
 narration: Save the manufacturer, product number, material statement, quantity, purchase date, and seller with your receipt. A photograph of the package can be useful when the same shop changes a listing later. Store that image with your inventory documentation so you do not have to repeat the research for the next order.
