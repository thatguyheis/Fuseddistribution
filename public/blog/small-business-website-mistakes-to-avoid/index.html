Small Business Website Mistakes To Avoid

June 24, 2026 Local Business Marketing

Small business website mistakes to avoid start with not focusing on what actually gets calls. You got a site, but nobody's reaching out to you. If you're tired of feeling invisible online, you need a simple plan that gets you found by the people actually looking for your service right now. We cut the fluff and show you how to get noticed.

Stop Wasting Time: The Top 3 Website Mistakes Killing Your Local Leads

Stop Wasting Time: The Top 3 Website Mistakes Killing Your Local Leads

Your Google Business Profile is your storefront. Most local business owners set it up wrong. You might have photos that don't show your real work, or your hours are wrong. You aren't responding to reviews when people leave them. Fix this first. This is your most direct way to get found right now.

Next, check your website's main action. Does it have one clear button? Tell people exactly what to do. Should it be "Call Now" or "Book an Appointment"? If people have to hunt for the next step, they leave. Make it impossible to miss.

Finally, stop using vague words online. Don't just use "plumbing." Use "plumber in [Your City]." Use terms people actually type into Google. This is how you show up when someone needs you fast.
