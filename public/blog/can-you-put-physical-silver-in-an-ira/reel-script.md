## OVERLAY: TRADITIONAL IRA CONTRIBUTIONS MAY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: With a Traditional IRA, contributions may be tax-deductible in the year they are made, and earnings grow tax-deferred until retirement, meaning you don’t pay taxes on the growth until you withdraw the funds. Conversely, with a Roth IRA, you contribute after-tax dollars, but qualified withdrawals in retirement are entirely tax-free. The choice between these two types depends heavily on your individual circumstances, current tax bracket, and anticipated future tax rates.

Narration: These coins and bars are recognized for their purity and authenticity. Bullion Purity: The silver must meet a minimum purity standard of .999 fine (99.9 percent pure silver).
