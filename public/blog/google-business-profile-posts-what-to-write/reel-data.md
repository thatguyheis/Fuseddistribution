## stats
- text: 7 DAYS THEN IT'S GONE
  explanation: Google rotates update posts out of prominent display after 7 days, so weekly posting keeps something fresh visible
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Google rotates your update posts out of prominent view after 7 days. That is not a punishment. It is a rhythm. One post a week keeps something fresh on your profile at all times, and it takes about fifteen minutes.
- text: 1 SERVICE PER POST
  explanation: One service, one photo, one reason to call beats company news and mission statements
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Here is the formula. One service, one real job photo, one reason to call. We fix burst pipes in your city, same day. That beats we are a trusted full service company, every single time. People search for a problem, not a brand story.
- text: 42% MORE DIRECTION REQUESTS

  graphic_type: percent_fill

  narration: Real work photos do the heavy lifting. Google reports profiles with photos get 42 percent more direction requests and 35 percent more website clicks. Post your team on a job site. Post the finished project. Proof beats promises.
- text: 7X MORE CLICKS
  explanation: Complete profiles get 7 times more clicks than incomplete ones, according to Google
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Complete profiles get seven times more clicks than incomplete ones. Posts are part of what complete looks like. A profile with fresh posts, photos, and current offers reads as open and busy. A bare profile reads as maybe closed.
- text: OFFERS OUTLIVE UPDATES
  explanation: Offer posts stay visible through their end date and carry a badge, while updates rotate out after 7 days
  graphic_type: none
  narration: The offer format is the underused weapon. Offers stay visible through the end date you set, and they get a badge. Fifty dollars off tune-ups booked by June 30 gives a number and a deadline. Great deals on service gives nothing. Use one offer a month.
- text: 77% READ REVIEWS FIRST
  explanation: 77 percent of consumers read reviews on a profile, 56 percent visit the website, 28 percent call
  graphic_type: percent_fill
  graphic:
    value: 77
    label: Read Reviews
    remainder_label: Skip Them
  narration: Here is what people actually do on your profile. 77 percent read reviews. 56 percent visit the website. 28 percent call. Your posts back up what your reviews claim. A photo of this week's job is evidence. That is why proof posts matter.
- text: CALLS, NOT IMPRESSIONS
  explanation: Judge posts by calls, direction requests, and bookings, not by views and impressions
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Do not celebrate impressions. They tell you how many people saw the profile, not how many became customers. Judge every post by calls, direction requests, and bookings. And give every post a clear path to the phone. Call to schedule beats any clever caption.
