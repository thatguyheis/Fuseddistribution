## VARIANT A (~88 words / ~29s)

### HOOK (0-2s)
A year of company news posts. Zero calls.

### BODY (3-24s)
I run a local business, and I treated our Google profile like a press page. Anniversary posts, team announcements, holiday greetings. Nobody cared. Then I changed one thing. Every Monday I post one service, one photo from a real job, and one line about why to call this week. Calls from the profile started showing up within a month. Google also drops your post from view after seven days, so weekly is the whole trick. Fifteen minutes. Once a week.

## VARIANT B (~80 words / ~27s)

### HOOK (0-2s)
If you run a service business, copy this.

### BODY (3-24s)
Week one, post one service with a real job photo. Week two, post a finished project and what the customer needed. Week three, post an offer with an actual deadline, even twenty five dollars off works. Week four, post your availability. That is the entire month, fifteen minutes a week. I used to overthink this and post nothing for months. A profile with fresh posts looks open and busy. A bare one looks closed.
