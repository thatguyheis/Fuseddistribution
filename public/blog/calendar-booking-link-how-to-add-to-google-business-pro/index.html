Why You Need a Booking Link Instead of Just a Phone Number

You’ll see a significant increase in bookings simply by offering this streamlined experience. You need a booking tool that works with Google. Don’t just slap a random link on your site. Use a tool that connects properly with Google’s system. This makes it easier for customers to find you and for Google to see that you handle reservations. Put this link where people look. Make sure it shows up clearly in your website description or under your services on your Google Business Profile. Studies show that mobile users are 80% more likely to book a service through a direct link than by calling. Check it on a phone. Most people find you when they are on their phones. The link on your website has to be the exact same link in your Google Business Profile. Inconsistency messes up how Google sees your business.
