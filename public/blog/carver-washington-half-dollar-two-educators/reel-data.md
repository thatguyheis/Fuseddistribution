- CW6: The Mint identifies Isaac Scott Hathaway as the coin's designer; the obverse pairs Washington and Carver, and the reverse shows a map of the United States. Source: https://www.usmint.gov/learn/coins-and-medals/commemorative-coins/george-washington-carver-booker-t-washington-half
