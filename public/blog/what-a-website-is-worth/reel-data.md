- text: "76% Visit Within 24 Hours of a Local Search"
  narration: 76 percent of people who find a local business on their phone visit within 24 hours. 28 percent make a purchase from that search. The window between someone finding you online and walking through your door is less than a day.
- text: "90% of Visitors Leave a 5-Second Page"
  narration: When a page takes five seconds to load, 90 percent of visitors leave before seeing anything. At one second, only 9 percent leave. That's not a technical problem — it's a revenue leak hiding in plain sight.

  narration: The average service business saves 4.5 hours per week by switching to online booking alone. Stacked with automated follow-up emails, digital intake forms, and online payments, that compounds to 10 to 15 hours per week — 13 to 20 thousand dollars annually in labor costs.
