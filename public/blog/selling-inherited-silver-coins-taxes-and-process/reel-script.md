# Reel Script: selling-inherited-silver-coins-taxes-and-process
format: long-form
segments: 8
target-duration: 136s

---
## HOOK
**Visual:** Inherited silver sale checklist
**Duration:** 6s minimum
Narration: Selling inherited silver starts with authority and records, not a buyer’s offer.

---
## OVERLAY: CONFIRM AUTHORITY
**Visual:** Estate appointment and distribution documents
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Confirm whether the executor, trustee, or beneficiary is authorized to sell. Read the will, trust, appointment, and distribution documents before moving or pricing the coins. A family relationship alone does not settle authority.

---
## OVERLAY: INVENTORY THE LOT
**Visual:** Inventory worksheet and coin groups
**Duration:** 17s minimum
Narration: Photograph the collection and assign group IDs. Record count, denomination, date, mint mark, face value, purity clue, weight, packaging, and condition. Preserve holders, and separate common coins from possible collector pieces.

---
## OVERLAY: CHECK BASIS DATE
**Visual:** Estate appraisal and valuation record
**Duration:** 24s minimum
Narration: Ask the executor for the estate’s valuation and basis records. Confirm the valuation date and whether an alternate valuation date was elected. Do not replace documented basis with today’s spot price. Map each lot to the estate inventory and keep the appraisal method and supporting documents. Common bullion and rare collectible coins can require different valuation approaches. Preserve the original records so the professional reviewing the sale can explain where each basis amount came from.

---
## OVERLAY: COMPARE OFFERS
**Visual:** Dealer quotes side by side
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Compare written offers that identify the items, pricing basis, fees, shipping, insurance, payment, and return terms. A dealer quote is a market offer, not automatically an independent appraisal.

---
## OVERLAY: RECORD THE SALE
**Visual:** Invoice, shipping receipt, settlement
**Duration:** 24s minimum
Narration: Keep the signed authorization, invoice, shipping receipt, payment confirmation, and final settlement. Record gross proceeds and each fee separately, then reconcile the payment to the item IDs. For a hypothetical example, one thousand dollars of gross proceeds minus fifty dollars of permitted selling expenses and eight hundred dollars of documented basis leaves one hundred fifty dollars of gain. These are teaching figures. The actual tax rate and allowed expenses depend on the seller’s facts.

---
## OVERLAY: REVIEW TAX CHARACTER
**Visual:** Tax worksheet and professional review
**Duration:** 17s minimum
Narration: Inherited property is generally treated as long term for holding period purposes. Collectibles can face a maximum 28 percent long term capital gain rate, while personal use losses and investment losses differ. Ask a tax professional to classify the sale.

---
## QUESTION
**Visual:** Completed sale file
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Text: Which sale record comes first?
Subtext: DOCUMENT THE SALE
Narration: Confirm authority, inventory the coins, preserve basis, compare written offers, and keep gross proceeds separate from fees. Which sale record will you complete first?
