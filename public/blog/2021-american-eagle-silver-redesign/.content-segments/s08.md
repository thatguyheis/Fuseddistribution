The Mint’s 2021 transition set offered a unique opportunity to observe the shift in the American Silver Eagle’s design. Presented as a two-coin set, it showcased both the original and the new reverse designs side-by-side. This deliberate pairing allowed collectors to directly compare the two distinct imagery choices, highlighting the significant change occurring within the coin’s design. 

The set included a reverse proof coin from both the Philadelphia and San Ashton mints, each utilizing the 99.9 percent silver composition. This dual-mint production, combined with the visual comparison, provided a tangible representation of the redesign process. 

Crucially, this two-coin set didn’t simply display the new reverse; it demonstrated the institution’s approach to introducing the change, offering a clear and accessible way for collectors to understand the transition.
