The Mint presented the transition between the original and redesigned American Silver Eagle coins through a special two-coin set, the 2021 reverse-proof set. This set paired the original design, struck at West Point, with the new reverse design, struck at San Francisco. Both coins were composed of 99.9 percent silver, reflecting the bullion standard of the American Silver Eagle. This dual presentation allowed collectors to directly compare the two distinct versions created at the two different U.S. Mint facilities. The reverse proof set was specifically designed to highlight this change in design, offering a tangible representation of the redesign process.
