The 2021 redesign of the American Silver Eagle’s obverse represents a subtle but significant shift in the coin’s design history. The Mint’s description - that the obverse returned to Adolph A. Weinman’s 1916 Walking Liberty design and utilized source artwork - highlights a deliberate effort to achieve a more faithful rendering of the original. This move acknowledged the earlier design’s prominence and the subsequent alterations made during previous redesigns. 

Previously, the 2017 redesign had departed from Weinman’s original, introducing stylistic changes to the figure’s drapery. Returning to the source artwork signaled a commitment to honoring the foundational design and correcting what was perceived as a deviation from the intended aesthetic. 

This decision underscored the Mint’s recognition of Weinman’s enduring legacy and the importance of maintaining a connection to the coin’s historical roots. It demonstrated a desire to present a version of the Silver Eagle that closely aligned with its initial conception.
