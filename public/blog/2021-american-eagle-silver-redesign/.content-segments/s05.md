The obverse changes more quietly than the dramatic shift on the reverse. Following the redesign, the Mint returned to the original sculptural source material established by Augustus Saint- Gaudens for the American Silver Eagle. This meant a restoration of the details previously altered by Mercanti’s heraldic reverse. Specifically, the depiction of Liberty’s profile was refined to align with Weinman’s original design, bringing back the familiar features that had been modified in earlier iterations. 

This adjustment was part of a broader effort to ensure the coin’s iconic representation. The Damstra/Gaudioso 2021 design incorporated these restored details, effectively undoing the earlier changes while maintaining the overall aesthetic established by Saint- Gaudens. This approach reflected a commitment to preserving the established legacy of the American Silver Eagle.
