The change in the American Silver Eagle’s reverse design in 2021 involved a distinct new imagery. Prior to 2021, Mercanti designed a heraldic reverse featuring an eagle and oak branch. Following this, Emily Damstra designed the 2021 reverse, depicting an eagle landing at a nest with an oak branch. Michael Gaudioso sculpted this design. The new reverse presented a different visual narrative than the previous heraldic depiction. 

The Mint’s redesigning of the coin reflected a shift in design periods. The Mint stated that the reverse changed after the law’s 25-year design period while the Walking Liberty obverse remained. This change was facilitated through a specific design process. 

To illustrate this transition, the Mint created a two-coin set. This set paired the original design struck at West Point with the new reverse struck at San Francisco. Both coins were 99.9 percent silver, allowing for a direct comparison of the two designs. The 2021 transition set presented the old and new reverse designs together, making the change between the two designs visible in one release.
