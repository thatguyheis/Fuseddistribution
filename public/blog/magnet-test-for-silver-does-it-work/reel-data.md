  text: MAGNET MOVES GENERATES CURRENT
  narration: As the magnet moves, it generates a current in the metal, and that current creates a counterforce that brakes the slide. What the test does not detect is any fake made from a non-magnetic, conductive metal. Copper, brass, and aluminum all produce eddy currents. A copper round plated with silver will slow the magnet. A copper bar stamped with the right hallmarks will feel identical to real silver under this test.
