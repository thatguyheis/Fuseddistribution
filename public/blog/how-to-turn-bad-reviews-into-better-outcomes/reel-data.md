- type: overlay
  text: BAD REVIEWS DEATH SENTENCE
  narration: Bad reviews are not a death sentence for your business. They are a signal, and how you respond to that signal determines whether you lose that customer forever or win back their trust, along with the respect of everyone watching. Here is the short version: respond fast, take responsibility, offer a real fix, and follow through.
- type: overlay
  text: FIRST TIME HEARD COMPLAINT
  narration: Is this the first time you have heard this complaint? Could this happen again to another customer? A customer who says "the staff was rude and my order was wrong" is giving you two separate pieces of feedback. You cannot fix a pattern you have not identified. If you have multiple negative reviews, paste them into a document and look for words that repeat.
- type: stat
  text: 60 WORDS
  narration: An example that works: "Hi there, thank you for sharing this with us. I am sorry your experience did not meet the standard we hold ourselves to. What you described with specific issue is not acceptable, and I want to make it right. Please reach out to us at your contact information so we can resolve this personally." That is about 60 words.

  text: 70% UNHAPPY CUSTOMERS RETURN BUSINESS
  narration: Once you move the conversation private, you can get into what actually went wrong and offer a real resolution. 70 percent of unhappy customers will return and do business with a company again if their complaint is resolved in their favor . That number is a reminder that the goal of every negative review response is not to win an argument.
- type: stat
  text: 56% CONSUMERS CHANGED OPINION ABOUT
  narration: Second, it gives you something real to say when you follow up with the original reviewer. Going back to a customer to say "we heard you, and here is what we changed" is rare. It turns a one-star review into a loyalty story. 56 percent of consumers changed their opinion about a business based on how the owner responded to a review .
- type: stat
  text: 90 DAYS CUSTOMERS
  narration: Can I replace the item / get a manager / comp your drink?" The key word is "now." Customers who hear "let me check on that" and then wait ten minutes feel abandoned. Customers who see immediate action feel valued. Hold a short team meeting, ten minutes maximum, and walk through the most common complaints you have received in the past 90 days.
- type: stat
  text: 3 THINGS CUSTOMERS CONSISTENTLY PRAISE

If they came back in or gave you contact information, check in.
