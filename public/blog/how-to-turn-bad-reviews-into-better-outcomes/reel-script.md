## OVERLAY: FIRST TIME HEARD COMPLAINT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Is this the first time you have heard this complaint? Could this happen again to another customer? A customer who says "the staff was rude and my order was wrong" is giving you two separate pieces of feedback. You cannot fix a pattern you have not identified. If you have multiple negative reviews, paste them into a document and look for words that repeat.

## STAT: 60 WORDS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: An example that works: "Hi there, thank you for sharing this with us. I am sorry your experience did not meet the standard we hold ourselves to.
