# Behind The Scenes Content Ideas For Small Business
Are you a small business owner struggling to connect with your audience and build trust? You’re not alone. Many small businesses feel like they’re shouting into the void, unsure how to truly show what they do and why it matters. The good news is, you don’t need a massive marketing budget or a team of experts to create engaging content that resonates. Behind-the-scenes content is a powerful way to humanize your brand, build relationships, and drive sales. It offers a glimpse beyond the polished facade of a business, revealing the people, processes, and passion that fuel your operation. Let's explore some specific ideas that will help you start creating this valuable content today. The key is authenticity and showing the *real* you and your business.
## Employee Spotlights: Small Business Stories
People connect with people. Highlighting your team is a fantastic way to build a personal connection and build loyalty. Consider short video interviews where employees share their passion for your business, their roles, and what they love about working with you. A simple question like, “What’s your favorite part of your job?” or “How did you get started?” can go a long way. You can also share employee profiles on your blog, detailing their backgrounds, skills, and how they contribute to your company’s mission. For instance, a local bakery might feature a baker discussing their favorite type of dough or a barista explaining their coffee brewing techniques.
## Day-in-the-Life Content - Behind the Scenes
People want to see *how* you do things, not just *what* you do. A “day in the life” video or blog post offers a peek into your daily operations, providing transparency and building trust. Show your team setting up for the day, working on projects, collaborating, and even taking a quick break. Don’t just highlight the polished moments; show the challenges too. Authenticity is key. For instance, if you’re a bakery, show the kneading of dough, the decorating process, and even the occasional spilled frosting. This demonstrates the effort and care that goes into your products.
## Process Reveals: How We Do It
Customers want to understand *why* you do things a certain way. Break down your key processes - from website design to customer service - into digestible steps. Create short videos or infographics explaining your workflow. For example, if you’re a web design company, you could create a video showing the steps involved in building a website: initial consultation, wireframing, design, development, testing, and launch. You could also explain your SEO process. “We start with keyword research, then build a content strategy, optimize your website, and track your progress.” Transparency builds trust.
## Team Introductions - Meet the Faces
Beyond employee spotlights, dedicate a regular feature to introducing your team members. Include a photo, a short bio (highlighting their role and perhaps a personal interest), and a fun fact about each person. This helps your audience connect with the individuals behind your brand and makes them feel more familiar with your business. You could do this on your blog, on social media, or even on your website’s “About Us” page. Consider a “Team Member of the Month” feature to highlight a different team member each month, showcasing their contributions and personality. This humanizes your business and makes it more relatable.
## Workspace Tours - A Peek Inside
People are curious about where businesses operate and how they function. A virtual tour of your office, studio, or workshop can be a great way to build trust and show your audience that you’re a real, tangible business. Even a quick video showcasing your workspace, highlighting the equipment and atmosphere, can be effective. Don’t feel you need a perfectly staged space - a genuine, lived-in environment is often more appealing. Consider adding captions and music to enhance the viewing experience.
## Mistakes & Lessons Learned - Transparency
Nobody’s perfect. Sharing your mistakes and the lessons you’ve learned along the way builds trust and shows that you’re willing to be vulnerable. Don’t shy away from discussing challenges you’ve faced and how you’ve overcome them. For instance, you could share a story about a website redesign that didn’t go as planned and what you learned from the experience. Be honest and open about your failures - it’s a powerful way to connect with your audience. ## Product Creation - From Concept to Completion
Take your audience along for the ride as you create your products or services. Show the initial concept, the design process, the manufacturing steps, and the final product. This gives your audience a deeper understanding of what you do and the value you provide. If you’re a furniture maker, you could document the process of selecting materials, designing a piece, and building it from start to finish. ## Small Business Challenges - Honest Discussions
Small business owners face unique challenges - marketing, finances, staffing, and more. Openly discussing these challenges - and offering potential solutions - can resonate with your audience and build a sense of community. You could host a live Q&A session on social media or create a blog post addressing common challenges faced by small businesses. Sharing your struggles and offering insights can position you as a trusted advisor.
## Q&A Sessions - Ask Us Anything
Give your audience a chance to ask questions and get answers directly from you. Host a live Q&A session on social media, or create a dedicated “Ask Us Anything” section on your website. This is a great way to address common concerns, dispel myths, and build trust. You could also collect questions in advance through social media polls or email. Responding to questions promptly and thoroughly demonstrates your commitment to customer service.
## “Making Of” Content - Project Reveals
Showcase the details of a specific project from start to finish. This could be a website redesign, a marketing campaign, or a new product launch. Break down the process into smaller steps and share your insights along the way. This provides valuable behind-the-scenes content and demonstrates your expertise.
## Comparing Website Design Costs
Let’s look at some realistic cost comparisons. A basic, single-page website built with a website builder like Wix or Squarespace can cost as little as $15 per month. A more reliable website with custom design and functionality, built with WordPress and a premium theme, might cost $200-$500 per year. A fully custom website, designed and developed from scratch, can range from $3,000 to $10,000 or more. Understanding these price ranges helps customers make informed decisions.
## Shareability Metrics
Track the performance of your behind-the-scenes content. Pay attention to metrics like shares, likes, comments, and saves. This data will help you understand what resonates most with your audience and refine your content strategy. Aim for a share-to-like ratio of at least 2:1 - meaning that for every like you receive, you should aim to get two shares. This indicates that your content is truly resonating with your audience and driving them to spread the word.
Ready to start creating behind-the-scenes content that connects with your audience? Start small, be authentic, and focus on providing value. Don’t be afraid to experiment and see what resonates with your followers. By consistently sharing your story, you can build trust, strengthen your brand, and drive sales.
