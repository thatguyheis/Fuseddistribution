  text: SMALL BUSINESS OWNER STRUGGLING
  narration: Are you a small business owner struggling to connect with your audience and build trust? Many small businesses feel like they’re shouting into the void, unsure how to truly show what they do and why it matters. The good news is, you don’t need a massive marketing budget or a team of experts to create engaging content that resonates.
- type: overlay
  text: HIGHLIGHTING TEAM FANTASTIC WAY
  narration: Highlighting your team is a fantastic way to build a personal connection and build loyalty. Consider short video interviews where employees share their passion for your business, their roles, and what they love about working with you. A simple question like, “What’s your favorite part of your job?” or “How did you get started?” can go a long way.
- type: overlay
  text: DAY LIFE VIDEO BLOG
  narration: A “day in the life” video or blog post offers a peek into your daily operations, providing transparency and building trust. Show your team setting up for the day, working on projects, collaborating, and even taking a quick break. Don’t just highlight the polished moments; show the challenges too. For instance, if you’re a bakery, show the kneading of dough, the decorating process, and even the occasional spilled frosting.
- type: overlay
  text: INCLUDE PHOTO SHORT BIO
  narration: Include a photo, a short bio (highlighting their role and perhaps a personal interest), and a fun fact about each person. This helps your audience connect with the individuals behind your brand and makes them feel more familiar with your business. You could do this on your blog, on social media, or even on your website’s “About Us” page.
- type: overlay
  text: EVEN QUICK VIDEO SHOWCASING
  narration: Even a quick video showcasing your workspace, highlighting the equipment and atmosphere, can be effective. Don’t feel you need a perfectly staged space - a genuine, lived-in environment is often more appealing. Consider adding captions and music to enhance the viewing experience. Sharing your mistakes and the lessons you’ve learned along the way builds trust and shows that you’re willing to be vulnerable.
- type: overlay
  text: TAKE AUDIENCE ALONG RIDE
  narration: Take your audience along for the ride as you create your products or services. Show the initial concept, the design process, the manufacturing steps, and the final product. This gives your audience a deeper understanding of what you do and the value you provide. If you’re a furniture maker, you could document the process of selecting materials, designing a piece, and building it from start to finish.
- type: overlay
  text: GREAT WAY ADDRESS COMMON
  narration: This is a great way to address common concerns, dispel myths, and build trust. You could also collect questions in advance through social media polls or email. Responding to questions promptly and thoroughly demonstrates your commitment to customer service. Showcase the details of a specific project from start to finish. This could be a website redesign, a marketing campaign, or a new product launch.
