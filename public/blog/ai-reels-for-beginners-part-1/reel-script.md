# Reel Script: AI Reels for Beginners, Part 1
format: long-form
segments: 7
target-duration: 115s

## HOOK
**Visual:** A real work clip, then a clean five step path graphic.
**Duration:** 5s minimum

Narration: Your first AI reel does not start with a prompt. It starts with something real from your business.

---

## OVERLAY: REAL WORK FIRST
Narration: Gather photos and short clips of the work you actually do: a product, a project, a tool in use, or a question you answer every week. AI can help shape the material, but it does not know what happened at your job site. Check every fact and every detail before a video goes live.

---

## OVERLAY: APPROVE THE TOPICS
Narration: Next, write down the questions customers already ask. Try thirty ideas as a first list, then approve a few. Give each one a clear audience and one useful answer. Keep private customer details out, and mark anything that needs a current fact check.

---

## OVERLAY: LABEL YOUR B ROLL
Narration: Build one labeled folder of supporting footage. Name a clip for what it truly shows, record its source and permission, and reuse it only when the scene fits the words. Stock or illustrations should never pretend to be your real customer or your real work.

---

## OVERLAY: CHOOSE A VOICE
Narration: Choose your own recorded voice or one permitted narration voice and keep it consistent. Listen to the final track. Check names, numbers, pronunciation, missing words, and pauses. Captions should match the voice and stay easy to read on a phone.

---

## OVERLAY: MAKE THREE TESTS
Narration: Then make three test reels from approved topics. Review the facts, opening, pacing, captions, and every visual. Fix the largest problem before you make more. A small test batch teaches you about the workflow without filling your calendar with unreviewed videos.

---

## OVERLAY: PICK A SUSTAINABLE CADENCE
Narration: You may hear that two posts a day is the magic number. It is not a guarantee. YouTube says there is no minimum Shorts cadence and recommends a schedule you can sustain. Start with a pace you can review well, then use your own analytics to learn.

---

## CTA
Narration: In part two, I walk through the full workflow and its flowchart. Start with real clips, then choose the first customer question you want to answer.
