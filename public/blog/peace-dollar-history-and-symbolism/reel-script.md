## OVERLAY: 1921 / A POSTWAR MESSAGE

**Visual:** 1921 / A POSTWAR MESSAGE
Text: 1921 / A POSTWAR MESSAGE
Narration: The Peace dollar arrived in 1921, after World War One. It commemorated peace between the United States and Germany, giving an everyday denomination a message about what should follow the fighting.

---

## OVERLAY: ANTHONY DE FRANCISCI

**Visual:** ANTHONY DE FRANCISCI
Text: ANTHONY DE FRANCISCI
Narration: Sculptor Anthony de Francisci created the design selected by the Commission of Fine Arts. Treasury Secretary Andrew Mellon approved it in December of that year.

---

## OVERLAY: LIBERTY. EAGLE. OLIVE BRANCH.

**Visual:** LIBERTY. EAGLE. OLIVE BRANCH.
Text: LIBERTY. EAGLE. OLIVE BRANCH.
Narration: On the front, Liberty wears a crown of rays. On the back, an eagle rests on a rocky summit, holding an olive branch and facing the light of a new day. Beneath it is the word PEACE.

---

## OVERLAY: THE EAGLE AT REST

**Visual:** THE EAGLE AT REST
Text: THE EAGLE AT REST
Narration: Those details make the reverse more than a picture of America's national bird. The eagle is at rest, and the branch gives the coin's message a visible symbol.

---

## OVERLAY: THE SWORD THAT DISAPPEARED

**Visual:** THE SWORD THAT DISAPPEARED
Text: THE SWORD THAT DISAPPEARED
Narration: But an earlier version included something that never reached the finished coins: a broken sword in the eagle's grasp.

---

## OVERLAY: PEACE OR DEFEAT?

**Visual:** PEACE OR DEFEAT?
Text: PEACE OR DEFEAT?
Narration: According to the Smithsonian, many Americans interpreted that broken weapon as a sign of defeat and protested. A design celebrating peace risked telling a very different story to the people looking at it.

---

## OVERLAY: MORGAN CHANGES THE HUB

**Visual:** MORGAN CHANGES THE HUB
Text: MORGAN CHANGES THE HUB
Narration: There was no time to make new models. Mint engraver George Morgan, working under de Francisci's supervision, cut the sword away from the steel hub and shaped olive leaves from what remained. The Smithsonian records that the alteration took a full day.

---

## OVERLAY: A SYMBOL YOU CANNOT SEE

**Visual:** A SYMBOL YOU CANNOT SEE
Text: A SYMBOL YOU CANNOT SEE
Narration: That means an important part of this coin's history is a symbol you cannot see on the finished dollar.

---

## OVERLAY: 1921 TO 1935

**Visual:** 1921 TO 1935
Text: 1921 TO 1935
Narration: The original Peace dollar series was struck from 1921 through 1928, then again in 1934 and 1935.

---

## OVERLAY: A MESSAGE IN SILVER

**Visual:** A MESSAGE IN SILVER
Text: A MESSAGE IN SILVER
Narration: Its story shows how much meaning a small design can carry. A broken sword suggested defeat to critics. An eagle, an olive branch, and a new dawn carried the message forward: peace.
