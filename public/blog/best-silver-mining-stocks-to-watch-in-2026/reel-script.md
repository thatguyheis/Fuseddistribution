**Visual:** Coeur Mining
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Coeur combines silver and gold operations. Reconcile portfolio changes and acquisitions in current filings before using older production totals. Evaluate mine costs, capital needs, and financing separately.

## OVERLAY: HECLA MINING
**Visual:** Hecla Mining
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Hecla reports on Greens Creek, Lucky Friday, and Keno Hill. Compare individual grades, throughput, capital needs, and operating risks instead of relying only on a company total.

## OVERLAY: COMPARE DEFINITIONS
**Visual:** Compare Definitions
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Use the same reporting period and understand each cost measure. Byproduct credits, attributable production, and capital spending can make headline figures difficult to compare directly.

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE BALANCE SHEET
**Visual:** Check The Balance Sheet
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Read cash, debt, maturities, share count, reclamation obligations, and capital commitments. A production increase can require financing and does not guarantee a better shareholder result.

---
## OVERLAY: WRITE WHAT COULD CHANGE
**Visual:** Write What Could Change
**Duration:** 16s minimum
Narration: Separate producing assets from projects and guidance from results. Record what evidence would challenge your view, including cost increases, permitting delays, dilution, or operating interruptions.
