format: long-form

## HOOK

**Visual:** Silver invoice checklist and order record
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: A clear silver bullion invoice record can save you from trying to remember what you bought months later. Keep the invoice beside its order confirmation and delivery details. This checklist helps you find and compare documents. It does not decide your tax treatment or guarantee that every invoice detail is complete.

---

## OVERLAY: START WITH THE SOURCE INVOICE

**Visual:** Invoice number, date and dealer name
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Record the purchase date, invoice number, and dealer name. Keep the original invoice unchanged. Add the dealer contact shown on the document, and match the invoice to the order confirmation and payment receipt. If a detail is missing, write that it is not shown and ask the dealer. Do not fill gaps from memory.

---

## OVERLAY: LIST EACH PRODUCT LINE

**Visual:** Product description, quantity, weight and purity
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: For every line, save the product description and quantity. Copy any stated weight and purity as written. Keep item price, discounts, tax, shipping, handling, and listed insurance charges separate. Do not guess a missing specification or treat a product nickname as proof of purity, coverage, or value.

---

## OVERLAY: CONNECT THE SHIPPING RECORDS

**Visual:** Carrier reference with package delivery notes
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Add the carrier and tracking reference, then record the shipping and delivery dates when known. Keep delivery notices, seller messages, and any condition notes with the invoice. Carrier rules and seller terms vary by service. A listed shipping or insurance fee alone does not establish that a particular loss is covered.

---

## OVERLAY: FILE A FINDABLE COPY

**Visual:** Secure folder with index and backup
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: File the source documents in one secure folder or paper record. Use a neutral filename with the date and invoice number, without payment account details. Make a separate backup you control. If the seller corrects an invoice, retain the earlier version and mark which copy is current instead of silently overwriting the record.
