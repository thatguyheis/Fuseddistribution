## OVERLAY: DETERMINING LONG TERM VALUE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: Determining the long-term value of silver - whether as paper or physical - is challenging. Historically, silver has performed well as a hedge against inflation and economic uncertainty. However, past performance is not indicative of future results. Factors such as changes in industrial demand, technological advancements, and government policies can all impact silver’s value. Many analysts believe physical silver offers a more stable and predictable long-term investment due to its intrinsic value.
