## OVERLAY: CAC is only one view
**Visual:** CAC formula beside customer outcome fields.
**Duration:** 30s
Narration: Customer acquisition cost, or CAC, is defined marketing cost divided by attributed new customers. It can help you compare a channel, but it is not a complete business score. BDC lists CAC alongside customer lifetime value and average order value, and also points owners toward customer satisfaction and churn. The practical lesson is simple: connect the number to the business goal before you let it choose the winner.

---
## OVERLAY: Keep scope and dates equal
**Visual:** Two channel rows with matching dates and cost scope.
**Duration:** 30s
Narration: Before comparing channels, write the rules. Use the same start and end dates, the same definition of a new customer, and the same cost scope. Decide whether that scope includes media, creative, software, staff time, or fees. If one channel is measured on ad spend only and another includes the work around it, the lower CAC may simply reflect a narrower calculation. If the sales cycle is longer, label this week’s result preliminary.

## OVERLAY: Fictional arithmetic only
**Visual:** Fictional comparison: A, CAC 20 and contribution 30; B, CAC 30 and contribution 90.
**Duration:** 30s
Narration: Here is fictional arithmetic, simplified and excluding other business costs. Option A has a CAC of twenty dollars and a first order contribution of thirty, leaving ten after acquisition cost. Option B has a CAC of thirty and contribution of ninety, leaving sixty. A wins on CAC. B leaves more in this narrow example. These are not real business results, a benchmark, or a forecast. Contribution, repeat buying, and the timing of cash still need their own review.

---
## OVERLAY: Check conversion quality and sample
**Visual:** Conversion types separate from paying customers; small sample warning.
**Duration:** 30s
Narration: Check what your denominator means. Google Ads says “All conversions” can include actions outside the main Conversions column, including certain calls, store visits, and view through conversions. Segment by conversion action before calling the count customers. Then inspect the sample. One or two records can move CAC sharply, and a new campaign may not have had time to convert. Record unknown attribution instead of assigning credit to the channel that looks best.
