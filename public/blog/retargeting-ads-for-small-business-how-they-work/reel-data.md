  text: RETARGETING ADS POWERFUL TOOL
  narration: Retargeting ads are a powerful tool for small businesses. They allow you to show ads specifically to people who have already shown interest in your products or visited your website. This strategy focuses your advertising spend on warm audiences, making your marketing efforts much more effective. We will break down exactly how this works, what you need to set up, and how to measure if it is working for your local business.
- type: overlay
  text: SMALL BUSINESS OWNER MAIN
  narration: For a small business owner, the main goal is to bring those interested visitors back to your site and encourage a purchase. You are not wasting money on people who have no prior knowledge of your brand. You are targeting a specific group of people who have already demonstrated some level of intent.
- type: overlay
  text: VISITOR LOADS SITE PIXEL
  narration: When a visitor loads your site, this pixel tells the ad platform, "This person is interested in my business." The ad platform then uses this data to build a list of people who have visited. The ad system then serves your ads only to those individuals on other websites or social media platforms. This targeted approach is what makes retargeting so efficient for small operations.
- type: overlay
  text: SEGMENTING AUDIENCE BASED BEHAVIOR
  narration: By segmenting your audience based on their behavior, you can create much more precise ads. This specificity increases the likelihood that the ad will resonate with the user because the ad speaks directly to their demonstrated interest. Setting up retargeting requires a few key steps. First, you need a platform that supports retargeting. Many advertising tools offer this feature built in, which simplifies the process for small business owner.
- type: overlay
  text: MUST RELEVANT SPECIFIC PAGE
  narration: The ad must be relevant to the specific page they visited. For instance, if they looked at your plumbing services, the ad copy should focus on plumbing solutions rather than general home repair. There are several ways you can apply retargeting to achieve different goals. One common strategy is the abandoned cart ad. This is when you show ads to people who started a purchase but did not complete it.
- type: overlay
  text: SUCCESS ABOUT NUMBER CLICKS
  narration: Success is not just about the number of clicks. Did the retargeting ads lead to new website visits? More importantly, did they lead to actual sales? Track the cost per acquisition for these retargeted users. For more on this, see How To Make Reels For Your Local Business. We can look at key metrics to determine performance.
- type: stat
  text: 10 TO 15 % TOTAL ADVERTISING BUDGET RETARGETING
  narration: Start small to test what works best for your specific audience. A good starting point is allocating 10 to 15 percent of your total advertising budget to retargeting campaigns. If you are running a campaign targeting abandoned carts, set a daily budget. For example, spend $150 per day on ads that target users who left items in their cart.
- type: stat
  text: 10 % OFF SALE LANDING PAGE
  narration: If the ad promises a 10 percent off sale, the landing page must clearly show that discount. This consistency builds trust and reduces bounce rates. Retargeting works best when it supports your main goals. Do not run retargeting campaigns in isolation. Connect them to your email marketing efforts. For example, you can retarget people who abandoned a cart and then send them a follow-up email with a helpful tip.
