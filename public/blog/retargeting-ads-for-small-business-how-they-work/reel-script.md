## OVERLAY: VISITOR LOADS SITE PIXEL

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: When a visitor loads your site, this pixel tells the ad platform, "This person is interested in my business." The ad platform then uses this data to build a list of people who have visited. The ad system then serves your ads only to those individuals on other websites or social media platforms. This targeted approach is what makes retargeting so efficient for small operations.
