## OVERLAY: CANADIAN SILVER MAPLE LEAF

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: A Canadian Silver Maple Leaf purchase starts with three checks: identify the exact coin, compare the full delivered price, and decide how you will store and eventually sell it. The coin's silver content is only one part of the decision. Fees, the dealer's terms, your custody arrangements, and the price a buyer will offer later all matter. This guide concerns the standard bullion coin.

---

## OVERLAY: RECORD QUOTED COIN PRICE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Record the quoted coin price separately from payment charges, shipping, insurance during transit, and any tax shown at checkout. Divide the complete order total by the number of coins to compare delivered cost for the same product. An apparently cheaper listing can change position after checkout costs are included. Use quotes collected close together in time and for the same quantity.

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE SELLER REMEDY

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: Read the process for a missing shipment or an item that differs from its description. Keep copies of the terms that apply to your order. If the seller changes payment details, confirm the change through an independently verified contact route. Be cautious about pressure to act immediately or promises of an assured gain. A product name and a photograph do not establish who is receiving your payment.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK AUTHENTICITY AND STORAGE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: When arranging an authenticity assessment, ask what it can establish and keep the result with the purchase record. Avoid altering or damaging a coin while trying to prove authenticity yourself. A clear seller remedy is particularly useful when the item is still within an agreed inspection period. Decide where the coins will go before they arrive. Compare access, physical security, record keeping, and any ongoing cost for each storage option you are considering.

---

## OVERLAY: KEEP PURCHASE RECORDS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: Store copies of correspondence about authenticity, condition, shipping problems, or replacement items alongside the invoice. A short, consistent record for each purchase is easier to use later than a collection of screenshots without dates or explanations. Before adding to a holding, ask potential buyers how they quote for the product and condition you intend to purchase.
