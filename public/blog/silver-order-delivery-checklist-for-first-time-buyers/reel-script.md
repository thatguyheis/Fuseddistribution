## HOOK

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 14s minimum

Narration: Before your first silver delivery, plan the handoff as carefully as the order. Know what should arrive, who can receive it and how to report a problem. Keep the confirmation and the receiving instructions together until you have checked the contents.

---

## OVERLAY: SAVE THE ORDER

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Record the product, quantity, stated weight and purity, and any promised packaging. Save the final total and your order number privately. If the listing allows different designs or makers, clarify what you should expect. Keep the seller official contact channel with the confirmation so you can find it quickly.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK RECEIVING TERMS

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Confirm the address, recipient and signature requirement before dispatch. Ask the seller before changing delivery to a pickup location or leaving instructions for an unattended parcel. Those changes can affect the receiving arrangement. Read the specific terms rather than assuming every shipment has the same protection after it arrives.

---

## OVERLAY: FOLLOW THE TRACKING

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Separate payment clearing, dispatch and carrier transit estimates. A label being created is different from the carrier receiving the parcel. Check tracking through a verified channel. For an unexpected request for payment or credentials, navigate to the official service yourself. Contact the seller promptly about problems and applicable reporting deadlines.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE CONTENTS

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Prepare a private place to inspect the order. Follow the seller instructions if the parcel appears damaged, and retain relevant packaging and photographs. Compare the contents with the invoice and listing. A visual check may reveal a mismatch, but it does not establish the metal purity or prove authenticity.

---

## OVERLAY: RESOLVE AND RECORD

**Visual:** Original delivery checklist diagram
**Duration:** 17s minimum

Narration: Report missing items or discrepancies through the seller official support route. Provide the order reference and ask which evidence is needed. Get return instructions before shipping anything back, including packaging, costs and protection. Keep unresolved issues open in your records even when tracking says delivered. Store the items under your planned arrangement.
