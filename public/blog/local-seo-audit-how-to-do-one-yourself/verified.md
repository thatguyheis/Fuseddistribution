## What to Know About Local SEO
Local SEO is all about optimizing your online presence to attract customers in your immediate geographic area. It’s not just about ranking for general terms like “pizza”; it’s about ranking for “pizza near me” or “best Italian restaurant in {Your City}.” Google is the dominant search engine, and its local search results are crucial for driving local traffic. A recent survey found that 77% of local searches lead to a purchase. This audit will help you identify [areas](/blog/neighborhood-seo-targeting-specific-areas/) for improvement, giving you a competitive edge.
## How to Get Started: A Step-by-Step Guide
1. **Google Search Yourself:** Start by searching for your business name on Google. What comes up? Is it your website? Is it a listing from Yelp or another directory? This is your baseline.
2. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Check:** This is *critical*. If you don’t have one, create one immediately. It’s free and gives you control over how your business appears in Google Maps and local search results. Ensure your information is accurate - address, phone number, website, hours of operation. A recent survey found that 85% of consumers use Google Business Profiles to find local businesses.
3. **Website Audit:** Let’s assess your website’s SEO friendliness.
4. **Keyword Research:** What terms are your ideal customers using to find your business?
5. **Citation Audit:** Where is your business listed online?
6. **Mobile Friendliness Check:** Is your website easy to use on smartphones and tablets?
7. **Page Speed Test:** How quickly does your website load?
## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) - The Foundation
Your GBP is the foundation of your local SEO. Here’s what to focus on:
* **Accuracy:** Double-check *everything*. Address, phone number, website URL - it must be 100% correct.
* **Categories:** Choose the most relevant categories for your business. Don’t just pick the first one that seems right. More specific categories help Google understand what you do.
* **Description:** Write a compelling description that includes your keywords. Don’t just list your services; tell a story about your business. Aim for around 750 characters.
* **Photos:** High-quality photos are essential. Showcase your business, your products, and your team. Add at least 10 photos.
* **Posts:** Regularly create posts on your GBP to announce promotions, events, or new products. Posts help you stay top-of-mind with local customers.
## 2. Website Audit: Is Your Site SEO-Friendly?
Your website is your digital storefront. It needs to be optimized for local search.
* **NAP Consistency:** Ensure your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are consistent across your website and all online listings. Inconsistencies can confuse Google.
* **Local Keywords:** Use local keywords throughout your website content - city name, neighborhood, and relevant service terms. For example, if you’re a plumber in Austin, use “Austin plumber,” “Austin plumbing services,” etc.
* **Schema Markup:** This is code that helps Google understand your website’s content. Implementing local business schema markup can improve your visibility in search results.
* **Mobile Responsiveness:** Your website *must* be mobile-friendly. Google prioritizes mobile-first indexing. Use Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test tool to check.
* **Page Speed:** Slow websites frustrate users and hurt your rankings. Use Google PageSpeed Insights to identify areas for improvement. A fast website loads in under 3 seconds.
## 3. Keyword Research: What Are People Searching For?
Knowing what your customers are searching for is crucial.
* **Brainstorm:** List all the terms people might use to find your business.
* **Google Keyword Planner:** Use Google Keyword Planner to research search volume and competition for your keywords.
* **Google Trends:** See what’s trending in your local area.
* **Local Keyword Tools:** Explore tools like Ubersuggest or Ahrefs for more advanced keyword research. Focus on long-tail keywords - longer, more specific phrases (e.g., “best organic coffee shop downtown Seattle”).
## 4. Citation Audit: Where Are You Listed?
Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number on other websites. They’re a significant ranking factor.
* **Major Directories:** List your business on Yelp, TripAdvisor, Facebook, Yellow Pages, and other relevant directories.
* **Local Business Listings:** Check for listings on local chambers of commerce, industry associations, and community websites.
* **Review Sites:** Monitor and respond to reviews on Yelp, Google, and other review sites.
## 5. Mobile Friendliness Check
A significant portion of local searches happen on mobile devices. For more on this, see [Local Keyword Research For Small Businesses](/blog/local-keyword-research-for-small-businesses/).
* **Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test:** Use this tool to see if your website is mobile-friendly: [https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly/](https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly/)
* **Responsive Design:** Ensure your website adapts to different screen sizes.
## 6. Page Speed Test
Website speed is a ranking factor and impacts user experience.
* **Google PageSpeed Insights:** [https://pagespeed.google.com/insights/](https://pagespeed.google.com/insights/)
* **GTmetrix:** [https://gtmetrix.com/](https://gtmetrix.com/)
## Common Mistakes to Avoid
* **Ignoring Google Business Profile:** Seriously, don’t.
* **Inconsistent NAP Information:** This is a major ranking penalty.
* **Not Using Local Keywords:** You’re missing out on valuable traffic.
* **Neglecting Mobile Optimization:** Most local searches are done on mobile.
* **Ignoring Reviews:** Reviews influence local search rankings and customer trust.
* **Lack of Content:** Create valuable content that addresses your customers' needs and interests.
## Measuring Your Success
Tracking your progress is essential. Monitor your website traffic, Google Business Profile views, and search rankings. Use Google Search Console to see how your website is performing in search results. Regularly update your GBP and website content to stay ahead of the competition.
