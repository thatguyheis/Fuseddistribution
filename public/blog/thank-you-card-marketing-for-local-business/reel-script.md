## STAT: 35% CUSTOMERS LIKELY LEAVE REVIEW

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: Don’t simply state, “Leave us a review.” Instead, provide clear, actionable instructions. Include a direct link to your Google review page - make it as easy as possible for them to do it. Surveys have found that customers who received a personalized thank you note were 35 percent more likely to leave a review than those who didn’t. This isn’t about nagging; it’s about guiding them to a simple, beneficial action.

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: If you’re happy with the work, leaving a quick review on Google helps other local folks find us.” This demonstrates genuine care and builds trust. Then, give them a clear next step. ## OVERLAY: SOMETHING LIKE THANK MUCH

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Something like, “Thank you so much for choosing {Your Business Name} for your {Service}.” Then, immediately connect it to the benefit they received. “We’re thrilled we could help you Solve their problem/Achieve their goal.” Next, directly ask for a review.
