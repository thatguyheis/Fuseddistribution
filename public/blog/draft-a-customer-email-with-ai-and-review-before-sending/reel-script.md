# Reel Script: draft-a-customer-email-with-ai-and-review-before-sending
format: long-form
engagement-policy: 1
content-kind: educate

---
## HOOK
**Duration:** 4s
Narration: Would you send an AI reply unchecked?

---
## OVERLAY: Start with a draft
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Choose a low-risk message such as confirming a public event time. Use a fictional customer example while learning, not a real email thread. Replace names, account details, contact information, and other private data with placeholders. Keep AI in the drafting step; a person should control the final review and send action.

---
## OVERLAY: Share approved facts
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Tell the model what the customer asked, which details are confirmed, and what outcome you want. Quote the approved policy or public event page. Do not ask it to guess refunds, warranties, prices, or commitments. If the source does not answer part of the question, tell the model to mark that gap for a person.

---
## OVERLAY: Set tone and format
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Ask for a warm, direct reply with the length and structure you need. Name any required greeting, subject line, or next step. Say which facts must remain unchanged. If the customer asked several questions, request a separate answer to each one and a list of unanswered items after the draft.

---
## OVERLAY: Check every claim
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Compare the draft with the original message and approved source. Verify names, dates, links, amounts, availability, and promises. Remove details that do not belong in the answer. A fluent sentence is not evidence. If the reply adds a fact, find its source or remove it before the email moves forward.

---
## OVERLAY: Route hard cases
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Complaints, safety concerns, refund decisions, legal questions, and private account issues should go to the responsible person. A smooth sounding draft is not approval to decide or send. Put the stop conditions and reviewer role in your SOP, then test the workflow with a fictional example that includes one missing detail.
