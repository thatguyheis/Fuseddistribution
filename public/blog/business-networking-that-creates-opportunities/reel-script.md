# Reel Script: Business Connections That Create Opportunities
format: long-form
content-kind: educate
engagement-policy: 1

---
## HOOK
**Duration:** 8s
Narration: Your next good customer may be one introduction away. But networking works best when people understand who you help and how to introduce you.

---
## STAT: 41% said referrals brought the most leads
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: A 2026 LinkedIn and Ipsos study of 300 U.S. small business decision makers found that 41 percent said referrals brought them the most leads. It is one survey result, not a guarantee for every business.

---
## OVERLAY: Start with people who already know you
**Duration:** 20s
Narration: Start with people who already know you. Make a short list of past customers, former coworkers, suppliers, neighbors, friends, and family. Tell them what problem you solve, who is a good fit, and how to introduce you. Ask whether anyone comes to mind. Share your website or contact details, with no pressure.

---
## OVERLAY: Make your network useful in both directions
**Duration:** 17s
Narration: Then make your network useful in both directions. Ask what customers need next. Connect people when you can help. Thank anyone who introduces you. Follow up promptly and share the outcome when appropriate. Trust grows when people see that you listen and keep your word.

---
## OVERLAY: Meet people through shared interests
**Duration:** 24s
Narration: Meet new people where shared interests already bring them together. Try your chamber of commerce, a neighborhood business group, a trade association, a volunteer project, a sports league, or a community event. Show up consistently. Ask thoughtful questions, learn what others do, and offer a useful idea before asking for business. Follow up with a personal note that mentions your conversation.

---
## OVERLAY: Track what creates opportunities
**Duration:** 15s
Narration: Track a few simple things each month: conversations, introductions, qualified leads, and customers. Notice which relationships create opportunities, then invest time there. Do not measure success by how many business cards you collect.

---
## QUESTION
Text: Who could you help this week?
Engagement-beat: identity
**Duration:** 6s
Narration: Build the habit: reconnect, contribute, follow through. Who could you help this week?
