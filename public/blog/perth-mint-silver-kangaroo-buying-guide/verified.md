## What to Know About Perth Mint Silver Kangaroo Coins
The Perth Mint in Australia has been producing silver coins since 1966, and the Kangaroo series, launched in 1984, quickly became a flagship offering. These coins aren't just bullion; they're also highly collectible. Here’s what makes them stand out:
* **High Purity:** All Perth Mint silver Kangaroo coins are 99.9% pure silver, guaranteeing a high value and resistance to tarnishing.
* **Attractive Designs:** The Kangaroo series features stunning, detailed designs showcasing a kangaroo and its joey, changing annually to reflect the current year. This makes them visually appealing and desirable for collectors.
* **Bullion Status:** They are graded and certified by reputable third-party grading services, providing assurance of authenticity and condition.
* **Limited Mintage:** Perth Mint often produces limited mintage runs, driving up demand and potentially increasing value over time.
* **Historical Significance:** The series has a long and consistent history, making it a reliable investment option.
## How to Get Started: Research and Budgeting
Before you buy, it’s crucial to do your research. Understanding the different Kangaroo series and their pricing is essential.
1. **Identify Your Goals:** Are you looking for a long-term investment, a collectible, or both? This will influence the type of coin you choose.
2. **Explore the Series:** The Perth Mint produces several Kangaroo series, including the Classic, Anniversary, and Millennium. Each series has unique features and pricing. The Classic series, starting in 1984, is a popular choice for beginners.
3. **Check Current Prices:** Silver prices fluctuate daily. Monitor the spot price of silver and compare prices from different dealers. You can find real-time spot prices on reputable financial websites.
4. **Set a Budget:** Determine how much you’re willing to spend. Start small and gradually increase your investment as you become more comfortable. A common starting point is to invest between $100 and $500.
5. **Consider Grading:** Coins in higher grades (Mint State or Uncirculated) command a premium. However, lower-grade coins can still be a good investment, especially if you’re looking for a bargain.

## Authentication and Grading
Ensuring the authenticity of your Perth Mint silver Kangaroo coins is essential. Here’s how:
* **Reputable Dealers:** Purchase from established and trusted dealers.
* **Third-Party Grading:** Consider having your coins graded by PCGS or NGC. These services provide an independent assessment of the coin’s authenticity and condition. A PCGS grade of 90 indicates a near-perfect coin, while a grade of 70 is considered Mint State.
* **Check the Mint Mark:** Verify that the coin has the correct Perth Mint mint mark (P).
