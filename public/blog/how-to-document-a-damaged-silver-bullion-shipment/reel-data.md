# Document damage without losing the record

## hook
A damaged parcel is stressful, but a clear record can make the next conversation easier. Keep the package and contents together, photograph what you see, and record the order details. These steps preserve observations; they do not prove that insurance applies or guarantee a claim payment. Write down the delivery and opening times while they are fresh.

## segments
- type: overlay
 text: PHOTOGRAPH THE ARRIVAL
 narration: Photograph every side, the label, tears, dents and inner wrapping. Keep original image files and note when you took them. If the outside looks damaged but the contents seem intact, record both observations. If the package is already open, say so and record what you found. Do not recreate an unopened delivery scene or discard, clean, repair or reship items before asking what the seller or carrier needs.
- type: overlay
 text: COMPARE WHAT ARRIVED
 narration: Use the invoice, packing slip and tracking record to list each expected item. Note its documented description, weight, purity and quantity, then separately describe what is present and visibly damaged. Do not infer authenticity, silver content, value or cause of damage by appearance alone. Record missing contents separately from condition. Use the seller documents for stated weight and purity, not an appearance test.
- type: overlay
 text: BUILD A DATED FILE
 narration: Save the paid invoice, order, label, delivery notice, seller terms, photographs and messages together. Keep a dated event log with each contact, case number, instruction and next step. The USPS domestic claims guide may require proof of value, coverage and damage; rules and deadlines depend on the mail service.
- type: overlay
 text: ASK WHO HANDLES THE CLAIM
 narration: Contact the seller promptly and ask whether it or the recipient should contact the carrier. Confirm in writing what evidence to save and whether the package must stay available. For bullion, check how the actual carrier classifies the item and what declared value or exclusions apply. A tracking number does not establish coverage.
