## Common Mistakes: Avoiding Costly Errors

Investing in precious metals can be complex. Here are some common mistakes to avoid:

* **Ignoring Premiums:** Don’t just focus on the spot price of silver. Premiums significantly impact your overall cost. Always factor in the premium when evaluating a purchase.
* **Buying from Unverified Dealers:** Stick to reputable dealers like Gainesville Coins to ensure you’re receiving authentic products. Counterfeit silver is a real concern.
* **Not Understanding Grading:** If you’re buying collectible coins, understand the grading scale (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Fine, Very Fine, Mint State) and the factors that influence its value.
* **Failing to Diversify:** While silver is a popular investment, consider diversifying your portfolio with other assets.
* **Emotional Buying:** Avoid making impulsive decisions based on market hype or fear. Stick to your investment strategy.
