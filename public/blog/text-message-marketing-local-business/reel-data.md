## stats
- text: 98% SMS OPEN RATE
  narration: Nine out of ten business texts get opened. The average email open rate for small service businesses sits around 22 percent, according to Mailchimp's 2024 industry benchmark data. That is a 76-point gap, and it is the reason text message marketing works when most other follow-up channels do not.

- text: 90 SECONDS TO READ
  narration: The average customer reads a business text within 90 seconds of receiving it. That attention window is faster than any other marketing channel. At the cost of a text message, nothing else even comes close.

- text: 45% SMS RESPONSE RATE
  narration: Text messages get a 45 percent response rate compared to 6 percent for email. For every 100 customers you reach, nearly half respond. No other channel at $30 a month comes close to that kind of two-way engagement with people who were already your customers.

- text: $1,500 PER MESSAGE FINE
  narration: Before you send a single marketing text, you need explicit written consent. The Telephone Consumer Protection Act requires it. Since 2023, FCC enforcement has increased, with fines up to $1,500 per message for violations. Getting opt-in right is not optional. It is the foundation the whole system sits on.

- text: 3 WAYS TO GET LEGAL OPT-IN
  narration: Three methods work reliably for local businesses. A checkbox at checkout or booking with clear disclosure language. A keyword opt-in where the customer texts a word to your number and gets an automatic confirmation. Or a paper sign-up sheet at the counter for service businesses where the customer is on-site.

- text: 20-30% REPLY RATE DAY ONE
  narration: The first message goes out the same day or next morning. No promotion. Just a direct line back to a real person. This message generates replies 20 to 30 percent of the time, questions about their service, something they forgot to mention, or just a thanks. Every reply is a conversation you can use to solve a problem before it becomes a negative review.

- text: DAY 3-4 SPECIFIC OFFER
  narration: Message two fires on day three or four. A specific offer matters more than you think. Ten percent off your next visit works. Come back soon does not. Give them a concrete reason with a loose deadline. A yes reply books itself.

- text: DAY 28-30 REBOOKING ASK
  narration: The third message fires at day 28 to 30. One-word reply, no friction. A customer who had a good experience a month ago and has not been contacted since is far more likely to rebook. You are not selling to a stranger. You are reminding someone who already chose you.

- text: BELOW 2% OPT-OUT RATE
  narration: Industry data from SimpleTexting shows opt-out rates stay below 2 percent when businesses send four or fewer texts per month. The 3-message sequence keeps you well within that range. The opt-out rate spikes when businesses send daily or weekly promotional blasts that customers did not expect.

- text: STARTS AT $29 PER MONTH
  narration: SimpleTexting starts at $29 a month for 500 texts. EZTexting runs around $24. You write three message templates once and they run automatically after each new opt-in. The only manual work is replying to the conversations they start.
