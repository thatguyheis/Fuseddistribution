## OVERLAY: COMPARE THE EXACT COIN

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: The Austrian Silver Philharmonic is a bullion coin to compare by its exact specification, delivered cost, condition, and resale terms. Start by confirming that a listing is for the silver coin rather than the gold or platinum version. The familiar orchestra design does not tell you whether a particular dealer's price or delivery terms suit your purchase. This guide explains the product and provides a practical way to examine a quote.

---

## OVERLAY: DEFINE WHAT YOU WANT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Decide what you want the purchase to accomplish before comparing listings. A buyer seeking the standard bullion product has a different question from someone seeking a particular year, presentation package, or independently graded example. Paying extra for an attribute makes sense only if that attribute matters to you and you understand what the seller is promising. Write the relevant attributes in your comparison notes.

---

## OVERLAY: CHECK DELIVERY AND PAYMENT TERMS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: If a delivery is missing, ask who handles the investigation, what documentation is needed, and how quickly you must report a problem. Save the terms that apply to your transaction. Be cautious if payment instructions change unexpectedly. Confirm the change using independently checked contact details rather than relying only on the message announcing it. A recognizable coin name does not verify the recipient of your money.

---

## OVERLAY: KEEP PURCHASE RECORDS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Store a copy of your purchase records separately from the physical coins when appropriate for your circumstances. If you later obtain a valuation, label it with its date and source. A valuation is not the same thing as the price you paid or a firm offer to buy. Clear records let you answer those different questions without reconstructing the transaction from an unsorted collection of screenshots.

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 29s minimum

Narration: Keep the finished checklist with the transaction record rather than assuming you will remember the details later. This process also makes comparisons with another bullion coin more useful. Compare the attributes you actually need, not a broad claim that one named coin is always best. For another product example, see the Canadian Silver Maple Leaf guide. Return to the current mint specification and the seller's actual quote for any purchase.
