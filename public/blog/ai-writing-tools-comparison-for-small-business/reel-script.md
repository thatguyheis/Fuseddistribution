## OVERLAY: WRITING TOOLS HELP SMALL

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: AI writing tools can help a small business turn rough notes into a service page, email, proposal, social caption, or internal procedure. A tool that is useful for brainstorming may be poor at preserving a brand voice. A tool that edits grammar may not understand the customer or the offer. The right choice depends on the work you need to repeat and the review process you can maintain.

---

## OVERLAY: LONG FORM PAGES LOOK

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 19s minimum

Narration: If you need long form pages, look for source handling, structure, and review support. If you need many channel versions, look for reusable instructions and predictable formatting. Give each candidate the same brief, the same approved facts, and the same audience. Ask each tool to produce the same small deliverable, such as a service page outline and a customer email.

## OVERLAY: IMPORTANT COMPARISON NAME ALONE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 18s minimum

Narration: The important comparison is not the name alone. Test whether the assistant follows your source material, keeps the requested format, and makes uncertainty visible when the brief is incomplete. If the prompt is vague, the answer may be generic. If the source material is incomplete, the tool may fill the gap with a plausible statement.

---

## OVERLAY: EDITOR MAY IMPROVE SENTENCE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: An editor may improve a sentence without understanding whether the claim is accurate or whether the page is aimed at the right customer. Keep a source and review step even when the AI feature is built into the writing environment. Search focused tools help organize a page around a topic, related questions, and on page structure. They can be useful for a business that publishes regularly and needs a consistent planning process.

---

## OVERLAY: BEFORE ADOPTING ONE CONFIRM

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 20s minimum

Narration: Before adopting one, confirm that the stored examples can be updated, removed, and clearly separated from approved facts. Remove outdated offers, old contact details, and examples that no longer represent the business. Label approved facts separately from style examples so the tool does not treat a sample sentence as current evidence. Choose this category when consistency is a bigger problem than brainstorming.

---

## OVERLAY: CUSTOMER CONTACT DETAILS PRIVATE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Customer contact details, private proposals, account information, unpublished prices, and internal credentials should not be pasted into a service without an approved handling policy. Ask how the tool accepts source material, how access is controlled, and how your team can remove outdated content. Keep sensitive details out of reusable prompts. Replace them with neutral labels when a real value is not needed for the writing task.

---

## OVERLAY: EACH JOB WRITE SOURCE

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 22s minimum

Narration: For each job, write the source material, the audience, the output format, and the review checks. Then run the same brief through each candidate. Score the result on useful criteria: factual control, clarity, editing effort, consistency, privacy fit, and ease of reuse. The purpose is to expose tradeoffs, not create a false impression of scientific precision. Keep the tool that produces a dependable draft and makes mistakes easy to find.

---

## OVERLAY: VARIED HELP START GENERAL

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 23s minimum

Narration: If you need varied help, start with a general assistant. If you need shared approvals, start with an editor your team already uses. If you need search planning, test a search focused tool. If consistency across writers is the problem, test a brand workspace. If the draft is sound but the prose needs cleanup, test an editing tool. That decision rule is more dependable than choosing the tool with the longest feature list.
