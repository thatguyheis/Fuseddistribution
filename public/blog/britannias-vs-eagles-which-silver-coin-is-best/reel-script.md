## OVERLAY: COMPARE THE EXACT OFFERS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: A Silver Britannia and an American Silver Eagle can both be sensible products to compare when you want a government mint bullion coin. The useful question is which exact offer fits your budget, buying conditions, and eventual selling route. A familiar design or a higher advertised price does not answer that question by itself. Start with equivalent bullion versions, then examine the complete transaction.

## OVERLAY: CHECK THE MINT SPECIFICATIONS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: The Royal Mint lists its 2026 one ounce Silver Britannia as 999 fine silver, with a diameter of 38.61 millimeters and a two pound denomination. The U.S. Mint lists the American Silver Eagle bullion coin as one troy ounce of 99.9 percent silver, with a diameter of 40.60 millimeters and a one dollar face value. Check the exact year and version in the seller's listing.

## OVERLAY: COMPARE COMPLETE PURCHASE COSTS

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Choose the same realistic order quantity for each coin. Compare the complete purchase cost, including payment charges, shipping, and any tax shown for your destination. Keep the currency consistent and include any conversion charge. Record when each quote was collected. Then ask potential buyers for the net selling amount for the exact product and condition. A retail premium alone does not establish what you will recover.

## OVERLAY: VERIFY SELLER AND PRODUCT

**Visual:** Supporting article visual
**Duration:** 21s minimum

Narration: Verify the seller's identity, payment recipient, delivery terms, and procedure for an item that differs from the order. A recognizable coin design does not establish the trustworthiness of a listing. If authenticity is uncertain, ask a qualified bullion dealer about an appropriate assessment. Avoid damaging a coin with improvised tests. A marketplace photograph cannot establish what will arrive in your parcel or what remedy will apply.
