## Meta Muse for Small Business

Confirmed by Nick and checked against [Meta’s announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/). Three additional topics are active research seeds; each requires current sources and matching demonstration evidence before publication.

- **Meta Muse for Facebook Page workflow planning**: Can Muse help with your Facebook Page before anything is published?
- **Meta Muse Instagram analytics and content decisions**: Your Instagram views changed. What should Muse check first?
- **Meta Muse and Buffer roles in a publishing workflow**: If Muse helps plan the content, what still belongs in Buffer?

The earlier three generic review ideas are covered by these more specific lessons. Product identification is resolved; account-specific functionality and outcomes still require verification.

Review format rotation: short explainer, single-task walkthrough, misconception correction, measured comparison, incident teardown. Preserve the existing video pipeline and release gates.
