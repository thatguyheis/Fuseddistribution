The story starts with the Walking Liberty half dollar, introduced in nineteen sixteen. Sculptor Adolph Weinman created its design. The United States Mint used that walking figure again when the American Eagle program began in nineteen eighty-six. A familiar image moved from a circulating half dollar to a new silver coin program. The design continued, even though the coin changed.

Start with the shape, before chasing a date or a mint mark. Liberty is shown in full stride, wrapped in the flag. Her right hand reaches outward, and her left arm carries branches.
