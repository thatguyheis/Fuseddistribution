# Presentation scene plan

Nick authorized full reel production after the rough draft. Codex reviewed the primary source references and the exact owner photos. This is a local sales presentation preview; no posting requested in this turn.

1. Hook: approved portrait hero at frame zero, then real fort reverse detail. Move from the inscription to the atmospheric palisade exterior as the narration introduces the anniversary.
2. Origins: exterior illustration, original dated portrait with an animated 1825–1925 timeline, then an illustrated courtyard. All generated plates explicitly say AI illustration; no exact reconstruction claim.
3. Community: modern hand-tool footage for the list of occupations, courtyard illustration for wider context, and modern forest stream footage for atmospheric transition. Stock footage explicitly labeled modern illustrative footage, not footage of Fort Vancouver.
4. Portrait: original McLoughlin photograph, anniversary dates, and tightly cropped holder label. No invented coin detail or certification authenticity claim.
5. Coin design: reverse and portrait photographs, an anniversary timeline, and a quiet fort exterior. The mint fact comes from the U.S. Mint; no tiny mint mark is invented or highlighted.
6. Payoff: alternate the photographed reverse and obverse, ending with the collection question and Fused branding.

Use 1080 by 1920, 30 fps, PNG intermediate frames, CRF 16 H.264 slow preset and 320 kbps AAC. This improves the delivery encode; source resolution limitations remain visible rather than being hidden with artificial coin detail. Keep original owned photo color. Supporting footage may receive mild grading, never an assertion about historical appearance. Use restrained 15-frame dissolves within scenes and existing measured overlaps between scenes; no lost narration. Clear larger captions stay in the lower safe area. Music fades at the opening and closing beneath the approved Nano narration.

Review gates: exact source hashes, local article and script QA, measured speech timing under 135 seconds, local Whisper timestamps plus names/date correction, inspection of all shots and transitions, audio decode and loudness checks, then exact-MP4 visual review and release validation.
