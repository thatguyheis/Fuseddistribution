Launch Guidance

What To Publish First

Start with a tight front door. The strongest move is a clean landing page, a small number of collection pages, and a handful of image-detail pages with licensing CTAs. Do not launch a bloated archive before the best work is curated.

Phase 1 Landing page, 4 collections, and 8 to 12 featured image pages.
Phase 2 Add location-led journal posts and curated packs for business buyers.
Phase 3 Expand into filters, public collection pages, and direct inquiry workflows.
