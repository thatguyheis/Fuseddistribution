About

One Operator. Multiple Businesses.

Fused Distribution is run by one person who handles everything directly. Websites, silver, education. No account managers. No subcontractors. Just the person doing the actual work.

👤

Nick

Founder & Operator

I started Fused by building websites for small businesses. Restaurants, repair shops, contractors, salons. Most of them were paying agencies too much for sites that never got touched after launch. I built a model where you pay a flat monthly rate and the site stays current, the hosting is handled, and you talk to me when something needs to change.

The silver subscription came from my own buying habits. I was sourcing bullion directly and found I could offer better pricing than the brokers by cutting them out. The education work grew from people asking how I was running all of this.

You deal with me directly. There is no coordinator passing messages back and forth or a different person on every call. That is not something I say to sound friendly. It is just how the business is built.

How We Work

What Working Directly With One Person Looks Like

Built before you pay

You see the finished site on a live preview link before you commit to anything. Real design, real content, real pages. Not a wireframe or a mockup.

No one in the middle

I source silver without a broker and build sites without a subcontractor. You talk to the person doing the work from day one and that never changes.

Pay month to month

No annual contract. No lock in. If the service is working for you, you stay. If it is not, you leave. That is the arrangement and it keeps me accountable every month.

More coming

New products get added when I find a sourcing edge or a gap worth filling. Silver, websites, books, and coaching are all running now. There is more on the way.
