## Business and process audit

1. **Views are the approved current objective; conversion measurement is a later gap.** The Worker contains consent-controlled GA loading and real lead/newsletter handlers, but this audit had no GA4, Search Console, sales or subscriber export. The lead handler sets a generic site source, and newsletter pages submit a page label. Neither connects a sale to a specific reel. No form submission or email was sent during this audit. Conversion delivery and downstream sales remain unverified.
2. **Offers exist; conversion intent needs separation.** Technology has a pricing page and contact route. Reserve advertises early access with monthly budgets, not proven booked recurring revenue. Technology content should lead to a relevant website assessment or consultation; silver content should lead to its checklist, newsletter or Reserve inquiry as appropriate. The future caption generator now routes the commercial slot to the technology contact section for technology posts and to Reserve for silver posts. Existing accepted posts were preserved.
3. **The content's differentiator should be evidence the viewer can use.** Demonstrate a real owned website defect and its repair; show a labeled coin specification comparison; provide a usable handover checklist. Use Nick's recorded expertise when footage is supplied. Generic stock imagery and a list of definitions are easy to substitute.
4. **Two audiences need clear paths.** Keep silver and technology series, landing pages and attribution distinct within the present channels. Test that separation before paying for additional channels, tools or production capacity.
5. **The site-to-video connection was incomplete.** The reviewed site contained 370 articles but only 106 actual players. The existing sync stages protected article panels without ensuring deployment, and current scheduling logs no longer contain every historical post. Fresh sent-post evidence recovered older matches. Forty-four new matching panels were deployed and verified, initially bringing the reviewed site to 150 players. The subsequent URL audit found 51 older hosted players returned 404. Their broken players were replaced with a clear availability message, leaving 99 present players: 70 YouTube embeds and 29 hosted videos. The remaining 271 articles lack a usable verified matching player; do not attach a loosely related reel simply to meet a count.
6. **API time was being spent without a dependable measurement result.** Keep the current 21:30 owner snapshot and shared publishing reserve. Do not re-fetch in each audit. Save the failure reason and resume on the next permitted run. Current exact-post-ID attribution now fills linkless captions when an unambiguous scheduling record exists; unknown historical records remain unknown.
7. **Channel effort should follow evidence.** Keep X as low-cost reuse for now: the observed 574 impressions and two clicks do not justify bespoke X production. YouTube and Instagram merit the creative experiment, but neither currently demonstrates profitable acquisition.
8. **Local availability remains an operational dependency.** Prepared Buffer inventory protects social publishing when the Mac is off. Blog release, render completion and local reconciliation still require the Mac and relevant runtime. Batch preparation and exact resumable checkpoints remain preferable to repeated AI checks.

## Creative experiment within existing capacity

Use the next 24 suitable new companion videos, split equally between silver and technology. Within each vertical, alternate six demonstration-first pieces and six current-format controls. Keep duration bounds, voice, music clearance, factual standards, caption review, posting windows and CTA destination comparable. This is a practical pilot, not a randomized causal experiment. Do not rerender or repost old videos to manufacture a baseline.

For the demonstration arm, the opening hero shows the decision or proof, not just the title. By the opening seconds, show one concrete object: a marked-up bill, specification comparison, website screen or worked example, accurately labeled as a demonstration where applicable. Develop one useful decision rather than stacking generic definitions. Preserve the topic-specific closing question and the engagement SOP's opinion requirements.

Suggested new angles, subject to novelty and factual review:

- Silver: compare delivered costs on equivalent products; distinguish fineness from fine weight; document a buyback quote; identify exactly which Maple Leaf product a quote describes.
- Technology: demonstrate a handover access check; show a broken booking path and its repair; test the same approved FAQ in two AI tools; turn one customer question into a useful service-page section.

Record variant, vertical, source hashes, final video hash, post ID, send time and CTA destination in the experiment ledger. Repeated platform copies of a video are not independent creative samples. Do not attribute the revised local source to an older upload.

Review at D+7 after the last relevant post, not simply seven days after starting production. Require six measured videos per arm within a vertical and platform before making a directional decision. Use median D+7 views as the primary selection metric, and report sample counts and spread. Comments, saves and production time are diagnostics, not prerequisites for a reach winner. Prefer the higher-view arm once each has six measured creatives within the same vertical and platform; ties or insufficient coverage remain inconclusive. Missing income or conversion data does not block selection. X impressions remain a separate diagnostic until actual video views are available. A reach winner is not a claim of revenue improvement. Maintain editorial, rights, release and capacity gates.

## Platform considerations verified during this audit

Ordinary URLs in YouTube Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable. Use an accurate profile/channel-link instruction or a supported related-video route; verify the destination before promising it. Do not tell viewers to tap a description link that cannot be tapped. [YouTube link guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en-419).

Use source, medium and campaign parameters consistently on external campaign links. Internal navigation should retain content context without overwriting acquisition source. [Google campaign URL guidance](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en-NA).

Blog embeds improve the reading experience, but a supplementary video does not automatically qualify the article for Google's video results. Do not promise an SEO ranking or traffic gain from this repair. [Google video guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/12/video-is-the-main-content).
