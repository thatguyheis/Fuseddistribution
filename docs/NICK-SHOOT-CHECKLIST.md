# Your first on-camera reel

**Topic:** A cheaper silver quote can still be the more expensive purchase.
**Target:**60–85 seconds. Record vertically,1080p, with the camera around eye level.

## Say this

“I think the lowest advertised premium gets too much credit.

If you're comparing two silver purchases, the number on the product tile is only the starting point. Add delivery, payment charges and any applicable tax. Then compare the same amount of silver, with the same assumptions.

Here's a made-up example, not a current quote: one option costs forty dollars plus ten dollars delivery. Another costs forty-five with delivery included. For that one-item order, the first total is fifty and the second is forty-five.

That doesn't mean the second product is always the better choice. Condition, the seller, your preferences and the terms still matter. A collector might happily pay more for a particular design.

My take: know what you're paying extra for before calling something a bargain.

When you compare silver, are you chasing the lowest delivered cost or a particular coin?”

## Shoot

- Phone vertical, stable on a tripod or books; lens clean; notifications silenced.
- Face a window or soft lamp. Keep the background simple and avoid confidential papers.
- Quiet room, phone close enough for clear speech. Record a10-second sound check and listen before the full take.
- Hold an actual coin or bar only if you want to. Do not call an unidentified piece genuine or quote unverified specs. Keep it steady near your face and avoid glare.
- Speak naturally. One good take is enough; leave two seconds of room before and after speaking.
- Save the original clip. Tell Codex which take to use. No music is needed in the raw recording.
