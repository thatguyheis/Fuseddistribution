## Verified sources and calculations

- [Royal Canadian Mint 2021 Silver Maple Leaf bullion specification](https://www.mint.ca/en/shop/bullion-products/2021-1-ounce-9999-pure-silver-maple-leaf-bullion-coin): named product is 99.99% pure silver. Identify the issue; do not generalize from a plated collectible.
- [US Mint 2025 Silver Eagle Uncirculated specification](https://www.usmint.gov/american-eagle-2025-one-ounce-silver-uncirculated-coin-25EG.html): .999 fine, 99.9% silver. This is a collector uncirculated issue, not a current bullion retail quote.
- [London Assay Office hallmark explanation](https://www.assayofficelondon.co.uk/hallmarking/what-is-a-hallmark): a hallmark comprises component marks and independent testing; UK compulsory components include sponsor, millesimal fineness and assay office marks. Millesimal fineness expresses parts per thousand. Do not confuse a lone purity stamp with a complete UK hallmark or generalize UK rules to every country/product.

Arithmetic: .999 = 99.9%; .9999 = 99.99%; difference 0.09 percentage points, not 0.9 or 9. For a hypothetical equal gross mass of 1000 g, fine silver mass is 999 g versus 999.9 g, a 0.9 g difference. These are mathematical examples, not tested objects or live products.
