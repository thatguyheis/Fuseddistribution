# Discovery Rubric

Score each candidate from 1-10 across:

- Timelessness: Does the core lesson remain useful across generations?
- Modern relevance: Can the idea be naturally reframed for builders, operators, freelancers, and creators?
- Search demand: Is there credible audience pull for the title or theme?
- Business usefulness: Will the book change reader behavior in a way that creates strong word-of-mouth and repeatable demand?
- Adaptation potential: Can the original message survive a setting and voice update without collapsing?
- Rights viability: Is there clear U.S. public-domain confidence and source-text availability?

Prioritize books that combine durable advice, strong adaptation potential, and low rights friction.
