# Rights Checklist

Before a title moves past `researched`, confirm all of the following:

1. U.S. public-domain status has been explicitly reviewed and documented.
2. The verification date and evidence are stored in the registry record.
3. A clean source text or trustworthy edition is available for editorial work.
4. The adaptation plan avoids implying endorsement or authorship by the original author.
5. Packaging copy clearly frames the book as a modern adaptation of a classic work.
6. The title has a differentiation plan beyond formatting: new narrative, annotations, commentary, illustrations, translation, or other meaningful value-add.
7. The title has an AI-use record covering manuscript text, cover art, interior art, translation, and marketing copy.
8. The title avoids real-person names, active brands, trademarks, or recognizable living-person depictions unless cleared separately.
9. Any title first published in or after 1930 has a second rights review with dated evidence and source citations before it moves beyond research.

If any item fails, stop the pipeline.
