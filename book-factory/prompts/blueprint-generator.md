# Adaptation Blueprint Prompt

Generate an adaptation blueprint that preserves the original lesson structure while changing the narrative world for modern builders.

Required sections:

- Core promise of the original work
- Audience lens
- Modern metaphor or setting
- Tone rules
- Core lesson map
- Chapter transformation plan
- Prohibited changes
- Editorial risks

The blueprint must preserve the lesson sequence, simplify jargon, and use concrete examples from business building, freelancing, sales, investing, software, or digital work.
