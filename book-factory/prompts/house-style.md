# House Style

The default style for this pipeline should feel:

- Sharp, confident, and contemporary
- Story-driven rather than abstract
- Grounded in modern work: software, services, online business, investing, freelancing, and creator economics
- Respectful of the original lesson architecture without sounding antique

Patterns from the current sample manuscript:

- Open with a recognizable modern tension.
- Use specific economic details instead of generic motivation.
- Keep the moral lesson simple and repeatable.
- Prefer concrete financial behavior over vague inspiration.
- End chapters with a line worth underlining.
