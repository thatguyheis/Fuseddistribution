# House Style

The default style for this pipeline should feel:

- Sharp, confident, and contemporary
- Story-driven rather than abstract
- Grounded in modern work: software, services, online business, investing, freelancing, and creator economics
- Respectful of the original lesson architecture without sounding antique

Patterns from the current sample manuscript:

- Open with a recognizable modern tension.
- Use specific economic details instead of generic motivation.
- Keep the moral lesson simple and repeatable.
- Prefer concrete financial behavior over vague inspiration.
- End chapters with a line worth underlining.

## Character Naming And Parody Rules

- Keep adapted character names visibly connected to the source whenever possible. Start from the original name's sound, rhythm, or syllable shape before inventing a new one.
- Use modern parody energy through role, behavior, setting, and fictional surnames rather than direct references to living public figures.
- Do not use real public-figure names, near-identical names, active brand names, or trademarked company names as character identities.
- If a character is inspired by a high-profile archetype, make them a composite and document the source-name mapping in the adaptation blueprint.
- Prefer names that feel like modernized descendants of the original cast, not random replacements.
