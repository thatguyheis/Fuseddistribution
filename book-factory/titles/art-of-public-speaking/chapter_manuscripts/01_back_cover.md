# Speak So People Ship
## Back Cover

The Art of Public Speaking remains useful because the underlying problem has not gone away. A practical public-speaking manual about confidence, preparation, delivery, persuasion, voice, audience attention, and clear argument.

*Speak So People Ship* rebuilds that classic for remote meetings, product demos, technical talks, sales calls, investor pitches, webinars, podcasts, async video, and team leadership. It is written for modern builders who want direct, modern, story-driven guidance without generic motivation or antique examples.

The lesson begins with one hard line: "Clear speech begins with clear thinking." From there, the book turns the original lesson sequence into modern scenes built for modern builders.

Inside, readers will find:

- A public-speaking classic rebuilt for technical professionals
- Communication skills for demos, pitches, meetings, webinars, and leadership
- A practical speaking book for builders who need trust, clarity, and action

If you want the original classic's lessons in language built for modern builders, this book was written for you.
