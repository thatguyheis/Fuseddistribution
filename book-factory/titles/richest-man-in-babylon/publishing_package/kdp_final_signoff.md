## Human Signoff Checklist

- Confirm `kdp_preflight_check.md` was completed before opening the live KDP session.

### 1. Package Identity

- Confirm the final title and subtitle match `package_identity_record.md`.

### 2. Publication Files

- Confirm the final manuscript file is the reviewed text intended for upload.
- Confirm the final print file is the reviewed print-ready text intended for upload.

- Confirm any marketing or A+ image files are the exact assets intended for upload.
- Confirm the exact asset paths are recorded in `cover_and_marketing_asset_record.md`.
- Confirm the exact entered KDP values and selected upload files are recorded in `kdp_submission_worksheet.md`.

- Confirm the KDP description matches the manuscript and does not overpromise financial outcomes.

- Do not submit until the exported ebook and print upload file paths are recorded.
- Do not submit until the cover file is final.
- Do not submit until the exact uploaded image asset paths are recorded.
