## What Each File Resolves

- `package_identity_record.md`: confirms the exact title, subtitle, contributor framing, and adaptation notice.
- `metadata.md`: confirms the upload-facing metadata note still frames the book as a fictionalized modern adaptation of a public-domain classic.
- `kdp_ready_blocker_matrix.md`: shows the remaining blockers, the exact local file that clears each blocker, and the order the uploader should resolve them.
- `kdp_listing_snapshot.md`: gives the uploader one locked copy block for title, subtitle, contributor framing, description, and keywords during the live KDP session.
- `kdp_description.md`: confirms the reader promise matches the manuscript and does not overstate financial outcomes.
- `keywords.md`: confirms the keyword set is acceptable for upload and avoids brand confusion.
- `manuscript.md`: confirms the reviewed ebook source text that the export should match.
- `print_master.md`: confirms the reviewed print source text that the export should match.
- `publication_export_record.md`: records the exact exported ebook and print files intended for upload.
- `cover_brief.md`: confirms the cover concept still matches the approved positioning.
- `cover_and_marketing_asset_record.md`: records the exact uploaded cover, interior-image, and marketing-image asset paths plus image AI answers.
- `kdp_ai_answer_matrix.md`: turns the final publication files into the exact local AI-disclosure answers the uploader should enter.
- `ai_disclosure_review.md`: records the final human AI-disclosure answers from the actual publication files.
- `kdp_submission_field_map.md`: maps each KDP listing, file-upload, and AI-disclosure field to the exact local source file the uploader should reference.
- `kdp_submission_worksheet.md`: records the exact values, upload files, and AI answers actually entered during the KDP session.
- `kdp_upload_checklist.md`: verifies the remaining package and disclosure blockers before submission.
- `kdp_final_signoff.md`: records the final human approval decision and review date.

## Blocking Rules

- Do not submit until the exact final cover path is recorded.
- Do not submit until the exact exported ebook and print upload file paths are recorded.
- Do not submit until any uploaded marketing or interior-image paths are recorded, or their absence is explicitly recorded.
- Do not submit until the AI answers have been chosen from `kdp_ai_answer_matrix.md` and copied into the review records.
- Do not submit until the AI-disclosure answers are copied from the actual publication assets.
- Do not submit until `kdp_listing_snapshot.md` still matches the current identity, description, and keyword files.
- Do not change the title, subtitle, or contributor framing during this review without a separate human review pass.
