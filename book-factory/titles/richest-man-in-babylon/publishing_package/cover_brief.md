# Cover Brief

- Working title: The Richest Dev in the Valley
- Positioning: narrative wealth parables for founders, operators, creators, developers, and freelancers
- Audience: modern builders

## Visual Direction

- Contemporary, premium, and disciplined rather than playful.
- Signal timeless wisdom translated into a modern Silicon Valley economic world.
- Favor strong typography, confident hierarchy, and one memorable symbolic element tied to the book's metaphor.

## Emotional Goal

Make the book feel like a sharp modern parable for ambitious readers who value money, competence, and earned leverage.

## Avoid

- Generic self-help iconography
- Cartoon finance imagery
- Anything that implies the original edition or original author created this cover
