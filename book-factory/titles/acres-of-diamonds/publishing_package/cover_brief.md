# Cover Brief

- Working title: Hidden Gold in Plain Sight
- Positioning: a narrative business parable about finding overlooked opportunity in ordinary markets
- Audience: modern builders

## Visual Direction

- Contemporary, premium, and grounded rather than flashy.
- Signal overlooked value, practical intelligence, and quiet economic truth.
- Favor strong typography and one visual metaphor that suggests hidden wealth beneath ordinary surfaces.
- Good motif options: cracked asphalt revealing gold, a utility map glowing under a city grid, or a plain storefront with illuminated seams beneath it.

## Emotional Goal

Make the book feel like a sharp modern parable for ambitious readers who want to see value others ignore.

## Avoid

- Generic motivational self-help imagery
- Treasure-chest cliché visuals
- Anything that implies the original edition or original author created this cover
