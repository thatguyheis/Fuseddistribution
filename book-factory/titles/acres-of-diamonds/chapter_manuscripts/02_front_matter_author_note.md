# Author Note

This manuscript is a modern adaptation of *Acres of Diamonds* by Russell H. Conwell. It preserves the original work's underlying lesson while translating its setting, examples, and voice for modern builders, operators, creators, and entrepreneurs.

This adaptation is not presented as text by the original author, and it should never be packaged in a way that implies endorsement, collaboration, or authorship by the source author. Every public-facing asset should clearly describe the book as a modern adaptation of a classic public-domain work.

Any named characters, businesses, and scenes in this edition are fictionalized devices created to carry the lesson into a modern setting. They are not depictions of real individuals or real companies.

Rights record: Nineteenth-century publication with a documented public-domain record.
