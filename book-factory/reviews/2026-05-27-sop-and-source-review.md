Example thread on disappointing customer experience: https://www.reddit.com/r/selfpublish/comments/1rsawrk/kdp_blocked_my_book/

## Platform Findings

- KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text, images, or translations at publication or republication. KDP says AI-assisted brainstorming, editing, refining, or checking does not require disclosure, but the publisher remains responsible for quality and rights.
- KDP allows public-domain content, but may reject undifferentiated versions when free versions exist. KDP explicitly recognizes differentiated public-domain editions such as translated, annotated, or illustrated versions.
- The SOP should not treat public-domain status as enough. It needs a second gate: differentiation and customer-experience value.
- The SOP should treat AI use as a publication metadata item, not an informal production detail.

## Source Comparisons

### The Richest Dev in the Valley

- Source focus: *The Richest Man in Babylon* is a parable-based personal finance book centered on Arkad, the seven cures, the five laws of gold, good luck through action, lending prudence, protection, debt repayment, and work.
- Adaptation fit: Strong. The manuscript preserves the financial principle sequence and modernizes the parable structure into Silicon Valley, freelance income, digital assets, debt, and wealth protection.
- Completed fix: Replaced real-person-adjacent `Musk` references in manuscript, chapter files, back-cover copy, registry, and blueprint with fictional `Morrow` references.
- Completed fix: Reworked the main cast toward source-aligned modern names: `Bansir`, `Kobbi`, `Arkad Morrow`, `Algamish Gains`, `Nomas Morrow`, `Mathon Thorne`, `Dabasir`, `Tarkad`, `Sharru Nader`, `Hadan Gray`, `Arad Gray`, `Kalabab`, `Azmur`, and `Agar`.
- Recommended fix: Add a print package like `Hidden Gold in Plain Sight` so this title has title page, copyright/adaptation notice, contents, and back matter.
- Decision: `revise`, pending print package and final compliance review.

### As You Think

- Source focus: *As a Man Thinketh* follows seven sections: thought and character, circumstances, health, purpose, achievement, visions and ideals, and serenity.
- Adaptation fit: Strong. The chapter sequence and moral architecture are preserved, and the modern-builder framing avoids reducing the book to simple positivity.
- Required fix: Add a title page, copyright/adaptation notice, contents, source note, and back matter. The current compiled manuscript starts directly at the foreword.
- Recommended fix: Add a short source-fidelity note explaining that the adaptation modernizes examples while preserving James Allen's chapter sequence.
- Decision: `revise`.

### Hidden Gold in Plain Sight

- Source focus: *Acres of Diamonds* argues that people often search far away for opportunity while missing value close at hand; it also connects success to work, service, self-betterment, and practical action.
- Adaptation fit: Good. The manuscript strongly preserves the nearby-opportunity lesson and translates it into ordinary modern markets, service businesses, trust gaps, and repeated demand.
- Completed fix: Added an author/source note acknowledging the source's service and self-betterment current, so the adaptation does not reduce Conwell's lecture to pure market opportunity.
- Recommended fix: Keep the print package as the model for the other two titles.
- Decision: `revise`.

## SOP Enhancements

- Add `ai_publication_compliance` before KDP package generation.
- Add `public_domain_differentiation` evidence to each registry record.
- Add `real_person_brand_risk` review for names, character descriptions, marketing prompts, and cover prompts.
- Add `print_package` generation as a standard post-package step.
