- text: 3-5 DAYS BACKGROUND CHECK
  explanation: The background check portion of LSA verification typically processes in 3 to 5 business days once submitted
  graphic_type: none
  explanation: Background checks take 3 to 5 business days. License and insurance review adds another week. Total approval runs 2 to 6 weeks, but document completeness is the main variable.
  narration: The biggest mistake businesses make is submitting incomplete documents. Background checks clear fast. The delay is almost always missing license or insurance paperwork. Do it in one session and you shorten the wait significantly.

- text: 2-6 WEEKS TO APPROVAL
  explanation: Full LSA verification including background check, license review, and insurance review takes 2 to 6 weeks depending on state and document readiness
  graphic_type: none
  explanation: The total verification timeline runs 2 to 6 weeks from application to live listing.
  narration: You cannot shortcut the verification timeline, but you can avoid extending it. Upload every document in the first session. Make sure your insurance certificate has current dates. A certificate that expires during review stalls everything.

- text: $15-40 STANDARD CPC

- text: 87% USE GOOGLE FIRST
