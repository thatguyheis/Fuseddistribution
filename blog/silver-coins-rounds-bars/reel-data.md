## stats
- text: 22% COIN PREMIUM SPOT
  narration: American Silver Eagles and Canadian Maple Leafs are the most recognizable silver you can buy. That recognition comes with a price — typically 18 to 25 percent above spot. You are paying for the government mint, the guaranteed purity, and the fact that any dealer in the country will buy them back on the spot.
- text: 5% ROUNDS PREMIUM SPOT
  narration: Generic silver rounds are privately minted, one troy ounce, 99.9 percent fine silver. The only thing missing is the government stamp — and the premium reflects that. At 4 to 8 percent above spot, rounds give you more silver for the same money with nearly the same storage and stacking properties as coins.

narration: Bars take premiums even lower — 2 to 3 percent on 10-ounce bars in normal market conditions. The tradeoff is divisibility. A 10-ounce bar is one piece you cannot split. So the lowest-premium option is not always the most flexible one, especially if you might need to sell part of your position later.

## caption
Coins, rounds, and bars all hold silver. But they are not the same buy. Government coins cost about 22 percent above spot. Generic rounds run about 5 percent. Bars can be as low as 2 percent. The right answer depends on what you actually need — and for most beginners, the answer is not what they expect.
