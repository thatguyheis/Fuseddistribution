- text: "~60:1 Ratio — May 2026"
  narration: The gold-to-silver ratio in May 2026 sits at roughly 60 to 1, meaning one ounce of gold buys about 60 ounces of silver. That's right at the 50-year historical average — neither a screaming buy signal nor a warning.
- text: "17:1 — Historic Low in 1980"
  narration: The modern era low of 17 to 1 was reached in 1980 during the Hunt Brothers silver squeeze. At that level, silver had dramatically outpaced gold and the relative value gap had essentially closed.
- text: "125:1 — All-Time High in March 2020"
  narration: The all-time modern peak came in March 2020 when COVID caused silver to collapse alongside industrial metals while gold held up. Investors who saw that 125 to 1 extreme and bought silver over the following months rode a major recovery back toward the long-term average.
