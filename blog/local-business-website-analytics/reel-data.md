- text: 15 MIN MONTHLY REVIEW
  explanation: Four questions answered monthly is enough to make real decisions from your data
  graphic_type: none
  narration: You do not need to check analytics every day. A monthly review covering four questions takes about 15 minutes. Did organic sessions go up or down? How many people visited the contact page? How many conversions happened? What device were they on? Those four numbers tell you where to focus.

  explanation: Tracking phone number taps shows exactly how many real leads your site produced
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Page views do not pay bills. Phone calls do. In GA4, you can mark phone number click events as conversions. After that, instead of seeing how many visitors you got, you see how many tapped your number. That one change transforms GA4 from a traffic counter into a lead tracker.

- text: SEARCH CONSOLE FREE
  explanation: Connecting Search Console to GA4 reveals which search queries drive your best visitors
  graphic_type: none

Connecting both tools in under five minutes adds a layer of data that changes how you read every report. You will find queries you never knew you ranked for.
