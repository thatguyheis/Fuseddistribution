Narration: From discovery to production, a new silver mine typically takes 8 to 12 years to develop. Even with strong silver prices, the supply response is slow. You cannot significantly increase production in a year or two.

**Stat 4** (154–177s)
Text: COMEX STOCKS DOWN 30%
Narration: COMEX silver warehouse stocks dropped from approximately 400 million ounces in early 2021 to under 280 million by early 2024. That is a decline of more than 30 percent over three years, tracking closely with the deficit period. Each consecutive year of deficit reduces the buffer available for the next disruption.

**Overlay 4** (177–197s)
Text: PHYSICAL PREMIUMS WIDEN IN DEFICITS
Narration: When exchange inventories tighten and industrial users compete with investors for physical metal, the premium you pay above spot for coins, rounds, or bars tends to increase. Buyers who own physical metal acquired at lower premiums are insulated from that effect.

**Overlay 5** (197–218s)
Text: INDUSTRIAL DEMAND IS PRICE-INELASTIC
Narration: Industrial users buy silver because their processes require it, not because of price. Solar manufacturers and EV makers do not cut back when silver gets expensive. That base of inelastic demand gives the physical market a floor that pure financial demand cannot provide.
