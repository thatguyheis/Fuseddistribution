- text: 170M TIKTOK USA USERS
  explanation: TikTok's monthly active US user base, making it a major local discovery platform
  graphic_type: none
  narration: TikTok has 170 million monthly active users in the USA as of 2024, according to TikTok's own Congressional disclosure. And unlike Instagram, TikTok's algorithm sends new creators' content to non-followers by default. That means a local business with zero followers can reach thousands of people in their service area on the very first video.

  graphic_type: growth

    from_value: 1

  narration: Short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than long-form video for service businesses, according to Sprout Social's 2024 Content Strategy Report. But engagement alone doesn't pay bills. The 5 video types that convert viewers into calls and bookings are what matter, and they're simpler than most local business owners think.

- text: BEFORE/AFTER VIDEOS
  explanation: Before and after transformations earn the highest save rate of any video format
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Before and after content earns the highest save rate of any format for service businesses. Viewers save it to show a partner, reference later, or share with someone who needs the same work done. A flooring installer, hairdresser, or landscaper who posts consistent before and afters builds a portfolio that keeps working long after the job is done.

- text: FAQ VIDEOS DRIVE SHARES
  explanation: Answering common customer questions in short clips generates consistent shares
  graphic_type: none
  narration: The second best-performing type is the FAQ video. Film a 30 to 60 second answer to the question customers ask you every single week. Do you offer free estimates? What's included in a basic cleaning? How long does a tile job take? These videos get shared because they're genuinely useful, and people share useful things.

- text: YOUR PHONE IS ENOUGH
  explanation: No production crew needed, just clear lighting and a recognizable background
  graphic_type: none
  narration: You don't need a production budget. Natural window light outperforms a ring light in a dim room because it's directional and creates natural contrast. The single biggest factor in whether a local business video holds attention is visual clarity in the first two seconds. Your phone, a $10 tripod, and a window is a complete studio.

- text: 5 VIDEOS THIS WEEK
  explanation: A simple 5-video starter plan any local business can execute without scripting
  graphic_type: none
  narration: Most local businesses never start because they try to plan 30 days of content at once. Five videos this week beats a 30-day calendar that sits unfinished. Video one is a 30-second introduction. Video two is a before and after. Video three answers your most-asked question. Video four is a day-in-the-life montage. Video five is a genuine customer reaction.
