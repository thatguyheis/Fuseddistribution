## caption
Silver's price swings make buyers hesitate — should you buy now or wait for a dip? The answer is: stop asking that question. Dollar cost averaging means you put a fixed amount into silver every month no matter what the price is doing. When it's cheap, you get more ounces. When it's expensive, you get fewer. Over time, your average cost smooths out and you never have to guess again.
