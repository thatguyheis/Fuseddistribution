# Buffer Performance Insight — 2026-07-27

Source: Buffer API snapshot captured in
`ops/profit-system/buffer-metrics/2026-07-27.json`.

## Key insight

The strongest current YouTube signal is not “silver” in general; it is the
decision/monetary-story topic family. Among mature silver posts, selling silver
to a dealer versus privately had 85 views, silver's 5,000-year currency history
had 54, and silver standard versus gold standard had 25. The two broad
industrial/outlook posts in the same mature cohort had 0 views each.

This is an experiment signal, not proof. The initial groups contain only three
and two posts, and the posts were not randomized. For the next three comparable
silver publishing days, test one decision-oriented, selling, or monetary-history
topic/hook and compare D+3 and D+7 views per hour against industrial/outlook
silver content. Require at least 15% improvement across three matched samples
before promoting the behavior to an active SOP rule.

## Measurement limits

- The current snapshot contains 28 sent posts and metrics for all 28.
- Buffer supplied YouTube views and X impressions, not a common view metric for
  both platforms.
- Buffer supplied no `totalTimeWatched` for these X or YouTube posts, so average
  view duration is unavailable and remains null.
- Metrics were last refreshed on 2026-07-26 and can lag by about 24 hours.
- These numbers are leading indicators only. No revenue, lead, conversion, or
  profit attribution is inferred from them.
